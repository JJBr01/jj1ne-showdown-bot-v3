# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn-1 Milotic → Kingambit switch**: correctly read the Wood Hammer OHKO (139-183%) and brought in a Grass resist. Clean defensive safe play.
- **Garchomp Stomping Tantrum on Sneasler (T4)**: removed the fast Unburden threat before it could snowball with Grassy Seed. Textbook win-con denial.
- **Milotic Ice Beam finishing Rillaboom (T7)** while Heat Wave went elsewhere — correct split that cashed the KO before Grassy Glide fired again.
- **Competitive proc**: Salamence's Intimidate on switch-in gave Milotic +2 SpA automatically — our team's built-in anti-Intimidate tech still works.
- **T2 Dragonite Protect** correctly dodged a 69%-KO-chance Draco Meteor.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied: low-HP switch rule (Garchomp pivoted at 36%), Protect-vs-OHKO discipline on Dragonite, no Tailwind into Trick Room issues, no Sucker Punch whiffs.

**Violated — the big one**: *"Setup is a win condition: if a foe boosts... punish it now."* Goodra-Hisui used **Shelter twice (+4 Def)** on turns 1 and 2 while we Protected and clicked Kowtow Cleave into a Protecting Rillaboom. We then **explicitly reasoned "Goodra at +4 Def is unkillable — target Rillaboom instead."** That is the exact failure mode the playbook forbids. Hisuian Goodra's **Body Press uses its Defense stat as the attacking stat** — every Shelter was a +2 Attack boost, not a defensive move. +4 Def = roughly triple Body Press damage. It then KO'd Kingambit outright (100%), hit Garchomp for 58%, and Milotic for 50%.

Also violated: **Whimsicott was benched.** Our one tool that reverses that line (Prankster Encore locking Goodra into Shelter, or Taunt blocking it entirely) was sitting at home. The team file literally says Whimsicott is never worth cutting.
