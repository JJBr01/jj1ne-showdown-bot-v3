# Analysis

**Record this batch: 5-5 (70 games, 36-34).** Sun (7-8) and standard-with-Dragonite (5-0) remain our best plans; rain (5-12) and sand (1-6) are still the sinkholes.

## Rain is a Mega problem, not a targeting problem
Two rain games, two very different results. vs jzbnaruto (WIN) we Mega'd Dragonite turn 1, Draco Meteor'd Espathra off the board immediately, then EQ'd Politoed, then finished Archaludon with Whimsicott. vs gloomdweller (LOSS, 14 turns) we **never Mega evolved**, left Drogon home, and brought Whimsicott/Kingambit/Milotic/Garchomp — a team with no single-turn Archaludon burst. Whimsicott did 198% damage with zero KOs while Archaludon sat behind Stamina, Psych Up Politoed and rain healing. The adjustment "never chip Archaludon" was followed correctly, but the bring itself made a KO impossible. The fix is at preview: rain demands Dragonite's Draco Meteor.

Secondary error: Kowtow Cleave into Archaludon gave it +1 Def via Stamina and cost Kingambit its life for 21% damage.

thatsyntaxerror: Psychic Terrain + Helping Hand Dire Claw did 85% + poison to Drogon on turn 1 despite Kingambit Protecting correctly. Drogon was dead by turn 2 having contributed 78%. The existing Indeedee rule needs extending: any Sneasler lead means Drogon stays home or switches.

## What's working
Mega Dragonite + Whimsicott Tailwind is 5-0 in standard and won the Gardevoir Trick Room game (focus-firing the TR setter with +2 Defiant Iron Head before it could Mega). Charizard in sun is 7-1. The Protect-discipline and focus-fire instructions are being followed; the losses are now bring errors and one bad trade, not turn-by-turn panic.
