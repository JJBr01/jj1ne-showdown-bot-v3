## 3. What Failed
**Turn 1 was a 2-for-0 blowout against us.**
- Whimsicott's Focus Sash is functionally nonexistent in sand: Excadrill Iron Head → 1 HP, sand upkeep → faint. The Tailwind it bought expired with almost no value because both our attackers died or were at the wrong end of the board.
- Kingambit traded itself for 87% on a Tyranitar that then Protected and healed nothing but survived long enough to switch out and come back. Three turns later Garchomp mopped it up anyway — so the Kingambit trade bought nothing we couldn't get for free.

**Turn 3 (engine fallback) cost us our best Pokémon.** Drogon at 153/153 HP, Excadrill at 1 HP behind a Tyranitar Protect. Any Protect on Drogon survives, Garchomp's EQ still chips, and we enter T4 at 2v2 with a healthy Mega Charizard. Instead we won the game at 1v0 instead of 3v0 — a win here, but the same line is a loss against any team with a fourth live threat.

**Net:** we won because the opponent's remaining pieces (Sneasler, Tyranitar at 13%) all died to a single Earthquake. This was a *lucky* win on a board we had already thrown twice.
