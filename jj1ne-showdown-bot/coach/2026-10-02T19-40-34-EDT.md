Milotic's Competitive punishes Incineroar's Intimidate; Mega Dragonite's Tailwind answers Volcarona's Quiver Dance speed; Garchomp and Kingambit sit in the back as closers. This is our highest-win-rate plan (`standard / Dragonite|zw`: 20-12).
- **Mega-stone awareness:** We correctly applied the durable lesson *"treat an opposing Pokémon holding an unused Mega Stone as already in its Mega forme."* The calc was run against **Garchomp-Mega-Z**, not base Garchomp, and it flagged Dragon Claw at 67–121% (39% OHKO) into Dragonite. That is exactly the piece that had to die first.
- **Focus fire over splitting:** Volcarona was non-threatening on turn 1 (it clicked Struggle Bug). Both actions went into the only Pokémon that could remove a member of our team. Ice Beam (56–102%) + Dragon Pulse (54–98%) was a near-guaranteed kill, and it landed: Dragon Pulse for 83%, Ice Beam to finish.
- **Competitive fired:** Struggle Bug's −1 SpA on Milotic became **+2 SpA** via Competitive. Free value from the lead choice.
- **Mega timing:** Dragonite mega-evolved turn 1 rather than holding it, in line with *"never hand them a free Mega / free positioning turn."*

The opponent forfeited at the end of turn 1. That is the game-state read working.

## 3. What Failed

Honestly: nothing lost us anything. But there is one real refinement worth codifying, because it is a recurring pattern across our 203 games.

**Overkill instead of banking speed control.** Milotic's Ice Beam alone was **56–102%** into Mega Garchomp-Z. Dragon Pulse was a *second* 54–98% hit into the same target.
