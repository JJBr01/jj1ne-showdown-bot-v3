# Coach Analysis (14 games, 7–7)

## What's actually winning/losing games

**First KO is the single strongest predictor.** When we take the first KO: 6–1. When they do: 1–4. Every loss in the last five featured either an early trade-down (Sneasler evaporating to Indeedee's Choice Scarf Expanding Force T1) or a Pokémon left at 8–30% being dragged around as a liability.

**We are losing to boosters, not to raw power.** vs marillat: Torterra Shell Smash'd behind White Herb (visible on Open Team Sheets, we had turn-1 info and still led into it) and from +2 it KO'd Sneasler, Milotic, and Garchomp in three turns. We killed Indeedee and Salamence — fine, but neither was the win condition. vs VictoriousDancing: Corviknight Bulk Up + Psychic Seed was the engine; we chipped it twice and never removed it.

**Fake Out lead is 0–1; Tailwind lead is 6–4.** Both Fake Out-lead losses had a Psychic Terrain mon (Indeedee) revealed on the sheet. Fake Out into Psychic Terrain is a dead turn and we did it anyway.

**Charizard plan on `standard`/`trickroom` is 0–3.** Drogon has 4 faints vs 3 KOs — the worst ratio on the team, usually to Rock Slide (Tyranitar, Excadrill, Sneasler). Dragonite-plan is 2–0.

## Concrete changes

1. **Preview rule:** if opposing sheet shows Tyranitar, Excadrill, or any Rock Slide user AND we can't guarantee the OHKO on it turn 1, Mega Dragonite — not Charizard. Charizard only into rain/sun/snow/water-heavy cores (currently 4–0 there).
2. **Preview rule:** never lead Sneasler when Indeedee/Hatterene/any Psychic Surge is on the sheet. Fake Out is dead and Sneasler is Scarf-EF food. Lead Whimsicott + Dragonite instead.
3. **Pilot rule — Shell Smash/Bulk Up priority:** once a foe is at +2 or higher, double-target it *that same turn*, even over a guaranteed KO elsewhere. In marillat we spent T4 correctly but T3 we split (Tailwind + chip) and lost the game there.
4. **Pilot rule:** Whimsicott Encore the booster the turn after it boosts if we cannot KO — Prankster Encore locks Shell Smash/Bulk Up and buys two turns. We have never used it in these reports.
5. **Pilot rule:** stop Protecting sub-15% Pokémon (Whimsicott T7/T9, Dragonite T4/T7). Those turns produced zero damage in three games. Attack or switch.
6. **Calc:** 13/26 in range is poor. Assume opponents are bulk-invested; require a predicted ≥110% before calling something a "guaranteed KO".
