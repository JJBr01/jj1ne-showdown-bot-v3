# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 target selection (even on fallback):** Weather Ball removed Meowstic immediately. Meowstic was their Trick Room/Thunder Wave support — killing it on turn 1 denied Trick Room entirely for the rest of the game. Their archetype was `trickroom` and TR never went up once.
- **Turn 2 pivot instead of Protect:** Whimsicott at 1/137 was a dead action. Switching it to Kingambit (full HP, resists Extreme Speed) converted a wasted slot into a real attacker. This is textbook "switching is a real tool."
- **Focus-firing Toxapex (T3–T5):** Toxapex with Regenerator/Recover heals through chip. Kowtow Cleave + Weather Ball stacked each turn and killed it on T5. Exactly the "burst the wall in one turn, do not chip across turns" directive.
- **Milotic as the closer:** 104% damage, 2 KOs (Sinistcha and Mega Dragonite) — it carried the endgame. Milotic from the back has now decided two of the last three games.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
**Applied:**
- Did not click Tailwind into a Trick Room archetype.
- Did not chip the bulky healer across multiple turns — double-targeted it.
- Recognised a 1% Pokémon as a dead asset and switched rather than Protect-stalled.
- Kingambit was correct to bring: their sheet (Dragonite/Toxapex/Corviknight/Incineroar/Sinistcha/Meowstic) had no Ground or Fighting move shown, satisfying the two active Kingambit bans.

**Violated:**
- **BURNED/LOW MEGA RULE** (active proposal, 13 games left): Drogon at **13/153**, paralyzed, under Infestation residual, used **Protect on T6**. It then took residual to 8/153 and died on T7 to Extreme Speed having dealt zero damage in its final two turns. Protect guaranteed its death.
- **Mega-turn-1 discipline:** the T1 wolfe-fallback attacked into a confirmed Thunder Wave user. Paralysis then erased Drogon's T2 and T3 completely (`cant|par` twice). That is two full actions in the 2-action economy handed over for free — worse than any spread move was worth.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Rate-limit fallback on the single most important turn.** T1 was `wolfe-fallback`. The engine attacked; Meowstic paralyzed our Mega. Against a sheet with Thunder Wave, Drogon should Protect on its Mega turn (Drought still sets).
2. **Drogon became a 4-turn liability.** Paralyzed, trapped by Infestation, sub-25%. We kept it in and then Protected with it. It should have been attacking every turn from T5 onward, or we should have accepted it was lost and played around it.
3. **Kingambit killed itself.** Burned by Matcha Gotcha, at 18/207, it fired Kowtow Cleave (47% on Sinistcha, not a KO) and fainted to Life Orb recoil. 131% total damage, **0 KOs**. Its damage all went into a Regenerator wall.
