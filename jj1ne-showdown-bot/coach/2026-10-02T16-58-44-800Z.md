## Analysis

**1. Rock attackers still delete us (sand 2-10).** Vs test0824 the pilot Protected Drogon on T2 *and* T3 while Tyranitar used Dragon Dance and then Mega Evolved. That is exactly the "don't Protect-stall a booster" rule being broken, with the extra cost that +1 Mega-TT Rock Slide is a spread OHKO on Drogon, Milotic (at 39%) and Whimsicott. The correct line at T2 was switch Drogon out (it has no answer to TT) and start burning Tyranitar with Milotic Scald + the Competitive boosts we were already getting free from Icy Wind.
