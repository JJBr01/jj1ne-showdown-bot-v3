# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Trick Room denial was textbook.** Kingambit Kowtow Cleave + Garchomp Stomping Tantrum deleted Farigiraf before it ever moved. Trick Room never went up in a Trick Room matchup — that is the single most valuable thing we can do in that archetype.
- **Defiant conversion.** Staraptor's Intimidate on turn 4 gave Kingambit +2 Atk, which directly enabled the Iron Head OHKO on Sylveon. Kingambit traded 1-for-2 (Farigiraf + Sylveon) at 207 HP — excellent value.
- **Sun denial.** Dragon Pulse + Stomping Tantrum eventually removed Torkoal, cutting off Eruption and Drought.
- **Tailwind timing discipline.** We correctly withheld Tailwind while a Trick Room setter was alive and only clicked it on turn 7 once the board was clean of TR threats.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Focus-fire the Trick Room setter before it moves" — done, perfectly.
- ✅ "Never Tailwind into Trick Room" — respected.
- ✅ "Switch a Pokémon out when it's about to be KO'd" — applied in prior games but **violated here** (Kingambit at 25/207 stayed in on turn 4 and died; although that trade was acceptable since it got the Sylveon KO).
- ❌ **"Switching is a real tool… switch when Choice-locked into a bad move."** Massively violated. Garchomp fired Stomping Tantrum into a Flying-type Staraptor on turns 4, 7, 8 and onward. Four-plus actions of pure zero. In a 20-turn game that is the entire loss margin.
- ❌ **"When a foe boosts, that slot is the only kill target."** Staraptor boosted Def and SpD off its own Close Combat on turn 6 (Contrary). We did not pivot the whole game plan onto it.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Choice-lock paralysis (the game-losing error).** Garchomp is our fastest piece and it contributed 153% damage — all of it into Torkoal/Farigiraf — while being functionally a 0-move Pokémon for the entire Staraptor endgame. The correct line on turn 7 was *switch Garchomp to Milotic* (which was on the bench and never came in), resetting the lock and bringing in an Ice Beam answer to a Flying type.
2. **Double-dipping into a Protect pattern.** Turn 5: both actions into a 44% Torkoal that had Protected on turns 1 and 3 — both blocked by a double Protect, and Mega Staraptor evolved for free. Turn 6: both actions again into a 3%-after-chip Torkoal while the now-Mega Staraptor clicked Close Combat and boosted.
3. **Contrary Staraptor unanswered.** It gained +1 Def/+1 SpD on turn 6 and kept Close Combatting. Dragon Pulse was chipping ~15–26% into a +1 SpD target — that is a losing damage race by definition. We needed either Rock Slide (Choice-locked away), Whimsicott Moonblast focus-fire, or Milotic Ice Beam.
