# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**The lead was the whole game.** Kingambit + Charizard-Y into Indeedee/Hatterene Psychic Terrain is the textbook Wolfe "Must-Come" pick:

- **T1:** Kowtow Cleave OHKO'd Indeedee (112-134%), deleting Psychic Terrain on turn one. Follow Me could not stop it because Kowtow Cleave was already aimed at Indeedee.
- **Kingambit's Psychic immunity** blanked Hatterene's Expanding Force on T2 *and* T3 — two full opponent actions deleted from the 2-action economy for free. That is the single biggest reason this was a 3-0.
- **T2 Heat Wave in sun** OHKO'd Sinistcha *through* Rage Powder (spread moves ignore redirection) — the one move on our side that was immune to their entire support plan.
- **T3:** Kowtow Cleave removed Hatterene under their own Trick Room (Kingambit is faster in TR). Drogon Protected at 24% — correct, it was facing a displayed OHKO.
- **T4:** Drogon switched out instead of Protecting twice. This is the exact coach rule "a slot that Protected last turn and still faces a displayed OHKO must SWITCH" — followed perfectly.
- **Defiant payoff:** Mega Salamence's Intimidate gave Kingambit **+2 Attack** (log line `-boost|p2a: Kingambit|atk|2`), turning Kowtow Cleave into an 84% hit on a Mega. Never fear Intimidate with this piece.
