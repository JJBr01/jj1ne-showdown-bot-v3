**The win was earned on three clean decisions:**

2. **Kingambit as the Gholdengo answer.** Kowtow Cleave (Dark, no contact-irrelevant, 100% accurate) one-shot Gholdengo through Good as Gold, while Make It Rain only threatened Kingambit for 21-33%. That is a hard counter-matchup and we played it correctly — focus fire on the single most dangerous slot instead of splitting.

3. **The closing turn (T5) was textbook Wolfe.** Two foes at 8% and 2%, one of which (Volcarona) clicked Rage Powder. This is exactly the "safe play when ahead" directive.

4. **Tailwind timing.** Farigiraf was on their sheet, but we set Tailwind on a board with no Trick Room threat active and no setter on the field. Three Tailwind turns, zero wasted.
