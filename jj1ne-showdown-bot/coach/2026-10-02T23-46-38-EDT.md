# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Spread over single-target into redirection**: Heat Wave on T2 ignored Rage Powder worries and OHKO'd Rillaboom (111-131%) while chipping Volcarona. The redirection guard is working.
- **Milotic finishing the chipped Dragapult** on T3 with Ice Beam was a clean 2-action-economy conversion of Heat Wave chip into a KO.
- **Preview logic was directionally right**: Charizard-Y in the back as the Volcarona answer is correct — we just never got it there healthy.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- *Applied*: "Follow Me / Rage Powder invalidates single-target calcs → click spread moves." Heat Wave was the right click twice.
- *Applied (partially)*: "A foe that boosts becomes the mandatory focus-fire target" — Kowtow Cleave went at Volcarona T1, correct target, wrong Pokémon alive to throw it.
- **VIOLATED — the decisive one**: *"Volcarona at +1 SpA OHKOs Kingambit with Fiery Dance despite Dark/Steel typing — never leave Kingambit in front of a boosted Fire special attacker without Protect or a switch."* We did worse: we **led** Kingambit into Volcarona at **zero boosts** and it still died from 207/207 to Overheat. The rule needs to be unconditional on boosts.
- **VIOLATED**: SPREAD-FIRE KO BAND proposal — T2 we sent Rillaboom (Grass, 2x Fire) into the exact Overheat that had just one-shot a 207 HP Steel type.
- **VIOLATED**: "After a Mega is in the KO band of a live attacker, Protect or switch." Drogon at 96/153 with both Volcarona Overheat and Dragapult Draco Meteor (52-92%) live chose to attack and died.

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 1 — the lost game.** Lead Kingambit vs a sheet containing Volcarona. Kingambit is **4x weak to Fire**. Overheat from a neutral-nature Volcarona does 100% to 207 HP. Our own `|calc|` threat line listed only *"Kingambit threatened by Rillaboom Wood Hammer (26-37%)"* — the OHKO was **never displayed**, meaning the threat model only consulted *revealed* moves and ignored the 4x multiplier on a sheet-known Fire special attacker. We planned a turn around a Pokémon that was dead before it moved, and Drogon flinched to Fake Out on top. Net turn 1: **-1 Pokémon, 27% chip taken, 0 damage dealt.**

**Turn 2 — feeding the second body.** Knowing Overheat one-shot a 207 HP Steel type, we switched in Rillaboom (207 HP, Grass, 2x Fire) and clicked Wood Hammer for **21-24%**. It died. That is a second whole Pokémon traded for a quarter of a Volcarona. The correct line was Milotic in (Fire resist, Scald/Ice Beam answer) or Drogon alone + switch.

**Turn 3 — the Mega.** Drogon at 96/153 (63%) faced Dragapult Draco Meteor at 52-92% **plus** a live Volcarona Overheat.
