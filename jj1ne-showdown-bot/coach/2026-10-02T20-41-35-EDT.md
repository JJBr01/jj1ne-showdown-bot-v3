# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Protect on Drogon** while Mega Evolving was textbook: Kleavor clicked Stone Axe into Protect. The hard Rock-type rule is paying for itself.
- **Whimsicott Moonblast chip → finish on Kleavor** removed the 4x threat to our Mega by turn 3.
- **Sun Heat Wave deleted Mega Metagross** in one hit, producing a 4v2 board on turn 3 with both their Megas/breakers gone.
- The lead pairing (Whimsicott + Drogon) correctly pressured Kleavor and kept Dragonite sheltered in the back.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Any live Rock-type makes Protect mandatory on the Mega turn" — applied, and it worked.
- ✅ Mega Evolving Drogon turn 1 to set sun — applied.
- ❌ "If a foe Protected against our attack last turn, do not aim the same attack at that slot again" — **violated on turn 3** (Moonblast at Kleavor again). It happened to work.
- ❌ "Never create a turn where both of our actions deal zero damage" — **violated on turn 2**: both attacks went into a double Protect.
- ❌ "A Pokémon at or below 15% HP should switch out, not attack" — **violated**: Whimsicott at 1/137 attacked on turns 2 and 3.

## 3. What Failed
**The game was lost on turn 4 and repeated on turn 6 — both times to Kingambit's Sucker Punch.**

- **T4**: We were ahead 4v2 after the double KO. Kingambit + Rillaboom came in. Drogon was at 45/153 (29%). The threat line literally printed "Drogon threatened by Kingambit Kowtow Cleave (41-67% KO)". We clicked Heat Wave. Kingambit Sucker Punch (+1) killed Drogon before it moved. **Protect was the free win**: Sucker Punch *fails* into Protect, and Rillaboom's Fake Out was already spent on Dragonite. We would have kept our Mega and still been 4v2.
- **T6**: Identical error. Dragonite at 48/168 (29%) clicked Heat Wave into a live Kingambit at 33% with Sucker Punch available. Dead again before acting. Protect blanks Sucker Punch entirely; we'd have had Dragonite + Milotic vs two foes at ~30%.
- **T5/T6 Milotic**: Rillaboom's Grassy Glide is +1 in its own terrain — Milotic could never outrun it. Once Milotic was at full but in Wood Hammer/Glide range, attacking was a losing trade; it should have cycled Protect or been the Protect slot on the turn Dragonite attacked.
- **T2**: A completely wasted 2-action turn into a double Protect, with a 1%-HP Whimsicott that should have pivoted out to a healthy body.

The macro picture: **we had zero answer to a priority-stacked back line.** Our entire speed plan (Tailwind, fast Megas) is worthless against Sucker Punch + Grassy Glide. Against that shape, Protect discipline *is* the speed control.
