# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**The preview plan was textbook.** Against a sand archetype we used the CONFIRMED simulation plan — Mega Charizard-Y with a Whimsicott Tailwind lead, Garchomp and Milotic in the back. That is the 70.7% plan, not the rejected 53.3% Dragonite+Milotic plan.

**Turn 1 target selection was excellent macro play.** Chandelure was the obvious "scary" mon (Overheat showed 150–289% on Whimsicott), but it has Flash Fire — our Mega's entire moveset would have been dead into it. Instead we focus-fired **Oranguru**, the Trick Room / Future Sight support enabler, and removed it before it ever acted. This is exactly "KO their speed-control setter before it establishes field advantage." Whimsicott Moonblast (45%) + Weather Ball (55%) = clean KO.

**Turn 2 Weather Ball OHKO'd Chandelure** (100% damage) — the Fire-immune mon was removed by the Fire mon only because the sun was up and Weather Ball doesn't care, and because Chandelure was already the second target.

**The 1 HP Whimsicott switch was correct.** Sandstorm would have killed it at end of turn for free. We converted a dead body into Milotic — sand-immune, bulky, and the natural Hippowdon counter. That's a defensive safe play / repositioning switch.

**Stomping Tantrum discipline held.** Garchomp never clicked Earthquake beside Milotic. Stomping Tantrum OHKO'd Toxtricity (93%, 140–268% predicted).

**Protect-read discipline held.** Toxtricity Protected T3; we attacked it again T4 and the consecutive Protect failed. Textbook "remember what they keep doing."

**Safe play while ahead.** T3 and T4 were pure guaranteed-damage lines with zero predictions while up on Pokémon count. Scald burn on Hippowdon converted into the final KO.
