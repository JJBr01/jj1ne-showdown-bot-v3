# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Lead**: Whimsicott (Focus Sash) + Drogon, Mega on turn 1 — the stat-proven sun plan (now 16-3).
- **Turn 1**: Tailwind with Sash intact was nearly free. Whimsicott ate a Heat Wave to 1 HP and still owned the next turn.
- **Turn 2 (game-winning turn)**: Perfect application of two principles at once —
  - *"Damaged Pokémon do not deal less damage"*: Whimsicott at 1 HP fired Moonblast and KO'd the 57% Garchomp instead of Protecting.
  - *"Deny the win condition"*: Ancient Power (4x into Charizard-Mega-Y) deleted their Mega for 69%+. Heat Wave/Weather Ball would not have done that; Rock coverage was the one answer and we clicked it.
- Double KO on turn 2 → opponent forfeited on turn 3. Zero wasted actions across the whole battle.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Whimsicott with Focus Sash must click Tailwind on turn 1 unless facing a listed ≥90% incoming hit."
- ✅ No Tailwind into Trick Room, no Prankster into Dark, no Sucker Punch gambles.
- ✅ Win-condition targeting: their Mega Zard-Y was the single most valuable piece and we removed it the turn it became killable.
- ✅ No overkill / correct move selection — we used the move that actually KOs rather than the biggest-looking spread.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Preview fell back to the engine again (HTTP 429)** — 4 of the last 5 games. Lead/team selection is repeatedly made without the macro layer. The sun plan happened to be hard-coded well, but in a sand/rain matchup this fallback is how we lose.
2. **Opposing Mega not modelled**: T1 Heat Wave → Charizard predicted 14–24%, actual **31%**. They mega-evolved the same turn, so our calc used base-forme stats/Drought-less sun math. In a close game the mirror error (under-predicting *their* damage onto us) is lethal.
3. **Minor risk acceptance**: Whimsicott sat in range of a double spread turn 1 (Zard Heat Wave + Venusaur Sludge Bomb). Sash + a miss saved us. Acceptable, but logged.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Pre-compute foe threat ranges using the **Mega forme** whenever a foe holds an unused mega stone at full HP on its first active turn.
- Lock in the heuristic: **opposing Charizard-Y on the field ⇒ Drogon clicks Ancient Power**, period.
- Continue the sun/standard lead: Mega Charizard + Whimsicott Tailwind.
- Fix the rate-limit fallback so the preview macro call always lands (retry/backoff), since preview errors are unrecoverable later.

## 5. Team Evolution & Opponent Inspiration (Mega Dragonite + Mega Charizard Core)
Altabes ran a classic sun core (Charizard-Y / Venusaur / Greninja / Garchomp) and lost specifically because they had **no coverage for a 4x weakness on their own Mega**. Lesson both ways:
- **Keep Ancient Power on Drogon permanently.** Every proposal to swap it for Solar Beam should be rejected — it is our only answer to opposing Zard-Y, Volcarona, Staraptor-Mega, Talonflame and other 4x-Rock-weak threats, and it has now directly won a game.
- **Milotic variant**: continue pushing the Icy Wind-over-Protect test. Weather mirrors are decided by who moves first after Tailwind expires, and we own no speed-drop tool.
- **Mega Dragonite spread**: keep leaning HP/SpD so Multiscale + bulk survives one Ice Beam/Moonblast, since all our recent losses came from it dying to a single special hit.
- Item loadout: stay on `scarf_garchomp` (11-11, the only non-losing variant).
