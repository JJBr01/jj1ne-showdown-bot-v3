# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Correct macro target identification.** Rain is statistically our worst archetype (12-35). The single most valuable denial on that board was Pelipper's Drizzle: it enables Hurricane (94-184% on Whimsicott), boosts Swampert's Water STAB, and cancels Drogon's Drought. We focus-fired it on turn 1 and removed it before it ever moved. Weather flipped to sun, Wave Crash was halved, and the opponent's game plan was gone one turn in.
- **Mega-evolving Charizard-Y immediately** to overwrite rain was the right call *in principle* — Drought resetting the weather is a 50% damage reduction on everything Water.
- **Turn 2 pivot discipline.** Drogon at 8% HP facing a displayed Wave Crash 126-204% was correctly recognised as a dead resource and switched to Milotic rather than clicking an attack. This directly follows the "MEGA SURVIVAL SWITCH" proposal and the "Charizard-Y in enemy rain must Protect or pivot" lesson.
- **Preview selection** (Whimsicott / Drogon / Milotic / Kingambit) was sound: Milotic as the rain sponge, Kingambit for Armarouge's Expanding Force, Whimsicott for Encore on Pelipper/Armarouge.
