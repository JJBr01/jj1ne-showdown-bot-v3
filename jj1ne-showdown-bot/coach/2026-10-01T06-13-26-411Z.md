# Coaching Review — 5/10, avgTurns 8

## What's actually losing games

**1. Charizard-Y is a liability lead (0-3 as the planned Mega in "standard"/"sand").** Drogon died T1 to Power Gem, T4 to Sneasler Rock Slide, T4 to Kingambit Sucker Punch. Every loss featured a Rock move or Dark priority we walked into. Meanwhile Dragonite-plan games are 2-0. **Zard's win is real only when the foe has no Rock/Stone Edge/Power Gem/Rock Slide user** — i.e., vs rain/sun/snow, not vs Garchomp/Glimmora/Tyranitar/Sneasler/Kingambit cores.

**2. Whimsicott is our #1 fainter (6) and only ~5 KOs.** It gets Tailwind up (3 turns, 10/10 games) then dies immediately to Armarouge Expanding Force, Sneasler Dire Claw, Abomasnow Blizzard, Glimmora Sludge Bomb, Kingambit Iron Head. Tailwind turns are being spent while Whimsicott eats a KO on T2 — we're buying speed and then not converting it.

**3. Speed control vs Psychic Terrain / Trick Room.** vs flandreblade we led Whimsicott into Indeedee Psychic Terrain — Prankster was blanked, Whimsicott died T2 for nothing, and we still never answered Armarouge (2 KOs on us). Terrain/TR teams require Garchomp/Sneasler leads, not Whimsicott.

**4. Damage model is systematically over-optimistic.** 9/20 calcs in range; nearly all misses are "more bulk than assumed" (Moonblast→Garchomp 85→63, Draco→Espathra 136→83, Ice Beam→Abomasnow 31→17). We commit to "this KOs" lines that don't KO, then lose tempo. Assume **~0.8× the predicted upper roll** before declaring a KO; require low-roll lethality.

**5. firstKO them → 1-3.** When we lose the opener we almost never recover. Lead safety > lead damage.

## Concrete changes

- Preview rule: if opponent team contains **Tyranitar, Glimmora, Garchomp, Sneasler, Kingambit, or any Rock-move user**, Mega Dragonite, not Charizard. Charizard only vs rain/snow/sun/water cores.
- If opponent has **Indeedee/Armarouge/Farigiraf (terrain or TR)**, do not lead Whimsicott; lead Sneasler (Fake Out) + Dragonite and Encore/Taunt from the back.
- Treat Tailwind as turn-2 insurance, not turn-1 reflex, when the lead is a KO target; consider Protect+double-attack to kill the Expanding Force/Glimmora user first.
- Only claim a KO if **min roll ≥ remaining HP after a 0.8 bulk multiplier**; otherwise double-target.
- Don't leave Whimsicott in once Tailwind is set and it's below ~60% — pivot to Milotic (6 KOs, our best scorer).
