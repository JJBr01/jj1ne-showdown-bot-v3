# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Kingambit as the psyterrain lead** is now consistently correct: Protect on T1 denied Pawmot's Will-O-Wisp, and Kowtow Cleave later one-shot Indeedee *through* its Focus Sash margin while being immune to Expanding Force.
- **Turn-1 Mega Drogon + sun Heat Wave** removed Mega Victreebel immediately — the sun conversion remains our single highest-value turn-1 action.
- **Milotic Ice Beam** correctly finished Zoroark-Hisui at 4% rather than Protecting; that was the right last-mon line.
- Protecting Kingambit on T1 against a listed 129-186% OHKO was exactly the "threatened slot must Protect" rule being honoured.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ Psyterrain lead preference (`Charizard|zk`) applied.
- ✅ Mega-evolve Drogon turn 1 for sun.
- ✅ Protect the slot facing a guaranteed OHKO instead of trading.
- ❌ **VIOLATED the team's oldest hard rule**: "Never Earthquake with Garchomp while Kingambit is beside it." Turn 2 did exactly that, for 122 damage to our own Kingambit.
- ❌ Did not account for Illusion despite Zoroark-Hisui being on preview.

## 3. What Failed
- **T2 Earthquake self-damage (game-losing).** Kingambit 207 → 85. On T3 it survived Dazzling Gleam at 12 HP and died to burn. Without the friendly fire, Kingambit lives into T4 with a healthy board and we win a 2v1. The calc even *displayed* the ally damage and the pilot overrode anyway.
- **Illusion blindness.** Slot B "Pawmot" was Zoroark-Hisui. Every number in that turn's calc was fiction; we spread into a target whose real bulk and typing differed.
- **Drogon traded itself on turn 1.** Heat Wave KO'd Mega Victreebel but Drogon fainted in the same action — an unscouted Mega ability. Weather Ball single-target would almost certainly have secured the same KO without the spread exposure.
- **T3 action split.** Garchomp EQ (again next to Kingambit, again blocked by rule) + Kowtow Cleave meant Garchomp died to Dazzling Gleam without contributing a KO.
- **Endgame with no speed control.** Milotic burned, alone, against a draining Mega — an unwinnable stall race.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. Make friendly-fire Ground moves a **hard block**, not a score penalty. No exceptions, ever, when a grounded ally is adjacent.
2. With Zoroark/Zoroark-Hisui previewed, annotate the board every turn: "one visible foe may be an Illusion — prefer single-target."
3. Against a first-time Mega-evolving foe, prefer the single-target KO from our Mega over a spread move.
4. Keep the back line functional: Milotic needs Icy Wind so the 2v2 endgame still has a speed tool.
