# Analysis

**Record this block: 6-4 (43-42 overall).** Wins came from clean, fast games where Drogon or Kingambit snowballed (klooooo 3 turns, anonimo a 3 turns, Comedie 4 turns). Losses were all the same shape: one opposing carry (Alakazam-Mega, Archaludon, Garchomp-Mega-Z, Tyranitar-Mega) was never removed and killed us one Pokémon at a time.

**roboshers (psyterrain, loss)** — the clearest coachable error. T1 the plan was sound (kill Torkoal), but Sleep Powder + the engine's forced switch left us behind. From T3 onward Alakazam-Mega was *the* win condition and we never focused it: T4 we Protected Dragonite and poked with Ice Beam (14%), T5 we Protected Milotic and fired Draco into a Protect-less Alakazam that Helping-Hand'd through. Four turns of single-target chip on an 86%-HP Alakazam is exactly the "chipping the carry loses" pattern we already flagged for Archaludon. The rule must generalize: **double-attack the Psychic carry the turn it appears.**

**nerumy07 (trickroom/psyterrain, loss)** — we traded Milotic down to 1 HP on T1, then Protected with it on T2, which accomplished nothing while Mega Garchomp-Z burned Kingambit to 10%. We lost both actives on T3 and the game was over. A 1-HP Pokémon should attack or leave.

**jintah (sand, loss)** — Tyranitar-Mega Rock Slide killed four of ours. We benched nothing and left Garchomp + Milotic both in spread range on T5. The existing sand rule is correct but was not followed; renewing it.

**itzjackalope (rain, loss)** — Dragonite KO'd both Maushold and Pelipper by T2 (good), then Archaludon Flash Cannon'd Whimsicott, Dragonite, Milotic and Garchomp sequentially. Same disengage rule applies; it was ignored once Dragonite hit −4.

**Trend:** psyterrain 6-15 and rain 5-13 remain the worst archetypes, and both losses share "we had the KO tools but split damage across turns." Sun (8-9, Charizard) and standard (14-18) are fine — keep Charizard default.
