# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 focus fire on Hisuian Arcanine**: Scald + Dragon Pulse removed the Sash/Head Smash threat immediately. Denying their fastest burst piece on turn 1 is exactly the "deny the most valuable win condition" directive.
- **Preview selection** (Milotic + Dragonite lead, Whimsicott + Scarf Garchomp back): Competitive baited their Intimidate, and Scarf Garchomp served as natural speed insurance once no Tailwind was ever set — it dealt 181% and closed the game.
- **Turn 4 double-target on Mega Garchomp-Z**: Moonblast + Dragon Claw killed their remaining win condition. Correct application of focus-fire over splitting.
- **Turn 5 Protect on Whimsicott** vs a listed Flare Blitz OHKO was textbook Protect discipline.
- **Turn 6 skipping Tailwind** with only Milotic in the back correctly followed the existing coach rule.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- Applied: "Protect the slot with a listed OHKO incoming" (T5).
- Applied: "Tailwind has no value when only Scarf Garchomp/one mon remains" (T6).
- Applied: Focus-fire the opposing Mega rather than splitting damage (T4).
- Applied: never EQ next to a non-Flying ally.
- **Violated: OPPOSING-MEGA PRE-COMPUTE.** On T2 we double-Protected while Garchomp mega-evolved for free; on T3 we attacked with a 35% Mega Dragonite into a Power Gem the calculator itself listed as a KO.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 2 double-Protect (engine fallback)** — zero threat was actually resolved. Both their moves were blocked but neither would have been lethal, and the turn gifted Garchomp a free Mega Evolution. That is a full 2-action economy loss.
2. **Turn 3 Mega Dragonite attacked at 35% into Mega Garchomp-Z Power Gem.** The calc line read "Dragonite threatened by Garchomp-Mega-Z Dragon Claw (59-104% KO)" and Power Gem had already been revealed. Our Mega died having scored 0 KOs. Protect or switching to Kingambit was free.
3. **Damage model conservatism**: 4 of 6 calc checks over-performed (Dragon Claw 32-33% vs predicted 19-28%). We planned two-turn KOs where one turn plus chip was enough.
4. **Whimsicott traded on T6** when Garchomp alone was finishing Rillaboom; a second Protect would have kept our Prankster alive for the endgame.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- **40% + super-effective = Protect/switch, always.** Especially the Mega.
- **No free Mega turns**: when a foe holds an unused Mega Stone and nothing on our side faces a listed ≥80% hit, at least one action must attack the stone holder.
- **Against Rillaboom, switch, don't Protect-cycle**: Grassy Glide re-threatens the same slot every turn independent of our speed control.
