# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Drogon's turn-1 Protect** correctly blanked Quaquaval's Aqua Step; the Mega came online safely.
- **Garchomp Stomping Tantrum** was an efficient, guaranteed removal of Houndoom (83-171% displayed, clean KO).
- **Kowtow Cleave through Follow Me** got the Indeedee-F KO, confirming Kingambit as the correct Psychic Terrain answer in principle.
- **Whimsicott Moonblast** eventually deleted the +2/+2 Quaquaval — the right target, four turns too late.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ Protected Drogon on turn 1 against a faster physical attacker.
- ✅ Never clicked Sucker Punch into a possible Protect / Psychic Terrain.
- ❌ **Violated "Never lead Kingambit in front of a Fighting-type"** — Quaquaval's Close Combat was displayed at 156-229% OHKO on turn 1 and Kingambit was still the lead.
- ❌ **Violated "punish setup immediately"** — Quaquaval boosted Speed (T2), Attack (T3), Speed+Attack (T5) and was never targeted until turn 4.
- ❌ **Violated "a Mega below the incoming damage threshold switches"** — Drogon stayed in at 37% into two live attackers and died with 0 damage dealt.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Lead construction (fatal).** Open Team Sheets showed Quaquaval, Golisopod, Houndoom, Indeedee-F. Kingambit had a hard OHKO threat staring at it and a Houndoom Heat Wave threat beside it. The correct lead was **Whimsicott + Drogon**: Prankster Encore is the single best tool against an Aqua Step snowballer, and Whimsicott was instead buried in the back.
2. **Ignoring the actual win condition.** Quaquaval is a self-boosting sweeper. We spent turns 1-3 on Houndoom (a 100 HP support-ish Fire type) while Quaquaval accumulated +1 Spe and +1 Atk for free. By turn 5 it was +2 Spe / +1 Atk and OHKO'd our entire remaining board.
3. **Drogon turn 2.** At 57/153 facing Quaquaval (Aqua Step already shown) plus Houndoom (Fire STAB), Drogon used Ancient Power into a 15% Houndoom. Even a hit kills a 15% Houndoom — but Heat Wave killed Drogon first. The Mega needed to **switch out**, letting Garchomp or Milotic absorb.
4. **Turn 3 switch direction.** We switched Kingambit (62/207) out and kept Garchomp in front of a displayed 380-1554% Triple Axel. Garchomp — the 4x-losing piece to Ice — was the one that should have left.
5. **Turns 4 and 5 double-targeting.** Both slots attacked the same foe twice while both of our Pokémon sat under displayed OHKOs. No Protect, no switch, no hedge: pure 2-action commitment with zero defensive value in a losing position.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Add **self-boosting attackers** (Aqua Step, Scale Shot, Moxie/Chilling Neigh, Fell Stinger) to the "setup sweeper" category. The moment a foe gains a stat from its own attack, it becomes the mandatory focus target.
- **Whimsicott must be a lead** against any board with a boosting physical attacker — Prankster Encore on an Aqua Step lock is a free win condition denial.
- With open sheets, run the **Kingambit lead ban** against Fighting coverage. Kingambit is a mid/late-game cleaner into Psychic Terrain, not a turn
