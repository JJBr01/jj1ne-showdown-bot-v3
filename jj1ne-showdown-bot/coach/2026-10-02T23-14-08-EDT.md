- **Turn 2 Protect on Drogon**: Arcanine-Hisui Head Smash displayed 235-384% OHKO. Protect while Whimsicott set Tailwind is the canonical "Defensive Safe Play." This single click is why we won — Drogon took the last KO on turn 7.

Milotic ate the Salamence Intimidate for **+2 SpA (Competitive)** and the following turn Ice Beam deleted Mega Salamence (123-226%). Textbook repositioning.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 6 — Milotic Scald into Arcanine, which Protected on Turn 5.** The coach note "After an opposing slot Protects, the following turn must target the other slot" has been written three times and is still only a prompt hint. Arcanine Protected again; the action was blanked, and the free turn let Dragapult's Phantom Force crash Milotic from 91 → 10 HP. In a 4-turn-longer game that loses us the match.
2. **Turn 6 — Kingambit at 1/207 clicked a Life Orb contact move.** It got the Dragapult KO, but the recoil killed it, handing them a free turn at 2v1. Protect was available and Kingambit still beats Dragapult the next turn under our Tailwind.
3. **Semi-invulnerable targeting**: on T5 we aimed Kowtow at a Dragapult that was mid-Phantom Force and missed/whiffed timing. Both actions should have gone to the grounded slot.
4. Two accuracy whiffs (Moonblast T1, Kowtow T5) — unavoidable, but reinforces the ahead-in-count accuracy bias already in `adjustScore`.
