## 3. What Failed
**The Wide Guard lock (the game-losing error).** From turn 4 onward, Pelipper clicked Wide Guard every single turn. Scarf Garchomp was Choice-locked into Rock Slide and we kept firing it: four consecutive turns, zero damage, half our action economy surrendered permanently. The correct line on turn 5 was **switch Garchomp out** to break the Choice lock and bring Milotic/Kingambit — the Choice lock is explicitly listed in our own rules as a reason to switch.

**Turn 2 (engine fallback)** we double-Protected while Pelipper got a completely free Tailwind. That is the turn the game was lost: they gained speed, and we never regained it.

**Turn 4** a 1 HP confused Whimsicott clicked Tailwind. 50% to self-hit; it self-hit and died. Tailwind at 1 HP with confusion is a coin-flip we didn't need — and the engine then had Drogon enter into a +0 Gyarados.
