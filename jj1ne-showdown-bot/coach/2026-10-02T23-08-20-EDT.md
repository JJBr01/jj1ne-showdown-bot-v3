# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**Turn 1 — correct archetype lead.** Whimsicott + Mega Charizard-Y into a standard sheet (Volcarona / Salamence / Maushold / Annihilape / Rillaboom / Toxapex) is the team's best-performing plan (19-14 historically). Tailwind went up on turn 1 per the standing rule, and it held for the entire (short) game. Drogon Mega'd through the Fake Out flinch — mechanically correct and worth noting: the Mega still happened.

**Turn 3 — the game-winning decision.** Salamence Mega Evolved on turn 3 and was the single most valuable win condition on their board (Hyper Voice OHKOs Whimsicott, Draco Meteor 55% to KO Drogon). With Tailwind up we moved first, and we correctly:
- focused BOTH actions into it rather than splitting,
- chose **single-target** moves (Moonblast + Ancient Power) instead of Heat Wave, because Toxapex had already revealed Wide Guard twice.

That is exactly the Masterclass sequence: *remember what they keep doing → after Wide Guard, don't spread into it*. Salamence died before acting. Game over.

**Resource discipline.** No Prankster into Dark, no Earthquake into an ally, no Protect-stall, no overkill stat-drop move.

## 3. What Failed

**Turn 2 — a full Mega action donated to Wide Guard.** Toxapex is one of the most famous Wide Guard users in the format. We clicked Heat Wave with a projected 111–131% on Rillaboom; Wide Guard activated on *both* slots and we dealt zero. Rillaboom survived at 31% and pivoted out with U-turn for free, resetting its chip and bringing in Mega Salamence on *their* terms. That is two of our four turn-actions neutralized in one turn, and it is the only reason this game went to turn 3 at all.

**Duplicate damage allocation.** Even if Wide Guard had not existed, Whimsicott's Moonblast (28–33%) into a Rillaboom that Heat Wave was projected to OHKO was redundant. The correct allocation was Heat Wave + Moonblast *at Toxapex*, or Heat Wave + Encore/positioning.

**Infestation trap not accounted for.** Drogon was partially trapped from turn 1 and bled 19 HP per turn (153 → 61 by the end). We never registered that our Mega **could not switch out**. In a longer game that is a dead Charizard. A trapped Mega's decision tree is only "Protect or kill" — repositioning is off the table, and the value of Protect rises sharply.

**Variance absorbed for free.** The turn-1 Fake Out flinch cost Drogon its action. Nothing to do there, but it reinforces why the game had to be closed fast.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time

Only fire Heat Wave / Rock Slide / Dazzling Gleam if it **KOs both slots** (so the Wide Guard turn still costs them tempo) or if that foe has already used its action.
