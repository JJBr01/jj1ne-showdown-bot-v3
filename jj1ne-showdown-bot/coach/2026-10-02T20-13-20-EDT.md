# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Lead plan**: Mega Charizard + Whimsicott Tailwind into sand is the simulation-validated 70.7% plan, and it won in 3 turns.
- **Turn 1 Weather Ball**: Drought sun turned Weather Ball into a Fire-type nuke and OHKO'd Sneasler — removing their Fake Out/Dire Claw carry immediately. The 2-action economy tilted our way instantly.
- **Turn 2 Protect on Drogon**: Drogon at 37% was listed at 75–125% from Rock Slide / Power Gem. Protect was mandatory and blanked Mega Garchomp's Dragon Pulse. Protect was correctly assigned to the slot with the larger listed OHKO (per the active PROTECT-THE-THREATENED-SLOT proposal).
- **Turn 3 focus fire**: Dragon Claw + Weather Ball deleted Mega Garchomp-Z before it could fire Power Gem. Denying them "speed and power" simultaneously closed the game.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied correctly:
- Protect Drogon vs sand / 4x-Rock coverage (game-saving on T2).
- Tailwind attempted turn 1 at full HP.
- Focus-fire the single foe that OHKOs our Mega rather than splitting.
- Protect went on the genuinely threatened slot.

Violated:
- **"Do not lead Whimsicott when the opponent previews a Fake Out user"** — Sneasler was in preview. Fake Out flinched Whimsicott, deleting the turn-1 Tailwind and burning the Sash buffer. Whimsicott died turn 2 before Tailwind ever landed at full value.
- **SPREAD-ATTACKER SASH GUARD** — Rotom-Heat (Overheat/Discharge) was previewed; Whimsicott's Sash should have been treated as already spent.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Whimsicott deleted by turn 2 (again)** — 133 faints in 213 games. Fake Out chip (29 HP) + Overheat finished it. We got 1 Tailwind turn out of our only speed-control piece.
2. **Damage calc is badly wrong for sun Weather Ball**. Predicted 13–25% on Sneasler; actual 100%. Same error last game (Indeedee: predicted 8–12%, actual 68%). Every move-selection decision involving Drogon in sun is running on numbers 4x too low — we are one unlucky evaluation away from passing up a guaranteed OHKO.
3. **Switch-back threats are invisible to the threat model**. Rotom Volt Switched out on T1 and returned on T2; its Overheat was never listed as a threat to Whimsicott, so Protect allocation was made on an incomplete board. The move that actually KO'd our piece didn't exist in our planning data.
