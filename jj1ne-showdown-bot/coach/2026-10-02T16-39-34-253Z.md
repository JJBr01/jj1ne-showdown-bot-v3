## Analysis (last 10: 3-7)

**The Dragonite Draco spiral is now the single biggest leak.** Against Ceej2k, Dragonite fired Draco Meteor on T1, T3 and T4, ending at -6 SpA with 102% total damage and zero relevance for the last four turns while Archaludon climbed to +3 and swept. The same pattern appears in Fell Blue, madagu and ShadowBrew: Draco T1, then a forced pivot, then the mon dies having contributed one KO. The existing "-2 → pivot" rule fires too late. The fix is upstream: only Draco when it kills, otherwise Heat Wave/Tailwind/Protect.

**Rain target priority is inverted.** We keep opening on the Prankster support (Sableye, Grimmsnarl) because it's the "safe guaranteed KO", while Pelipper lives to set rain + Tailwind and Archaludon accumulates Stamina/Electro Shot boosts. Ceej2k is the textbook case: Sableye died T1, and the game was lost to a +3 Archaludon that we never threatened. Rain is 8-22 overall; this is why.

**Kingambit still leads into Fighting.** The adjustment exists but was overridden twice this block — Fliegenmann (Armor Cannon turn 1) and ShadowBrew (Close Combat turn 2 through a Protect). Protecting it doesn't save it; it just delays the free KO. On psyterrain boards with Fighting coverage, Kingambit should not be in the four at all unless it's the 4th slot.

**Whimsicott at 1%.** Three games ended with Whimsicott held in at 1 HP "to use it anyway". Tailwind was already up in two of them. Those turns bought nothing and forfeited Encore — our only answer to Primarina/Espathra-style setup.

**What's working:** sun stays dominant (13-1 with Drogon + Whimsicott) and the c4ntw1n4g4m3 win shows the Indeedee-focus rule executing correctly — Indeedee dead turn 1, then Drogon cleaning two with Heat Wave. The standard/Dragonite plan (13-3) remains correct; it's the in-battle Draco discipline that's failing, not the preview.
