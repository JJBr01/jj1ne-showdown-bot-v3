# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn-1 lead execution was perfect.** Whimsicott Tailwind + Drogon Protect — Raichu-Y's Fake Out hit the shield, Mega went up, Tailwind secured. 6 Tailwind turns to their 0.
- **Kingambit closed the game.** 219% damage, 3 KOs (Rillaboom, Incineroar, Mega Raichu-Y). The slow Dark attacker as a late-game cleaner is now a proven win condition on this team.
- **Garchomp used Stomping Tantrum, not Earthquake,** correctly sparing Whimsicott, and ground Incineroar down from 89% to 25%.
- **Low-HP Protect discipline:** Whimsicott at 27% Protected into Incineroar Parting Shot and blanked it.
- **Drogon pivoted out at 46%** instead of trading into Flare Blitz, and lived to deliver the final Weather Ball on Milotic.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied: turn-1 Tailwind mandate, Protect the Mega vs a faster nuke, low-HP Protect rule, no-EQ-next-to-ally rule, turn-1 Mega, switch rather than trade with a threatened Mega.

Violated: **"Prankster moves fail against Dark types"** — a rule explicitly in the team-specific list — was broken on T6 (Encore → Incineroar, `-immune`). A second Encore on T3 failed because Raichu had switched out.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Two dead Encores = one full turn of action economy donated.** T3 Encore fizzled (no legal target), T6 Encore was Dark-immune. During those turns Rillaboom set Grassy Terrain and Wood Hammered Whimsicott from 94% to 21%.
2. **T2 double-target into an obvious Protect.** Raichu was healthy, was the clear focus target after surviving T1, and had never Protected. Both Moonblast and Weather Ball hit the shield; they got a free Snarl (-1 SpA on both our slots).
3. **Garchomp over-traded.** It attacked at 100% → got Wood Hammered to 5% → died to Grassy Glide on T8. The NO-TRADE rule (<=55% HP vs a listed near-OHKO) said Protect/switch.
4. **Damage estimates ran hot** on Drogon's sun moves (T1 Heat Wave checks in earlier games; Milotic required many turns here), stretching a won game to 15 turns.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Code-block every Prankster status move aimed at a Dark-type foe, and block Encore at any slot with no `lastMove` (just switched in).
- Enforce the split rule: one action at a healthy, unused-Protect foe; the other at its partner.
- Enforce Garchomp's no-trade threshold against Rillaboom/priority users explicitly.
- Keep sheltering Kingambit — it is the single highest-EV piece in standard matchups right now.
