## 1. What Worked
- **Lead choice was conceptually right.** Kingambit into a sheet containing Indeedee-F + Armarouge is the Wolfe "Must-Come" piece: immune to Expanding Force and Psychic, super-effective on both Psychic setters.
- **Whimsicott carried the game.** 233% total damage, the Baxcalibur KO on the switch-in, and the chip that forced Dragapult into Protect loops. Moonblast into the switch was the single best click of the match.
- **Protect discipline on T3.** Kingambit faced a listed 80-122% Armor Cannon; Protect was correct and it worked (Aura Sphere bounced).
- **T4 pivot.** Burned Kingambit facing a guaranteed KO → switched to Drogon instead of trading. Exactly the "switch is a real tool" directive.
- **Zero Sucker Punch clicks** on a team with Psychic Terrain access. The anti-priority guardrail held.
