# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Protect allocation was textbook.** Torkoal's full-HP Eruption was the listed OHKO on Kingambit; Protect went on the threatened slot (not the partner), while Mega Drogon's Heat Wave broke Venusaur's Sash and chipped Torkoal. Drought also flipped the weather to Sun, forcing Politoed to spend a switch-in just to restore rain.
- **Kingambit lead vs a previewed Indeedee/Trick Room team** was correct in principle (Psychic/Prankster immunity, Dark STAB into the setters).
- **Turn 4 focus-fire on Torkoal** correctly applied the "Eruption users at full HP are spread OHKO threats — remove them" rule, and the combined Stomping Tantrum + Ancient Power got the KO even through Dragonite's Protect.
- **Weather war awareness** was good: we tracked Drought vs Drizzle and knew Weather Ball's swing.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied correctly:
- Protect on the slot with the largest listed incoming OHKO (Kingambit T1).
- Focus-firing a full-HP Eruption user rather than splitting damage.
- Not clicking Tailwind into a Trick Room team before TR actually resolved.

Violated:
- **"Never let the opponent have BOTH speed advantage AND power."** From turn 3 onward Mega Dragonite was faster than everything we had and held a 110 BP spread STAB that is 100% accurate in rain. We had zero Tailwind turns the entire game.
- **"Set Tailwind on turn 1-2 while Whimsicott is at full HP."** Whimsicott never entered the field until it was a forced replacement on turn 6.
- **"Don't double-Protect / don't Protect twice in a row with the same mon when a switch exists."** Drogon Protected on T3 and T5 at 37% HP and died on T6 anyway, contributing 0 damage after turn 4.
- **"No overkill."** T6 we aimed both Moonblast and Weather Ball at a 32% Politoed that Moonblast alone was killing.

## 3. What Failed
**The core failure is a target-selection failure, not a Protect failure.**

Mega Dragonite was the opponent's entire win condition. Its KO ledger:
- T3 Flamethrower → Kingambit (KO)
- T5 Hurricane → Garchomp (KO)
- T6 Hurricane → Drogon (KO)
- T7 Hurricane → Whimsicott (KO)

**We never aimed a single attack at it across seven turns.** Every offensive action went into Torkoal (a chipped, already-weather-displaced support) or Politoed (a 77%→32% support). Those two Pokémon combined KO'd nothing. This is the exact "deny their most valuable win condition" directive inverted.

Specific turns:
- **T4**: Correct to kill Torkoal, but we should have recognized that Dragonite Protecting on T4 (it did) meant it was positioning to sweep.
- **T5**: Drogon at 37% Protects a second time, Garchomp chips Politoed for 45%. Dragonite Hurricanes Garchomp out of the game. The correct line is Garchomp **Dragon Claw into Mega Dragonite**
