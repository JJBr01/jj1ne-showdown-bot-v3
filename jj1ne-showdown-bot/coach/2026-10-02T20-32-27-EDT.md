# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 was textbook.** Kowtow Cleave into Sash Indeedee (Dark STAB, unaffected by Psychic Terrain's priority block) was a guaranteed OHKO and removed Psychic Surge. Dragon Pulse from freshly-Mega'd Dragonite OHKO'd Mega Salamence *before* Draco Meteor (which was listed at 66% OHKO on us). We went 2-0 up on turn 1.
- **Lead choice was correct for psyterrain.** Kingambit was immune to Expanding Force, and Salamence's Intimidate gave it +2 Atk via Defiant. That is exactly the Must-Come logic from the preview playbook.
- **Mega timing** (turn 1, into the piece that threatened to OHKO us) was right.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied: Psychic Terrain / Sucker Punch discipline, focus the terrain setter immediately, Kingambit as the anti-Expanding-Force Must-Come, Mega on turn 1 against a faster threat.

Violated — and these are rules we had already written down:
- **COIN-FLIP FOCUS-FIRE BAN** (active proposal, 13 games left). T2 we double-committed into Sneasler at an 83% OHKO while our own calc said Sneasler guarantees a 153–222% OHKO on Kingambit and moves first under their Tailwind.
- **"Tailwind clicked at 2 Pokémon remaining is a wasted action."** We clicked it on T3 anyway.
- **"Never create a turn where both of our actions deal zero damage."** T3 was Tailwind (never used) + a 22–42% Scald while both our mons died.

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 2 is the entire loss.** Board after their double-switch: Sneasler + Basculegion, their Tailwind active, our Kingambit (slow) + Mega Dragonite (168 HP, Multiscale intact).

Our calc literally printed the warning. Sneasler moved first, Close Combat OHKO'd Kingambit — Iron Head never fired, Dragon Pulse's 27–52% did nothing. Then Basculegion's **Last Respects** OHKO'd a full-HP Mega Dragonite through Multiscale.

**The hidden mechanic we missed:** Last Respects' base power is 50 × (their fainted count) + 50. By KOing Salamence and Indeedee on turn 1, *we* turned Basculegion's move into a 150 BP STAB nuke. Our turn-1 double KO was correct in isolation but we never updated the threat model afterwards — Basculegion appeared in no threat line on T2 (we only listed Wave Crash at 32–56%).

Correct T2 line: **Dragonite Protect + switch Kingambit to Milotic**, or **Kingambit Protect + Dragon Pulse into Basculegion**. Either keeps two bodies alive and forces their Tailwind to burn turns.

T3 then compounded it: Tailwind at 2 mons left instead of double-attacking Basculegion.
