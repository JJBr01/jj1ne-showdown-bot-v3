## 1. What Worked

**Mega Charizard-Y was the whole game plan and it executed perfectly.** 394% total damage, 2 KOs, 5 turns. The sequencing was textbook Wolfe:

- **T1 (engine fallback, but correct):** Heat Wave into Whimsicott + Kangaskhan put their *speed control setter* at 1% and their *Fake Out pivot* at 17% with one action. This is the "deny both speed and power" principle — their Tailwind went up, but both power pieces were already in one-shot range.
- **T2:** Heat Wave finished Kangaskhan and dropped the switched-in Baxcalibur to 21%. Moonblast was the correct insurance action on Kangaskhan.
- **T3:** Correct Protect discipline — Milotic faced a displayed Sneasler Close Combat (51-68%) *and* Baxcalibur Glaive Rush, so it Protected; Drogon's Heat Wave simultaneously KO'd Baxcalibur and chipped Sneasler to 5%. One action, one KO plus a near-KO. That is the 2-action economy working for us.
- **Milotic as designated closer** worked exactly as the playbook prescribes: Scald KO'd Sneasler, and it survived to clean up with Ice Beam.
- **Win-con sheltering:** Mega Dragonite never touched the field until the game was already decided. Correct.
