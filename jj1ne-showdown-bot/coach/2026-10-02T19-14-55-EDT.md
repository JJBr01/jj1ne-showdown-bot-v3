## 1. What Worked
- **Lead choice (Kingambit + Charizard-Y)** was correct for a Psychic/rain-flavoured sheet: Kingambit is immune to Expanding Force, and Drogon's Drought instantly overwrote Pelipper's Rain Dance, letting Heat Wave chip Sceptile and burn Pelipper for free residual.
- **T1 Kowtow Cleave redirect**: Dragapult U-turned out, Kowtow Cleave re-aimed into Pelipper for 62% + a burn interaction — no wasted action despite the pivot.
- **T3 Kingambit switch at 26% HP**: textbook "switch a Pokémon that is about to be KO'd." Kingambit returned later and personally closed the game, KOing Inteleon and then Dragapult with a legal Sucker Punch.
- **T4 Phantom Force recognition**: correctly identified that Dragapult was semi-invulnerable and redirected both actions onto Sceptile, securing the KO instead of whiffing into thin air.
- **T6 Protect on Dragonite** against a listed 107–188% Draco Meteor was the right discipline call.
- **Final-turn Sucker Punch** was legally verified (no Psychic Terrain, no Armor Tail) and converted the game.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ Switch-out rule at low HP (Kingambit T3).
- ✅ No Tailwind into a Trick-Room-flavoured board.
- ✅ Sucker Punch legality check before clicking.
- ✅ Protect only when a listed OHKO exists (T6 Dragonite), not as a default.
- ❌ **Violated the ≤25% HP rule**: Drogon at 16% attacked on T4 with two live foes on the field and died before its damage mattered.
- ⚠️ T2 was an engine fallback (rate limit), so one of ten turns was piloted blind; the Ancient Power omniboost was fortunate, not planned.
