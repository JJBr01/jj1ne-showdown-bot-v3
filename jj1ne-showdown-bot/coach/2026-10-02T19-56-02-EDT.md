# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Turn-1 focus fire on the correct target.** Runerigus was the piece listed as threatening Kingambit (Body Press 56–83%) and is the classic Trick Room / Body Press body on a `trickroom`-archetype team. Kowtow Cleave (80–139%) + Dragon Pulse (30–56%) killed it before it acted. The opponent conceded on the spot.
- **Mega timing.** Dragonite Mega-evolved on turn 1 at full HP, banking Multiscale before any chip.
- **Correct move selection on Kingambit.** Kowtow Cleave, not Sucker Punch — Farigiraf (Armor Tail) was on their preview, and Sucker Punch into a Protect/status turn is a donated action. The current tactics module's Sucker Punch guardrails are doing their job by default.
- **Rough Skin value.** Kingambit took only 20 damage from the KO; the matchup never developed further.
