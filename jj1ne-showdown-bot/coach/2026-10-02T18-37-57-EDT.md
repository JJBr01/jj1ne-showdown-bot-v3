# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Lead selection was correct and data-backed.** Milotic + Mega Dragonite vs a standard Gholdengo/Incineroar/Rillaboom sheet matches our best historical plan (standard / Dragonite|zw: 17-9, 65%). Milotic's Competitive is live insurance against Incineroar Intimidate; Dragonite covers Tailwind.
- **Turn-1 win-condition denial.** Mega Garchomp-Z was the *only* foe on the board that both outsped Mega Dragonite and threatened an OHKO (Dragon Claw 67-121%, 39% OHKO chance). Splitting damage would have left it alive to fire again. Focus-firing it with Dragon Pulse + Ice Beam deleted their Mega on turn 1 — the opponent forfeited immediately.
- **Mega evolution on turn 1**, per the standing rule ("Mega by turn 2; a Mega that evolves on turn 7 contributes nothing").
- **Free Competitive proc.** Volcarona's Struggle Bug dropped Milotic's SpA by 1 → Competitive refunded +2, leaving Milotic at effectively +1 SpA and 96% HP. That is exactly the dynamic our Milotic slot exists for.

## 3. What Failed

In-battle: effectively nothing. The game ended on turn 1 by forfeit. Two small observations:

1. **Information starvation.** We scouted only Garchomp's item (Garchompite Z) and one Volcarona move. We still don't know their Gholdengo, Incineroar, Rillaboom or Raichu sets. Zero Tailwind turns were used.
2. **Marginal overkill check.** Dragon Pulse alone was 54-98% and Ice Beam alone 56-102%. Neither guaranteed the KO individually, so doubling was correct — but note for the future that this is exactly the board where the "no overkill" rule does *not* apply: when neither single move guarantees the KO on the one foe that threatens our win condition, you commit both.
3. **Structural, not tactical.** The 14-game window is 2-12 and the `scarf_garchomp` loadout is 2W-9L. The real failures are in the previous four logs (unpunished Calm Mind Primarina, Kingambit dying to Sneasler Close Combat with 0 damage dealt, Drogon deleted by Fake Out at 18%), all of which already have open proposals. This win does not change that diagnosis.
