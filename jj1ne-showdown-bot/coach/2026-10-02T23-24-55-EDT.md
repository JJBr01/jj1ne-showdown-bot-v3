# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Preview read was correct**: identifying Espathra as the Baton Pass / Calm Mind win condition and committing turn-1 focus fire plus turn-2 Encore was textbook Wolfe win-con denial. Espathra reached +4/+4/+3 but never got a single attack or pass off.
- **Charizard-Y Drought** pre-empted Politoed's Drizzle and made Heat Wave the turn-3 double KO (Espathra 1% + Sneasler 10% both fainted to one spread move). That turn put us nominally 4–2.
- **Milotic Protect on T5** correctly blanked Rillaboom Wood Hammer.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- Applied: "punish setup immediately" (Encore + focus fire), "Charizard-Y resets rain", "don't Tailwind into a bad field".
- **Violated — the decisive one**: the standing proposal *OHKO-WINDOW MANDATORY RESPONSE* ("when a slot has a displayed >=100% roll from a faster live foe, the only legal actions are Protect or switch"). On T4 Drogon was shown `Glimmora Power Gem (110-191% KO)` and attacked. On T5 Dragonite was shown `Power Gem (80-145% OHKO 70%)` and attacked. Both Megas died for ~20% total chip.
- **Violated**: "never Mega-evolve into a displayed OHKO window" — in spirit, we brought the second Mega (Dragonite) onto a board where the Rock attacker that had just killed Mega 1 was still alive and faster.

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 4 is the whole game.** Board: Drogon 36/153 (4x Rock weak), Whimsicott 1/137. Opponent brought in a **fresh Rillaboom** (Fake Out guaranteed) and **Glimmora which Mega-evolved** with Power Gem displayed as a 110–191% kill on Drogon.

- Whimsicott at 1 HP clicked **Tailwind** — a fresh Fake Out user was on the field, so that action was mathematically guaranteed to be deleted. Zero-value action.
- Drogon clicked **Weather Ball at Rillaboom** — Rillaboom was Fake Out-ing, so the KO was a free KO on a mon that wasn't attacking, while we surrendered our Mega.
- Correct line: **Drogon Protect** (blanks Power Gem, survives at 36% in sun) + Whimsicott anything. Drogon at 36% in sun still OHKOs Rillaboom next turn and threatens Glimmora; we would have been 3–2 with the Mega alive.

**Turn 5 compounded it**: Dragonite entered at full Multiscale but Power Gem was still an OHKO (80–145%, 70%). We attacked for 12–21% on Glimmora instead of Protecting or switching to Milotic. Second Mega spent for nothing.
