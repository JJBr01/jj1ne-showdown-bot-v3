## What's going on (66-136, 4-6 in the last 10)

**1. Kingambit leads keep dying on turn 1.** Two of the five losses opened with Kingambit getting erased or neutered immediately (Armarouge Armor Cannon 100%, Annihilape forcing a Protect-then-switch). The "Kingambit is Psychic-immune, lead it vs Indeedee" heuristic is costing us games because every Psychic Terrain team pairs Indeedee with a Fighting or Fire special attacker that OHKOs it. Psychic immunity is worthless if Armor Cannon/Close Combat kills first. The coach note already says this ("Kingambit dies turn 1 to any Fighting move") — it needs to be a hard preview rule including *special* Fire/Fighting.

**2. Psychic Terrain is still our worst archetype (8-23).** The repeated error is splitting turn 1: we chip the Expanding Force user with one attack while Indeedee survives, sets Trick Room/Follow Me, and the terrain keeps boosting spread damage all game. Indeedee is the lynchpin — kill it and Expanding Force becomes a single-target 80 BP move with no terrain bonus.

**3. -2 Dragonite keeps attacking.** vs klbvnvcpo it fired Heat Wave three straight turns at -2 while Mawile/Pelipper picked off the team; vs Atropos86 the same. The rule exists but the pilot overrides it because "Heat Wave probably KOs." Make it unconditional: -2 and a Mega on the field = switch.

**4. Mega Mawile is a new Metagross-class problem.** Huge Power Play Rough did 93% to Kingambit and 86% to Milotic. We have no safe body; only the Flying/Fire Dragons can fight it, and only by hitting it, not standing in front.

**5. What's working:** Mega Dragonite + Whimsicott standard plan (13-3) and sun Charizard (12-1) are both strong — keep them. The two wins this set were both clean turn-1 focus-fire KOs (Lucario, Archaludon) that denied the opponent their enabler. That is the pattern: kill the enabler turn 1, don't split.
