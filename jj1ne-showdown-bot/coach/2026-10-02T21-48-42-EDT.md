# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Drought overwriting rain** on Charizard's entry was correct macro — it cut Politoed/Pelipper Water damage.
- **Turn 1 Heat Wave** broke Vivillon's Focus Sash and chipped Gengar; the spread choice was sound given Whimsicott had to Protect.
- **Garchomp's Earthquake on T4** was genuinely good value: it hit the freshly switched Politoed for 36% and Incineroar for 57% while Drogon (Flying) was immune.
- **Milotic finishing Vivillon** with Scald was the one clean sequence of the late game.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- *Applied:* "Protect Whimsicott turn 1 vs a Sludge Bomb OHKO" — correct, Whimsicott survived T1.
- *Applied:* Drought reset of enemy weather.
- **Violated:** "Prankster moves fail against Dark types" — we Encored Incineroar. This is a team-specific standing rule and it was broken outright.
- **Violated:** "Against Perish Song + Shadow Tag: KO the trapper fast (focus fire, don't split)". We split: T1 Protect + Heat Wave, T2 Encore (failed) + asleep, T3 Moonblast (14-20%) + Weather Ball. Gengar lived at 81% after three turns and the perish clock did its job.
- **Violated:** "Mega Entry Gate / win-con sheltering" — Mega-evolving Drogon on turn 1 into a Sleep Powder lead handed away the Mega for the entire game.

## 3. What Failed
1. **T2 Encore into Incineroar (Dark + just switched in).** Two independent reasons the move could never work. In a 2-action economy with a perish clock, that is half a turn donated.
2. **Turn-1 Mega.** Drogon Mega'd, got Sleep Powdered, was then Fake-Out flinched, then perished — it used exactly ONE move all game. The Mega should have been banked.
3. **No focus fire on Gengar.** Gengar was the Shadow Tag trapper; killing it on T1-T2 was the ONLY way to escape Perish Song. Our combined output on it across three turns was ~55%.
4. **T5 & T6 Earthquake into a Flying-type.** Vivillon is immune. Both EQs dealt **zero** to any foe and **145 total damage to our own Milotic**, which was the direct cause of Milotic's death and the loss. This is the single worst technical error in the game.
5. **No bench left.** With perish counts active and an empty bench, the game was lost mechanically on turn 5.
6. **Whimsicott never set Tailwind** and died with 19% total damage dealt.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- vs **Gengar-Mega preview**: do not Mega turn 1. Commit both actions to Gengar on turns 1-2 (Moonblast + Weather Ball ≈ 95-133%). Keep two bench mons alive specifically to reset perish counters.
- **Status-move legality check** every time: is the target Dark (Prankster immune)? Did it actually move last turn (Encore legality)? Does it have Good as Gold?
- **Spread-move self-damage check**: if all living foes are Ground-immune and our partner is grounded, Earthquake is strictly negative. Click Dragon Claw or Rock Slide.
