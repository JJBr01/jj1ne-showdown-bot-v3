# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 focus-fire on Raichu-Mega-Y.** Moonblast chip + sun Weather Ball removed the Zap Cannon threat before it ever moved. Correct application of "deny speed and power."
- **Turn 2 double-target on Staraptor.** Whimsicott was dead regardless, so trading its action for guaranteed damage was right; Drogon cleaned the KO.
- **Mega evolved turn 1.** No repeat of the turn-7 Mega mistake.
- **Damage model is calibrated:** 3/3 calc checks inside range.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- Followed: Mega by turn 2; focus-fire the single biggest threat; no Sucker Punch misfires.
- **Violated — "Whimsicott's first action of the game is Tailwind."** It clicked Moonblast on turn 1 *and* turn 2. We finished the game with **0 Tailwind turns**. Every subsequent turn we moved second.
- **Violated — "Assume Fake Out from a fresh Rillaboom lead; do not spend the Mega's action on a committal attack."** Rillaboom switched in on turn 2's end; on turn 3 our 18% Drogon clicked Weather Ball and was deleted by Fake Out before acting.
- **Violated — "Setup is a win condition: punish it now."** Primarina used Calm Mind on turns 1, 3 (and effectively 2/4 of pressure) reaching +4/+4 entirely unmolested except for two Stomping Tantrums.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Zero speed control.** Whimsicott's purpose on this team is Tailwind. Two Moonblasts (52% total damage, 0 KOs) was a strictly worse use of the same two actions, and it left Garchomp/Milotic moving last versus a +4 Primarina.
2. **Our Mega died to a priority move at 18% HP.** The threat table literally listed Drogon as threatened; a fresh Rillaboom is a 100%-likely Fake Out. Protect or switch were both available. This is the single most expensive error of the game — Weather Ball into Rillaboom would have KO'd it (155-188%) had we moved, but Fake Out is +3 priority and *always* resolves first. The move was unwinnable by design.
3. **Primarina was never the target.** It got four boosting turns. By turn 4 it OHKO'd Garchomp with Moonblast from 21% HP — a textbook "a foe at 2% HP deals 100% damage" scenario combined with "never let them have power."
4. **Milotic switched into Rillaboom + Grassy Terrain** and was OHKO'd by Wood Hammer (139-183% predicted) without taking an action of value.
5. **Zero Protects clicked in a 4-turn game** where two of our Pokémon were under listed OHKO threat.
