## Analysis

**Record 63-126 (50%).** The last 10 (6-4) show two repeated, fixable errors.

### 1. Rage Fist chip-feeding (SilverSnowDrop, 0-4 sweep)
Annihilape KO'd **all four** of our Pokémon. Every hit we landed on it *increased* Rage Fist's power (+50 BP each), so our "chip now, KO later" plan made it stronger each turn. Worse: T2 Follow Me and T4 Rage Powder redirected our single-target kill shots, giving it two free turns. This is structurally the same failure as Contrary Incineroar and Stamina Archaludon — a foe that gets stronger from our attacks must be removed in one burst or disengaged from entirely. Correct T1 line: Moonblast + Heat Wave **both on Annihilape** (not spread into Maushold), or switch Drogon out immediately.

### 2. Redirection discipline
We fired single-target attacks into an active Follow Me/Rage Powder twice in one game. Only spread moves (Heat Wave) ignore redirection. On a redirect turn, the right plays are Tailwind, Protect, Recover or a switch.

### 3. Sand is still the worst matchup (2-9)
vs the Ars Phone: Drogon died T2 to Mega Tyranitar Rock Slide despite the "Protect Drogon vs Tyranitar" rule — we Solar Beamed instead. Mega Tyranitar Rock Slide now has **13 career KOs** on us. The adjustment to bring Charizard into sand is backed by old sim data, but the live record (2-9) says Drogon simply dies before contributing. Keep Charizardite as the Mega *slot holder* but lead Whimsicott + Milotic and kill Tyranitar with Dragonite Draco the turn it Megas.

### 4. Mega Glimmora
Same pattern as Mega Alakazam: a Mega whose STAB OHKOs both our Megas got ignored for two turns while we killed support. Focus it the turn it Megas.

### What's working
Sun 12-1 and the Dragonite|zw standard plan 11-3 stay. Drogon's Heat Wave is still our best damage engine when it survives turn 1 — the losses are concentrated in Rock/Fighting leads where it was benchable.
