## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 target selection was correct macro.** Basculegion-F's Last Respects is the single most dangerous late-game move on their sheet; Kowtow Cleave (ignores Protect, 96-182% displayed) was the right click. They respected it by switching Basculegion out — that is a win in itself.
- **Raichu removal (T1-T2).** Scald chip + Sucker Punch converted their Lightning Rod / Focus Blast speed piece on turn 2 with no wasted actions.
- **Damage model accuracy.** 5/5 calc checks inside predicted range — unlike the DougTheCat game, we were not misreading bulk. Every bad decision this game was a *choice* error, not a calc error.
- **Milotic T4 Protect** against a displayed 99-130% Leech Life was textbook defensive safe play.
- **Declining Tailwind with Whimsicott at 1 HP** correctly followed the Tailwind value gate.

## 3. What Failed

**The losing turn was Turn 3.** Board: +2 Mega Golisopod (76% HP) + Pelipper (100%) vs Kingambit (180/207) + Milotic (full). The displayed threats were explicit:
- `Kingambit threatened by Golisopod-Mega Liquidation (79-113% KO 76%)`
- `Milotic threatened by Golisopod-Mega Leech Life (99-130% OHKO 95%)`

We chose **Kowtow Cleave → Pelipper + Scald → Pelipper**. Both actions went into a Pokémon that threatened nothing immediately, while the +2 sweeper that had a displayed near-OHKO on *both* our slots was untouched. Kingambit died that turn. Pelipper survived at 19% and was never relevant again — it got Helping Hand off and that was it.

Correct T3: **Kowtow Cleave → Golisopod + Milotic Protect** (or Scald → Golisopod). Golisopod at 76% takes ~50% from Kowtow + Scald chip, and more importantly Kingambit lives to Sucker Punch next turn.

**Secondary failures:**
1. **Swords Dance on T2 went unpunished.** We saw the `-boost|atk|2` message in the log and the very next turn ignored it. This is the exact pattern flagged in the durable lessons ("A foe that boosts becomes the mandatory focus-fire target the very next turn").
2. **Leech Life + chip = negative action economy.** Every Scald we landed on Golisopod (27-38%) was partially or fully healed back by Leech Life drain (it healed 100→100, 78→95). Against a drain move, chip damage spread over turns is strictly losing. Either burst it in one turn or don't touch
