## 3. What Failed
**Turn 3 is the entire critique.**

Board: their Arcanine-H at **1 HP** (Sash consumed) and Gholdengo at 90%, which had **Protected the previous turn**. Our Garchomp at 2% HP, Kingambit at 39/207.

We clicked Garchomp → Stomping Tantrum at Arcanine. Arcanine Protected. Action wasted.

Why this was predictable:
1. A Pokémon at 1 HP with Protect available Protects — it has literally nothing else worth doing.
2. Their team had just demonstrated Protect-cycling with Gholdengo on T2.
3. We had telegraphed Stomping Tantrum twice in a row at the same slot.

- **Garchomp → Stomping Tantrum at Gholdengo** (double-target the non-Protect slot with Kingambit's Kowtow Cleave; the KO on Gholdengo is then guaranteed even if Kowtow rolls low, and Garchomp's chip sets up the Arcanine kill next turn), or
