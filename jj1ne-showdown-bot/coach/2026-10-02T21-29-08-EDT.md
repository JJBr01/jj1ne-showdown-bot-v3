# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Protect on Drogon** was correct and life-saving in the short term: Aerodactyl's Rock Slide/Dual Wingbeat were both lethal rolls on our 4x-weak Mega.
- **Tailwind went up uncontested** and our damage calcs were reliable (Iron Head → Sylveon 101–150%, actual KO).
- **Threat identification** was accurate: we correctly flagged Aerodactyl as the piece that kills Drogon.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Protect Drogon turn 1 against Rock/strong faster attackers" — applied.
- ❌ **SETUP PUNISH MANDATE violated.** Their Charizard-X Dragon Danced on turn 1 with Whimsicott (Encore!) on the field. We clicked Tailwind. Our playbook says explicitly: *"Setup is a win condition: punish it now."* We instead gave them speed control AND let them have a boost — the exact "both speed and power" state Wolfe says never to allow.
- ❌ **Sucker Punch discipline violated.** Turn 4 into a 14% Mega that had every reason to be swapped — it failed.
- ❌ **Anti-Protect targeting violated.** Sylveon Detected T5; T6 we attacked Aerodactyl (fresh Protect) instead of the slot that could not Protect again.

**Macro failure:** the opponent held Tailwind for 5 turns, we held it for 3, and they also had a +1 attacker. Speed + power simultaneously — the game was decided on turn 1.

**Mega audit:** Drogon dealt 62% damage and died. That's the "Mega finishes with ~0 damage = automatic loss signal" flag again.
