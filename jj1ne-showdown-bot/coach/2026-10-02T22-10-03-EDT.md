# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Lead choice was right.** Kingambit into Rillaboom + Wyrdeer resisted Grassy Glide and Psyshield Bash's STAB wasn't super effective; Kowtow Cleave removed Wyrdeer by turn 2 for free.
- **Drought overwrote Grassy Terrain** immediately on turn 1, cutting Grassy Glide priority and Rillaboom's terrain boost.
- **T3 Protect on Drogon** correctly blanked Rillaboom's Fake Out and the displayed Politoed Weather Ball OHKO — that was the one disciplined Mega turn of the game.
- **T6 Moonblast finish on an 8% Politoed** removed rain at the precise moment it mattered, and Kingambit's Protect that turn was free.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied:
- "Charizard-Y in enemy rain facing a Water special attacker must Protect or pivot" — followed on T3.
- "Switch out a Pokémon about to be KO'd" — Kingambit at 3 HP pivoted to Whimsicott on T5.
- "KO the rain setter first" — was the stated macro plan.

Violated:
- **"Mega-evolving does not require attacking."** T1 was the textbook case: Rillaboom (a known Fake Out user) was active and we attacked instead of Protecting. We lost the flinch AND 64% of Drogon's HP before it ever acted.
- **"Do not stack chip while under a displayed OHKO."** T4 Drogon fired a 19–28% Weather Ball into a displayed 61–107% Weather Ball OHKO and died.
- **"Tailwind is the team's single most valuable piece."** Zero Tailwind turns in a game whose endgame was decided by raw speed (Raichu-Mega-Y).
