# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Heat Wave**: Spread damage went through Sneasler's Protect and OHKO'd Rillaboom, deleting Grassy Terrain and a Fake Out user in one action. Good use of spread vs. a Protect-split board.
- **Turn 3 Protect + Stomping Tantrum**: Textbook "defensive safe play" — Protect the 7% Whimsicott while the partner removes the snowballing Unburden Sneasler. Scarf Garchomp's Ground move ignored Unburden entirely.
- **Turn 4 switch of a 12% Whimsicott**: Correct resource preservation instead of donating it to Ice Punch.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
**Applied:** Scarf Garchomp as the answer to an Unburden/fast snowball; low-HP piece Protects or switches rather than attacking; not Earthquaking next to Kingambit.

**Violated — and this one lost the game:** the standing rule *"Never keep Charizard-Y on a board containing an active Rock Slide / Power Gem user"* and the already-queued **ROCK-SLIDE LEAD GUARD** proposal. On turn 2 the engine printed `Drogon threatened by Garchomp Rock Slide (75-125% (OHKO 51%))` and we still clicked an attack. Garchomp Mega-evolved and Power Gem erased our Mega for zero damage output.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 2 — the losing turn.** Opposing Garchomp switched in on turn 1 with a visible Garchompite Z. Drogon was under a displayed 51% OHKO. Weather Ball alone was already a 71% OHKO on Sneasler; the correct line was **Drogon Protect + Whimsicott Moonblast**, which keeps the same ~71% kill chance on Sneasler and guarantees the Mega lives.
2. **Overkill double-target.** Both actions were spent on Sneasler when one sufficed, wasting the only action that could have saved the Mega.
3. **Four fallback turns** (preview, T1, T5, T6) from rate limits. On T6 the engine attacked a 16% Swampert rather than Protecting Kingambit — Kingambit fainted to High Horsepower that same turn.
4. **Wrong fourth.** Kingambit into Garchomp + Swampert + Rillaboom is Ground bait; it ate three High Horsepowers and contributed 0% damage. Milotic (Ice Beam/Scald) threatens both Ground types and walls High Horsepower far better.
5. **Focus Sash on Whimsicott, irrelevant again** — Dire Claw left it at 1 HP and residual finished it before the Sash mattered.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- **Hard Zard lock:** any active foe with Rock coverage ⇒ Drogon Protects or switches. Only exception: our move is a displayed ≥95% OHKO on that exact Rock user.
- **No-overkill rule:** if one action already shows ≥70% OHKO on a target, the second action goes to Protect/switch on whichever of our pieces is under the biggest displayed OHKO threat.
- **Preview:** vs Ground-stacked cores, Milotic replaces Kingambit as the fourth.
- **Fallback hygiene:** the engine fallback needs the same guard
