# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 2-3 Dragonite preservation.** Protect on turn 2 and the pivot to Kingambit on turn 3 both correctly answered the listed 98% Dragon Pulse KO. That's the "listed >=100% OHKO = Protect or switch" rule executed properly.
- **Whimsicott Tailwind with Sash intact** — the turn-1 Tailwind mandate behaved correctly, and Tailwind did eventually let Garchomp remove Pelipper first on turn 5.
- **Garchomp Dragon Claw on Pelipper** was a clean, 100%-accurate KO on the rain/Hurricane piece.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ `>=100% incoming OHKO means Protect or switch` — followed twice (T2, T3).
- ✅ `Whimsicott with Focus Sash clicks Tailwind on turn 1` — obeyed (T2, after the T1 fallback).
- ❌ `PRANKSTER-SUPPORT DENIAL` — Grimmsnarl used Light Screen (T1) and Parting Shot (T2, T6). Whimsicott never Encored or Taunted it.
- ❌ `KO the rain setter first, then burst Archaludon in ONE turn — never chip it` — the single most important rule for this archetype, violated on turns 1, 5, and 6.
- ❌ `Punish/neutralize setup immediately` — Archaludon used Electro Shot twice (+2 SpA) and gained +3 Def from Stamina with zero response.

## 3. What Failed
**The core failure was a negative-value damage loop against a Stamina Pokémon.**

Archaludon took Dragon Pulse on T1 (33%), T5 (−?%), T6 (23%) and each hit gave it **+1 Defense (Stamina)** plus a rain/Leftovers heal. We spent four actions making it *bulkier* while it climbed to +2 SpA. Behind Light Screen, Dragon Pulse was never going to be a KO path — yet it was the chosen move three separate times, including the final turn where Dragonite had a 46-54% roll against a 63% target (mathematically impossible KO).

**Turn 4 was the game-losing turn.** Board: Kingambit 100/207, Whimsicott 116/137 vs +1 SpA Archaludon behind Light Screen and a fresh Pelipper. Listed threats: Kingambit takes **65-97%** from Electro Shot, Whimsicott takes **120-229% (OHKO)** from Flash Cannon. Both slots were in kill range from one foe, and we clicked *two attacks at the other foe*. Result: both of our Pokémon fainted, Pelipper survived at 36%. That is the "if both of our actives are below ~50% and a foe can KO, don't attack with both slots" rule inverted.

**Zero Encore.** Whimsicott had Encore available all game against a non-Dark Archaludon that had just revealed Electro Shot. Encore locks it into a charge/boost move that deals us no damage, or into Protect. This was the single highest-value action available and was never considered.

**Tailwind spent into an empty board.** Tailwind was set on T2 while Dragonite was Protecting and Kingambit wasn't out yet; by the time we had attackers, two of the four turns were gone.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. **Treat Archaludon like a Shuckle, not like a target.** It has Stamina, Leftovers-equivalent recovery, and screens. Unless we can deal ≥85% in one action or double-target it in one turn, do not touch it. Win through the other three slots.
2. **Encore is the answer to a boosting non-Dark foe.** The turn after Electro Shot / Calm Mind / Iron Defense is revealed, Whimsicott's Encore is forced.
3. **No-trade discipline.** Kingambit at 48% facing a listed 65-97% hit must Protect or switch. Half-HP pieces don't get to "race."
4. **Pick ONE target per turn when both slots are threatened.** Turn 4 should have been Kingambit Protect + Whimsicott Encore (or a switch), not a split 66% chip into Pelipper.
5. **Tailwind timing.** Don't set Tailwind on a turn where both of our slots are Protecting/switching — it burns 2 of 4 turns on nothing.
