# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn-1 Scald burn on Mega Froslass** was the highest-value action in the game: ~21% residual over four turns converted a 73-100% Moonblast roll into a guaranteed KO on turn 4.
- **Milotic Protect on T2 and T4** correctly blanked two guaranteed Wood Hammer OHKOs (139-183% displayed). That is textbook "shield the slot in the KO band while the partner works."
- **Whimsicott Moonblast finishing burned Froslass (T4)** removed the Aurora Veil / Blizzard engine.
- **Tailwind up on turn 1** gave us the speed bracket for the first half of the game.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- *Protect discipline on a declared OHKO band*: applied correctly on Milotic twice.
- *"A Pokemon at 1 HP has exactly one useful action"*: applied on T6 with Whimsicott Moonblast — but the opponent's unrevealed Incineroar Fake Out deleted it first. The rule needs the corollary: if a priority user is on their sheet, the 1-HP slot gets **zero** actions, so don't plan around it.
- *OHKO-PRECEDENT rule (active proposal)*: **violated twice**. Wood Hammer showed a 139-183% roll on Milotic from turn 2 onward, yet on T3 Dragonite (18% HP) attacked into it and died, and on T5 Milotic attacked into it and died.
- *"Drop Rillaboom vs Fire-heavy boards"*: irrelevant here, we didn't bring it.
