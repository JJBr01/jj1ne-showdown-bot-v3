## Analysis

**Record check:** 27-50 overall, but the last 10 are 7-3 — the Mega-Dragonite-vs-Trick-Room rule and the Drogon+Whimsicott sun plan are both paying off (sun 7-8, psyterrain 5-7 now). The three losses share one shape.

**Loss pattern 1 — Drogon dies before it acts (Zetababy, pcrlbot).** In both games an unrevealed faster attacker (Raichu-Mega-Y Zap Cannon, Sneasler Rock Slide) OHKO'd Charizard on turn 1 while we attacked. Drogon has 24 faints, by far the most "value lost" on the team. The existing Protect rule exists but the pilot keeps overriding it because the aggressive line "removes both threats." It doesn't — Drogon dies first and we lose 4-0/4-1. Against Sneasler and Raichu-Y specifically, Drogon should not even be on the field turn 1.

**Loss pattern 2 — splitting damage (pcrlbot).** Whimsicott Moonblasted the Focus-Sash Sneasler twice while Ninetales freely Freeze-Dried Whimsicott, then Kingambit, then Garchomp. Two turns of Whimsicott and zero KOs. Heat Wave into Ninetales (sun already up, super-effective) kills the Aurora Veil/Freeze-Dry engine; Sneasler's sash would then have been worth attacking.

**Loss pattern 3 — ignoring the self-boosting carry (Nzell).** Archaludon got four free Electro Shots (+3 SpA) while we spent three turns on Grimmsnarl and Pelipper. Pelipper's Wide Guard and rain were real, but Archaludon was the sweeper — it personally KO'd three of our four. Scarf Garchomp exists exactly to Earthquake it; it never attacked Archaludon until turn 5.

**Keep doing:** Kingambit leading into Psychic Terrain (Kowtow Cleave OHKO on Indeedee, two 2-turn wins), Mega Dragonite + Kingambit vs Trick Room (now 3-1 in recent games), Ancient Power on opposing Mega Zard-Y.

**Note on calcs:** several Heat Wave/Moonblast results landed well below predicted (Staraptor 46% vs 71-84%, Oranguru 40-47% vs 65-76%, Grimmsnarl 44% vs 63-74%). Ladder sets are bulkier than the assumed spread — don't plan a turn on a single-hit KO against an unrevealed bulky target.
