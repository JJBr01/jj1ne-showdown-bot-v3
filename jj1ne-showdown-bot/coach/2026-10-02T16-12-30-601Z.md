## Analysis

**Record in this block: 4-6.** Two clear structural causes.

**1. Whimsicott keeps dying before Tailwind matters (75 faints, team-worst).** vs rijon18 it died turn 1 to Fake Out + Mega Gengar Sludge Bomb — we knew Gengarite was on the sheet and still clicked Tailwind into a faster OHKO. Result: 0 Tailwind turns and a loss where both Dragons later died to Toxapex. vs Ciclistautista we won anyway, but Whimsicott again traded turn 1. The rule should be: if a revealed lead OHKOs Whimsicott before it moves (Mega Gengar, Mamoswine Icicle Crash, Lucario Steel Beam), Protect or switch and set Tailwind on turn 2 — Prankster means we can set it any turn.

**2. New threat: Mega Metagross.** Psychic Fangs OHKO'd Dragonite (87%), Drogon (100%) and Whimsicott (53%) in three consecutive turns vs yash3hi. Our Heat Wave did 6% into it. Both our Megas are free KOs; Milotic and Kingambit are the only pieces that interact (Kingambit did 75% with one Kowtow). Add Metagrossite to the same bench-the-Megas list as Garchompite-Z and Glimmora.

**3. Unpunished setup, again.** That same game, Milotic Coiled three times (+3 Atk/Def/accuracy) and Metagross Psych Up'd the boosts. We correctly killed Altaria and Indeedee but never turned a single attack onto the booster, and the +3 Milotic closed the game. The standing "punish setup immediately" rule needs to override "finish the support target" once a foe has two boosts.

**4. Protect-stall waste vs Mega Gengar.** Four straight turns of double Heat Wave into a Pokémon that Protected on three of them. Perish Song was already counting down for both sides; splitting damage onto Incineroar/Rillaboom would have won the post-perish board.

**What's working:** Mega Dragonite standard plan is now 13-3 (81%) vs Charizard's 12-9 — the split is real and should stay. Sun matchups remain 12-1 with Charizard. Kingambit continues to earn its slot vs Psychic Terrain (2 KOs vs yash3hi before burn ground it down).
