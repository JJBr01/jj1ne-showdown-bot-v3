## Analysis

**Record 31-60 overall; last 10 = 4-6.** The losses cluster into two solvable buckets.

### 1. Archaludon rain (0-2 this batch, rain 4-9 overall)
Both MegaScraftyAura games followed the same script: Archaludon charges Electro Shot, we chip it from multiple angles, it heals in rain + gains Stamina Def and keeps the SpA boosts, then OHKOs Dragonite, Kingambit and Drogon in sequence. The existing "bench Drogon vs rain" rule worked (Drogon only died in the game where we brought it), but the follow-up rule is missing: **chip damage does nothing to Archaludon.** 57% Draco + Kowtow + Scald across four turns and it still swept. In the 03:13 loss we brought no Garchomp at all — our single best answer. And Floette got a free Calm Mind on T2 while we Protected Milotic; it finished the game.

### 2. Psychic Terrain / Expanding Force (Rethromono)
Expanding Force is a *spread* move at 1.5x. Leading Whimsicott + Drogon into Indeedee lost both by turn 2 — 94% and 59%+41%. Tailwind went up and was irrelevant because both setters died. Kingambit (Psychic-immune) must be the body on the field; it then cleanly ate Milotic/Kingambit later in that same game.

T2 of that game also shows a recurring micro error: we aimed **both** Moonblast and Heat Wave at a +1 Charizard-X at 38% — the single most obvious Protect target on the board. It Protected; Indeedee survived at 3% and double-KO'd us. Split the turn.

### What's working
Mega Dragonite in standard/TR (4-0 standard, 3-3 TR) and the Raichu-bench rule (two clean wins, Stomping Tantrum OHKO). Focus-firing setup sweepers (Serperior +2, Araquanid) is being executed well. Keep those.
