# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 target identification**: Correctly named Pelipper (rain setter) as the kill-first target. The opponent dodged it with a switch, but the double-attack still converted into 93% chip on Mamoswine, which later paid off as a free Kingambit KO.
- **Weather flip**: Drogon's Drought on turn 3 killed Rain, halving Water damage — and Heat Wave (plus Moonblast chip) finished the +2 Mega Blastoise, which was the correct priority target.
- **Kingambit cleanup**: Kowtow Cleave removed Mamoswine the turn it was available, exactly the right use of our slow attacker.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- The "rain setter dies first" rule was *stated and attempted* on turn 1. Good.
- The "Shell Smash emergency" rule was **verbally cited on turn 2 but not executed**: only one of two actions went into Blastoise. The rule explicitly says *both* actions.
- The "Protect / switch a Pokemon facing a >=100% roll" rule was ignored twice (Garchomp T2, Drogon T4).
- Whimsicott's Encore — the single tool that beats Shell Smash — was never clicked, despite the active proposal "Lead Whimsicott (Prankster Encore) whenever the opponent shows a Shell Smash user."

## 3. What Failed
1. **Lead**: Whimsicott + Scarf Garchomp into a team with Mamoswine. Garchomp is 4x Ice-weak and died to a *priority* move from a slower, chipped Pokemon on turn 2. That is the worst possible lead against this team sheet.
2. **Turn 1 — the game-losing turn**: Pelipper switched out, so both attacks redirected into Mamoswine and **Blastoise got a completely free Shell Smash**. Our own checklist calls an unpunished Shell Smash "a game-losing board state."
3. **Turn 2 — no Encore, no Protect, split actions**: We knew +2 Blastoise outspeeds and OHKOs both actives. We clicked two attacks anyway, and Garchomp's Dragon Claw went into a **7% Mamoswine** instead of the boosted sweeper. Prankster Encore on Blastoise (it is not Dark, Shell Smash is its last move) would have locked it into Shell Smash and reversed the whole game.
4. **Turn 3 accuracy gamble**: Heat Wave (90%) missed Mamoswine. Weather Ball in sun was the 100%-accurate single-target option on the win-condition slot.
