# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Protect on Drogon** vs Aerodactyl Rock Slide was textbook (4x Rock, they move first). Kingambit's Iron Head cleanly OHKO'd Sylveon — correct target (their Fairy spread attacker / support).
- **Identifying Lucario-Mega-Z as the win condition** was right; it is the piece that eventually swept us.
- Kingambit absorbed Hyper Voice + Rock Slide chip without dying on turn 1.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Protect Drogon turn 1 vs faster Rock coverage" — applied.
- ✅ "Focus the unused-Mega-Stone holder" — Lucario was correctly prioritized on T3.
- ❌ **"Never Earthquake with Garchomp while Kingambit is beside it"** — the single hardest standing rule on this team, violated on T3 *and* T4. It killed our own Kingambit.
- ❌ **Double-Protect / Protect-discipline** — the Protect went on the wrong slot on T2.
- ❌ **TAILWIND-AT-FULL-HP proposal** — Whimsicott came in T3 and did not Tailwind until T4; by then we were 2v3.

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 2 — the game-losing slot assignment.** Threat list literally read: *Drogon threatened by Aerodactyl Dual Wingbeat (268-367% OHKO)* and *Kingambit threatened by Lucario Aura Sphere (118-180%)*. We Protected **Kingambit** and let Drogon attack. Aerodactyl Rock Slide killed Drogon through the Heat Wave turn. Heat Wave didn't even hit Lucario (it Mega'd and Aura Sphere'd into Protect). Net: we lost our Mega for zero damage.

**Turn 3 — the Earthquake disaster.** Board: Aerodactyl (Flying, Ground-IMMUNE) + Lucario-Mega-Z. Our actives: Kingambit (grounded) + Scarf Garchomp. Earthquake can only hit Lucario — and Lucario clicked Detect. Result: `-immune|p2a: Aerodactyl`, `-damage|p1a: Kingambit|0 fnt`. We KO'd our own Pokémon with our own move and dealt **zero** damage. Meanwhile Aerodactyl freely set Tailwind. That is a -2 action-economy turn plus giving them speed.

**Turn 4 —** Earthquake again, again immune on Aerodactyl, again chipping our own Whimsicott (137→96) right before Dual Wingbeat finished it. Choice-lock made EQ repeatable, which is exactly the trap: the lock should have been reset by switching.

**Turn 5 —** Garchomp alone, locked into a move the only relevant foe is immune to. Dead.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. **Protect goes on the biggest listed OHKO threat, period.** Code-level guard added.
2. **Earthquake becomes illegal** when every live opposing active is Ground-immune/Protect-likely while a grounded ally sits beside us. Rock Slide into Aerodactyl was the move (Flying 2x, breaks Sash, hits both).
3. **Vs Aerodactyl/Staraptor-type leads: don't lead Drogon.** Lead Whimsicott + Kingambit, Tailwind turn 1 at full HP, bring Drogon only after the Rock user is gone.
4. **Choice-lock hygiene:** if the locked move is immune-walled, switch out immediately to reset.

Their Detect on the Mega turn blanked our only attacking action — Protect discipline on *their* side was better than ours.
