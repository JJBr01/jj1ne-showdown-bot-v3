# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 was ideal**: Drogon Protect (dodging Mega Blaziken Rock Slide, a 4x threat) while Whimsicott set Tailwind. This is exactly the team's standing rule and it worked.
- **Turn 5 Earthquake + Kingambit Protect** was the best turn of the game: a legal EQ next to Kingambit (ally Protected) that double-KO'd Inteleon and Mega Blaziken, taking us from 2-0 down to 2-2.
- **Kingambit Iron Head OHKO on Alcremie** (T6) was correct focus — killing the Draining Kiss attacker.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ Protect-Drogon-turn-1 vs a Rock-move threat.
- ✅ Tailwind on turn 1 (ahead of the turn-2 deadline).
- ✅ Earthquake only used with Kingambit Protected.
- ❌ **"Punish setup immediately"** — Mega Blaziken's Speed Boost is setup. It gained **+4 Speed** across turns 1-4 and we never once attacked it or switched away from it.
- ❌ **"Never give the opponent speed AND power"** — we literally handed Blaziken the speed half for free.
- ⚠️ Three of eight pilot calls were rate-limit fallbacks, and **all three fallback turns (T3, T4, T5-choice) were the losing turns**. The guardrails must live in code, not in the prompt.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 3 — double Protect immediately after their double Protect.** Zero information gained, zero damage, and a free Speed Boost. The opponent Detected/Protected on T2 precisely to bait this. When both foes Protect, at least one of our slots must attack or reposition.
2. **Turn 4 — Drogon left in a displayed 84-136% OHKO window with no Protect and no switch.** It had Protected on T3, so Protect was shaky; the correct line was **switch Drogon out** (to Garchomp/Kingambit) while Whimsicott attacked. Instead we lost the Mega and Whimsicott in the same turn, 2-0 down.
3. **Tunnel vision on Inteleon.** We spent T2 and T4 shooting at the Sash/Protect-happy sniper while the piece that actually killed us (Blaziken) accumulated boosts untouched. The win-con denial target was Blaziken, not Inteleon.
4. **Turn 7 endgame.** Kingambit 32% vs Golisopod 100%: Golisopod had *just* switched in on T6, so First Impression was live on T6 only — by T7 it was spent, but Liquidation still outsped-ish/KO'd. Kowtow left Golisopod at 8%. A Protect on T6's First Impression turn (instead of letting Garchomp eat 59%) changes that entire endgame.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Classify **Speed Boost / Stamina / Moody** as active setup. The clock is their ally; stalling is our enemy.
- Hard rule: **a slot facing a displayed ≥100% roll from a faster foe may only Protect (if it didn't Protect last turn) or switch.** Attacking is forbidden.
- **Never mirror a double Protect.**
- Against freshly-entered Golisopod: Protect the First Impression turn, then attack.
- Prioritize damage onto the escalating threat, not the Protect-spamming sniper.
