# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 double-pressure on the Trick Room/setup pair.** Moonblast + Heat Wave into Gallade/Scolipede forced Ditto's switch-in to eat 48% of spread damage, which converted into a free KO on T2. Spreading damage before their board solidified was correct.
- **Wide Guard adaptation (T4).** After seeing Wide Guard on T3, the pilot correctly abandoned Heat Wave and used **Weather Ball** to delete a **+2 Atk / +3 Spe** Scolipede. That was the single most important damage decision of the game and directly follows "after Wide Guard, don't spread into it."
- **Whimsicott endgame at 1 HP.** Protect → Encore (Iron Defense lock) → Moonblast KO. Encoring a defensive setup move turned a +4 Def Mega Aggron into a harmless target. This is textbook win-condition denial and it won the match.
- **No Earthquake next to the ally.** Garchomp used Stomping Tantrum while Whimsicott was adjacent — rule respected.

## 3. What Failed
1. **T5 (wolfe-fallback) threw our Mega.** Aggron Mega-evolved, Gallade had used Wide Guard on two consecutive turns, and Head Smash was a displayed OHKO on Drogon. The fallback engine fired Heat Wave: **blocked by Wide Guard**, then Drogon died. Zero-value turn plus loss of the Mega. This is the third game in a row where a Charizard/Dragonite died on a turn where Protect was available and correct.
2. **Kingambit contributed 0% damage and died in one hit.** Body Press was visible on a Mega Aggron. The Kingambit ban list only covers Fighting *types* and Ground *moves* — it must also cover Fighting *moves* on non-Fighting Pokémon (Body Press, Aura Sphere).
3. **Garchomp's damage was wasted.** 54% total across four turns of Stomping Tantrum into a +2/+4 Def wall. Once Iron Defense appeared, that attack was mathematically irrelevant; Encore should have come a turn earlier and Garchomp should have been the one threatening the Encore-locked target.
4. **Two rate-limit fallbacks again landed on the pivotal turn.** The guardrails in `tactics.js` must enforce the Mega-preservation rule, because the LLM is not guaranteed to be available on the turn that matters most.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Treat **Wide Guard as a 2-turn field condition**: any spread move is hard-blocked for two turns after it is seen, no exceptions.
