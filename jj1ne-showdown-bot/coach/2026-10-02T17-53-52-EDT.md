# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Target priority on turn 1 was correct in principle**: Arcanine-Hisui's Head Smash was the only move on the board that OHKO'd Mega Dragonite, so focusing it was the right *identification* of the threat.
- **Dragon Pulse removed Sneasler on turn 3** before it could Close Combat Garchomp — the Sneasler Close Combat KO pattern from past games was pre-empted that turn.
- **Whimsicott Moonblast cleanly finished Rillaboom** at 24%, trading the correct resource.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 1 double-OHKO acceptance.** Our own calc line literally read `Milotic threatened by Rillaboom Wood Hammer (139-183% OHKO)` and `Dragonite threatened by Head Smash (96-173% OHKO 95%)`. We clicked two attacks. Milotic died having dealt 83% to a *different* Pokémon that survived anyway. The correct line: **Milotic Protect + Dragonite Dragon Pulse**, or **switch Milotic → Garchomp + Dragonite Protect**. Either keeps four Pokémon alive.
2. **Turn 2 Earthquake into a telegraphed Protect.** A 31% Arcanine with Protect available is the single most predictable Protect in the game. We also used Dragonite's Protect at the same time → a fully wasted turn for both slots while Rillaboom Grassy-Glided Garchomp for 44%.
3. **Zero speed control, all game.** Tailwind went up on turn 5 with one Pokémon alive. Dragonite's own Tailwind was never clicked even after Multiscale was gone and Dragon Pulse was not securing KOs.
4. **Mega Salamence got a free entry at full HP** and immediately Draco Meteor'd our 36% Dragonite before it moved. That is the "opponent has speed AND power" board state the playbook forbids.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- **Guaranteed-OHKO guardrail**: when min-damage ≥ 100% from a faster foe, that slot must Protect or switch. This is now hard-coded.
