## Analysis

**Rain is still the worst recurring matchup (5-11).** Three of the last ten games were rain losses and all followed the same shape: we spread chip across Archaludon over 4-5 turns while it gained Stamina (+1 Def per hit) and SpA, healed in rain, and Electro Shot OHKO'd whatever was in front. The existing "don't chip Archaludon" rule is being violated because the pilot keeps choosing the "biggest threat" every turn. The correction must be positive, not negative: **kill the rain setter (Politoed/Pelipper) first.** Without rain, Archaludon loses the Electro Shot instant charge, loses recovery, and Muddy Water/Encore pressure disappears. The win vs jzbnaruto and vs galerito1 both featured early Politoed/Farigiraf removal.

**Sand is 1-6 and the Dragonite plan is 0-3.** Vs hoanglovemiku we Mega'd Dragonite into Tyranitar Rock Slide + sand chip and it dealt 17% total before fainting on turn 2. Milotic died to Steelix High Horsepower the same turn. Steelix Protected twice, wasting both of our attacking turns. Garchomp alone then 2-for-1'd. The lesson is to stop sending a 4x-Rock-weak-adjacent Mega into spread Rock and instead bring the grounded bulk (Kingambit, Garchomp, Milotic, Whimsicott) and accept a slower game.

**Psychic terrain lead error repeated.** Vs Rethromono we previewed "Kingambit leads, immune to Expanding Force" and then led Whimsicott + Drogon anyway — both died turn 2 to one move. Preview and lead must match.

**Baton Pass.** Espathra Calm Mind T1, Baton Pass T2 — our 33% chip was irrelevant, the boosts went to Archaludon. Boost-passers need a KO or Encore immediately, not partial damage.

**What's working:** focus-firing confirmed threats (Serperior +2, Volcarona, Araquanid) continues to convert; sun remains 7-8 with Charizard.
