## 1. What Worked
- **Lead choice**: Whimsicott + Mega Charizard-Y into a standard sheet. Turn 1 removed Noivern (their Tailwind setter) via Moonblast Sash-break + Heat Wave residual — their speed control was dead on turn 1 even though it got the Tailwind off.
- **Turn-2 Tailwind** (the active MANDATORY-TAILWIND proposal) was the single play that won the game. A 1 HP Whimsicott outspeeding Pawmot on turn 4 to KO it is a direct dividend.
- **Garchomp discipline**: Stomping Tantrum, not Earthquake, with a grounded Gholdengo target — correct type check and correct ally-adjacency check. The 96% roll plus Make It Rain recoil sealed it.
- **Mega timing**: Drought up on turn 1 so Heat Wave was sun-boosted.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 3 — Kingambit Kowtow Cleave into Gholdengo.** Gholdengo Protected on turn 2. It Protected again. Kingambit dealt **zero damage the entire game** and died to Pawmot Close Combat. The correct action was Sucker Punch at Pawmot (which was committing to Close Combat) or Kowtow Cleave at Pawmot.
2. **Turn 2 — no Protect on Drogon vs a Mega Glalie that Protected turn 1.** A Glalie that Protects on the turn it is obviously focused is screaming Explosion. We put both bodies next to it with no Protect; Explosion killed our Mega and left Whimsicott on 1 HP. Our Mega — the game's win condition — was spent for one Heat Wave.
3. **Resource bleed**: we won 1–0 on bodies. Four of our six fainted in four turns. That is a coin-flip win, not a controlled one.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Promote the fresh-Protect rule from "score penalty" to a **hard block** in `shouldBlockMove`.
- Add an **Explosion guard**: an Explosion/Self-Destruct user that Protected last turn or sits under 50% means the slot it would OHKO must Protect or pivot.
- Against mixed Protect-slot / attacker-slot boards, **Kingambit's Sucker Punch is the anti-Protect tool** — it ignores the Protect slot entirely and punishes the committed attacker.
- Keep the Whimsicott + Drogon standard lead and the turn-2 Tailwind; both are confirmed positive.
