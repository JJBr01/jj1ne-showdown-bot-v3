# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Lead construction**: Kingambit + Whimsicott was a near-perfect pairing against Ceruledge + Glimmora. Kingambit's Poison immunity completely blanked Glimmora's Sludge Wave — the opponent spent their entire turn-1 action dealing 0 damage to one slot and chip to the other. That is a full action-economy win from preview alone.
- **Punishing setup immediately**: Ceruledge clicked Swords Dance. We did not Protect-stall, did not split damage — Kowtow Cleave was a **minimum-roll guaranteed OHKO (108–164%)** and removed a +2 Fire/Ghost sweeper before it ever swung. This is the exact directive from the Masterclass playbook and from our own loss history (Espathra, Baxcalibur, Torterra).
- **Tailwind at full HP on turn 1**: The standing coach note ("Set Tailwind on turn 1–2 while Whimsicott is at full HP") was followed. Whimsicott got its one meaningful action before the Sash was spent.
- **Turn 2 pivot discipline**: Whimsicott at 1 HP was switched out instead of being donated for a marginal Moonblast. Simultaneously Kowtow Cleave targeted **Sinistcha, the Trick Room setter** — killing the support engine before the attackers, which matches the Farigiraf/Clefable lesson from previous games. The opponent conceded on the spot.
- **Mega preserved**: We never burned a Mega Evolution. Drogon stayed in the back away from Glimmora's Power Gem (4x) and a possible Politoed rain, exactly per the standing rule.

## 3. What Failed
Very little — but two refinements:

1. **Whimsicott's Focus Sash was spent on turn 1 by a spread move.** Our threat model listed "Glimmora Sludge Bomb (174–336% OHKO)" but Glimmora actually used **Sludge Wave**, a spread move. The Sash saved Whimsicott at 1 HP, but that means Whimsicott had exactly **one usable action for the entire game** and had to be pivoted out on turn 2. Against any team with a spread attacker in the lead slot, Whimsicott's Sash should be modeled as pre-consumed.
2. **Threat-model gap on spread moves.** The calc engine only considered the single-target Sludge Bomb. Glimmora, Gardevoir (Hyper Voice), Torkoal (Eruption) and Gholdengo (Make It Rain) must be flagged as *spread* threats so both our slots are evaluated, not just the named target.

Neither cost us the game (opponent conceded turn 2), but both are the kind of error that loses longer games — Whimsicott has now fainted **131 times in 210 games**, almost always after a turn-1 spread hit erases its Sash.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- Tag **Glimmora, Gardevoir, Torkoal, Gholdengo, Sylveon, Hyper Voice/Eruption/Make It Rain/Sludge Wave users** as spread threats; evaluate incoming damage on *both* our actives, not one.
- When the opposing lead contains a spread attacker and we lead Whimsicott, commit to Tailwind turn 1 (the Sash is a one-turn resource) — never plan a multi-turn Whimsicott game in that board.
- Continue pairing Kingambit with Whimsicott against Poison/Psychic cores: the Dark/Steel typing absorbs Sludge Wave, Expanding Force, Prankster, and Psychic in a single body.
- Continue the min-roll KO discipline on any foe that boos
