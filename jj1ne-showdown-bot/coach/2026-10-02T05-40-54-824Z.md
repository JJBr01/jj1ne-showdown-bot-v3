## Analysis

**Record check:** 3-7 in the last 10, 46-95 overall. The losses share three repeating causes.

**1. Drogon is a liability against Rock.** Three of the seven losses began with Drogon being deleted (Kleavor Stone Axe T1, Garchomp-Z Power Gem T2 and T4). The existing "Protect Drogon turn 1" rule failed because Protect only buys one turn — the Rock attacker is still there on turn 2. vs Squidissue, the pilot even overrode Protect to "double KO chance" and lost Drogon immediately. The fix has to happen at preview: if a Rock move is anywhere on the sheet, Drogon stays home. His 41 faints vs 50 KOs is the worst ratio on the team outside Whimsicott.

**2. Encore keeps whiffing.** Twice in these ten games Encore was used on a foe that could still switch (Test52152 T2) or whose locked move didn't matter (dvdrfcr — Indeedee Encored into Trick Room still got Psychic, Follow Me and a free Dragon Dance turn for Tyranitar). Encore is only speed control when the target is already committed and slower than Whimsicott. Otherwise Tailwind or Moonblast is strictly better.

**3. Chipping the snowball.** dvdrfcr is the archetype of our sand losses: Tyranitar was left at 64% after a split turn, Dragon Danced for free, then Rock Slide KO'd four of our Pokémon. The same error pattern as Archaludon. Focus fire the Mega the turn it evolves or disengage entirely.

**4. Mega Swampert** (Apophis666) is now a confirmed sweeper vs our Dragon/Ground core. We killed Archaludon AND Pelipper and still lost 3 mons to Ice Punch. Garchomp should not be brought into rain teams carrying Swampert.

**Wins** came from clean focus fire with full-health Drogon under sun (CollinGD0, 3 KOs) — Drogon is still excellent when no Rock move exists. Keep that split: Drogon only in sun/no-Rock matchups.
