# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Protect on Drogon** vs Mega Garchomp (Power Gem 62% OHKO) was textbook and preserved our Mega into the endgame, where it still took Pelipper down.
- **Turn 2 pivot** of Drogon into Kingambit rather than trading into a faster Mega Garchomp obeyed the "don't trade with our Mega into their faster Mega" rule.
- **Drought vs Drizzle**: every time Drogon entered, Sun replaced Rain and halved their Weather Ball — the preview plan was correct.
- **Slowking removal** by Stomping Tantrum was a clean two-turn kill on their Helping Hand/Future Sight support.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- Protect-the-threatened-slot: applied correctly (Drogon, not Kingambit).
- Don't trade our Mega into a faster Mega: applied on turn 2.
- **Violated**: "Against a revealed spread/Fake Out attacker on their lead, treat Whimsicott's Sash as already spent" — we led Whimsicott into a Toxicroak that Fake Out'd it turn 1 and Gunk Shot'd it turn 2. Tailwind existed for 3 turns but by then we had Garchomp + a 30% Kingambit.
- **Violated**: "Never let the opponent have BOTH speed and power." Turn 3 we double-targeted a freshly-switched Slowking and let Pelipper set Tailwind for free. From that moment Pelipper outsped Kingambit in rain and two-shot it.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Turn 3 target selection (the losing turn).** Pelipper was the entire engine: Drizzle, Tailwind, and rain-boosted Water Weather Ball. We had Kowtow Cleave listed at 51-92% on Pelipper and Stomping Tantrum available. Instead both attacks went into a Slowking that had just switched in (regenerator chip meaningless). Result: Tailwind for them, Pelipper alive, Kingambit dead two turns later.
2. **Turn 5 and Turn 6: Stomping Tantrum at 0-0%.** The calc line literally printed `Garchomp: Stomping Tantrum -> Pelipper (0-0%)` and we clicked it twice. Pelipper is Flying; Mega Garchomp has Levitate. Garchomp had Rock Slide (hits both, super-effective on Pelipper) and Dragon Claw available, or could have switched to reset the Scarf lock. Two consecutive fully-wasted actions in a 2v3 endgame.
3. **Lead construction.** Whimsicott + Drogon into Toxicroak (Fake Out) + Mega Garchomp (4x Rock on Drogon) meant both our leads were neutralized turn 1: one flinched, one Protecting. We generated zero damage in the first two turns while they got a free Mega, free Drizzle pivot and a free KO.
4. **Kingambit was used as the Pelipper answer** while being 4x-neutral to rain Weather Ball and slower under their Tailwind. Milotic (resists Water, Competitive, Recover) was left on the bench the entire game against a rain team.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- **Hard rule**: never submit a move the calc shows as 0-0%. Prefer the move that can hit *something*; if nothing can hit, switch (which also resets a Choice lock).
- **Rain protocol**: kill Pelipper/Politoed first. Removing Drizzle simultaneously removes the weather, the Weather Ball STAB and usually the Tailwind.
