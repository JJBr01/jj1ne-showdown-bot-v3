# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 2 focus fire on Pelipper**: Kowtow Cleave + Moonblast removed the Drizzle enabler. This was the single correct macro play of the game — the rain setter is the win condition enabler.
- **Kowtow Cleave over Sucker Punch vs Archaludon**: Correct. Kowtow ignores Archaludon's accumulating Stamina Defense boosts and the Reflect that Grimmsnarl set, and Sucker Punch would have failed into status/setup turns.
- **Turn 4 Kingambit Protect**: Displayed 80-121% Electro Shot (99% KO chance). Protect was correct and bought a turn.
- **Preview plan**: Mega Charizard into rain is theoretically right (Drought cancels Drizzle, halves Water damage). The plan was sound; the execution on turn 1 destroyed it.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "KO the rain setter first" — Pelipper died turn 2.
- ✅ "Never chip Archaludon over several turns" — *stated* in the knowledge base, but **violated in practice** (see below).
- ❌ **"Protect Drogon turn 1 against strong special attackers"** — violated by a rate-limit fallback. This is the third Mega lost on turn 1 to a fallback.
- ❌ **"Prankster is dead vs Dark"** — not triggered here, but Grimmsnarl's presence meant Whimsicott's Encore was always a dead move; the preview note promising "Encore locks Archaludon" was already false at preview time since Grimmsnarl guards that slot.
- ❌ **"Never click Tailwind into a losing position"** — T3 Tailwind into a displayed 159-307% Flash Cannon while the opponent already had Tailwind.

## 3. What Failed

**Turn 1 — the game-losing turn (fallback decision):**
```
|-weather|SunnyDay         <- our Drought
|switch|p1b: Pelipper      <- Drizzle
|-weather|RainDance        <- OUR SUN IS GONE
|move|p2a: Drogon|Weather Ball|p1a: Archaludon
|-damage|p1a: Archaludon|73/100   <- 27%, predicted 86-139%
```
Three compounding errors in one action:
1. **Weather Ball was a 50 BP Normal move**, not a 100 BP Fire move, because Pelipper's Drizzle fired before Drogon moved. The damage model assumed sun.
2. Grimmsnarl's Parting Shot had already applied **-1 Atk / -1 SpA** to Drogon before it attacked.
