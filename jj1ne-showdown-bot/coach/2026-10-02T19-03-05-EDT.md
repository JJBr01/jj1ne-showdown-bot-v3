# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **T1 Protect on Mega Dragonite** against Aerodactyl's listed 217–335% Dual Wingbeat was textbook: Multiscale preserved, Tailwind up, zero damage taken.
- **Dragon Pulse → Aerodactyl (99%)** followed by **Moonblast finish on T3** removed their Rock-type speed threat cleanly.
- **Kingambit Iron Head → Grimmsnarl** was a guaranteed OHKO and correctly ignored the bulkier Milotic.
- Defiant triggered off Icy Wind (+2 Atk) on T4 — the Defiant engine worked as designed even in a fallback turn.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Charizard-Y / Mega never attacks into a listed ≥100% incoming hit" — honored on T1–T3 with Dragonite.
- ✅ "Don't double-target when one action already KOs" — honored throughout.
- ❌ **Violated:** "On turn 1 (and on any switch-in turn), if a single foe is the only piece that outspeeds and OHKOs our Mega, spend both actions removing it." On T6 the fresh Floette-Eternal was exactly that piece and we gave it a free turn.
- ❌ **Violated:** "A listed ≥90% incoming hit means Protect or SWITCH, no exceptions." Dragonite at 30% HP had a listed 54–94% Moonblast KO and clicked **Tailwind**.
- ❌ **Violated:** project knowledge explicitly flags "Fairy Aura holders (Floette-Mega) with spread Dazzling Gleam threaten a two-for-one — treat as a team-wipe risk." We walked into it.

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 6 is the entire game.** Board was 2v2 (Kingambit 93/207, Dragonite 50/168 vs Milotic 60%, fresh Floette). We chose Iron Head → Floette + Tailwind. Floette Mega-evolved (Fairy Aura), moved before our base-70-Speed Kingambit, and Dazzling Gleam hit both slots: Dragonite dead, Kingambit to 5%. Milotic Scald finished Kingambit. We went from an even 2v2 to 1v2 with only Garchomp in one action.

Secondary failures:
- **T4 fallback turn** (no Claude call): Kingambit was Fake Out flinched and Dragonite only chipped. A whole 2-action turn lost.
- **Whimsicott Protect-cycling**: it Protected T2 and T3 while Milotic's Icy Wind spread chipped it anyway — it died having dealt 1% damage. After Tailwind was up it should have pivoted out to Milotic/Garchomp.
- **Tailwind at 2 mons left with Scarf Garchomp behind** is literally zero-value speed control.
- **T7 Earthquake into an obvious Floette Protect** after it had just killed two of ours — a predictable Protect turn.
