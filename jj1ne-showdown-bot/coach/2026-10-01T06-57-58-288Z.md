# Coach Report (13 games, 6–7)

## What's working
- **Rain/sun matchups (3–0).** Charizard-Y weather override + Whimsicott Tailwind is a clean, repeatable plan. Fast games (4 turns), Mega turn 1, first KO ours.
- **Killing the win condition early.** Espathra, Indeedee, opposing Charizard, Salamence-Mega — removing the enabler on T1–T2 wins games. Keep doing this.
- **First KO correlates hard with winning:** 5/6 when we get it, 1/5 when they do.

## What's losing games
1. **Psychic Terrain / Indeedee leads blow up Sneasler.** vs VictoriousDancing, Sneasler died turn 1 to Expanding Force *and* Fake Out was blanked — we lost a mon for zero value. We keep leading Sneasler into a visible Indeedee (seen twice, open sheets both times).
2. **Boosting foes not punished on the boost turn.** Torterra Shell Smash (White Herb, on sheet) went unpunished T2; it then KO'd Sneasler, Milotic, Garchomp. Corviknight Bulk Up + Psychic Seed likewise snowballed. Both were *visible on team sheets* before we moved.
3. **Sand = 0–2.** Tyranitar/Excadrill Rock Slide chews Drogon and Dragonite. Milotic contributed 57% damage and died to Brave Bird.
4. **Overestimating damage:** 12/24 calcs in range. Two "Draco Meteor KOs it" calls landed 77%. Stop treating 110%-predicted rolls as guaranteed against unknown spreads.

## Concrete, testable changes
- **Preview:** If opposing team contains **Indeedee/Indeedee-F**, never lead Sneasler. Lead Dragonite + Milotic/Garchomp; bring Sneasler as the Indeedee-gone cleaner. Also mark Indeedee as "Prankster Tailwind blocked" — Whimsicott is a bad lead too.
- **Preview:** In **sand (Tyranitar + Excadrill)**, don't bring Charizard at all; Mega Dragonite + Scarf Garchomp + Milotic + Whimsicott, and treat Excadrill (Sand Rush, Sash) as the win condition — break Sash turn 1, not turn 5.
- **Pilot:** Any foe with a **sheet-visible boosting move + White Herb/Weakness Policy/seed item** (Shell Smash Torterra, Bulk Up Corviknight) is pre-designated win condition: double-target it the turn it is on field, before it boosts. Do not Tailwind that turn.
- **Pilot:** Assume **-15% on predicted damage** when the foe's spread is unknown; only call a "guaranteed KO" if low roll ≥115%.
- **Pilot:** Don't spend Milotic turns on Scald chip vs Tyranitar; it earned 57% and died. Use Milotic only as an Ice Beam clicker / water sponge.
