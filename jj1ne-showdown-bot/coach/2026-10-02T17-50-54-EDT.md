# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Preview logic was half-right**: Kingambit is genuinely the correct anti-Psychic-Terrain piece — immune to Expanding Force, resists Psychic/Ghost, unaffected by Prankster. It did KO Indeedee-F.
- **Whimsicott got Tailwind up on turn 1** and it survived to turn 3 thanks to Focus Sash chip math.
- **Turn 4 Earthquake** was the single best decision of the game: a spread move that ignored Follow Me's ghost, KO'd the grounded Psychic-Seed Sneasler and still did 57% to Mega Gardevoir. That one move demonstrates exactly what the whole game should have looked like.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ No Sucker Punch into Psychic Terrain (guardrail held).
- ✅ Identified Indeedee as the terrain/Follow Me source.
- ❌ **Violated the standing coach note** "Psyterrain + Fighting coverage: bench Kingambit, lead Drogon + Whimsicott" — though in this case the deeper error was different.
- ❌ **Violated the Golden Rule** (never let the opponent have both speed and power): Mega Gardevoir had Pixilate Hyper Voice (spread, hits through Protect's partner), out-sped everything, and we never touched it for four turns.
- ❌ **Violated "never Protect a low-HP support while the partner is dying"** — T2 Whimsicott Protect at 14 HP while Kingambit was about to faint to spread chip.

## 3. What Failed
**The fatal structural error: we played a single-target game into Follow Me.**

Turn 1: Indeedee used Follow Me. Turn 2: Indeedee used Follow Me *again*. Our pilot wrote the calc line "Iron Head OHKOs Gardevoir-Mega (117-164% OHKO)" — **this was impossible**. With Follow Me active, Iron Head was redirected into Indeedee. The engine's own targeting display lied to us because it did not model redirection.

Result: **Mega Gardevoir took exactly 0 damage through turns 1, 2 and 3** while killing Kingambit (spread chip), Drogon and Whimsicott (one Expanding Force double-KO), then Garchomp. Four of our Pokémon died to a Pokémon we never attacked.

Secondary failures:
- **T2 Protect on 14 HP Whimsicott** — a wasted action. Hyper Voice is a spread move; it hit Kingambit through the partner slot regardless and Kingambit fainted. We should have Moonblasted or Encored Gardevoir.
- **Drogon should never have been brought.** Sheet showed Gardevoir (Mega Psychic, Expanding Force 56–104% on Drogon) + Sneasler (Dire Claw). Drogon came in on turn 3, got Dire-Clawed to sleep, and died without acting. That is a 0-action Pokémon.
- **Garchomp was sheltered in the back** when it was the only Follow-Me-proof attacker on the team. It should have led.
