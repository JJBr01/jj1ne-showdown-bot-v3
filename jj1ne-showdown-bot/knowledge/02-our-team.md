# Our team — Variant A (two Megas: pick one per game)
1. Drogon = Charizard-Mega-Y (Charizardite Y, Drought) Modest — Weather Ball / Heat Wave / Ancient Power / Protect. Speed 138.
2. Dragonite-Mega (Dragoninite; base ability Inner Focus, Mega = Multiscale) Modest — Dragon Pulse / Heat Wave / Tailwind / Protect. Speed 152.
3. Milotic (Leftovers, Competitive) Calm bulky — Scald / Ice Beam / Recover / Protect. +2 SpA whenever the opponent Intimidates.
4. Garchomp (Choice Scarf, Rough Skin) Adamant — Earthquake / Dragon Claw / Rock Slide / Stomping Tantrum. Speed 231 with Scarf (outspeeds Raichu-Y 200, Staraptor-Mega 178).
5. Kingambit (Life Orb, Defiant) Adamant — Kowtow Cleave / Sucker Punch / Iron Head / Protect. Slow (good under Trick Room). Immune to Psychic and Prankster; +2 Atk from opposing Intimidate. (Replaced Sneasler on Oct 1 after ladder losses to Indeedee + Expanding Force: +10 points vs the ladder teams that beat us, even vs tournament teams. Sneasler version: team-sneasler.js.)
6. Whimsicott (Focus Sash, Prankster) Timid — Tailwind / Moonblast / Encore / Protect. The team's speed control.

## What the simulations showed (8,000+ games vs Baltimore/Frankfurt top-40 teams)
- Mega Charizard-Y is the better Mega in most matchups (65% vs 56% on autopilot). Mega Dragonite was better vs Eric Rios' Frankfurt-winning core, Meowstic/Kommo-o, Toxapex/Zard, Sinistcha Trick Room and Lucario-Z teams.
- Whimsicott is never worth cutting: every replacement lost 7+ points. Tailwind is the single most valuable piece.
- Scarf Garchomp fixed the Raichu-Y / Staraptor-Mega / Arcanine-H / Gholdengo archetype (+11 points): it outspeeds and EQs Raichu-Y.
- Milotic over Incineroar +3.5 overall; Sneasler over Arcanine-H/Incineroar +2 overall, +7 vs the Raichu archetype, +10 vs Fairy/Froslass teams (Dire Claw).
- Best leads: vs Raichu-Y + Staraptor/Arcanine-H teams → Sneasler (Fake Out) + Charizard; otherwise Whimsicott (Tailwind) + Charizard.

## Standing rules for this team
- Never Earthquake with Garchomp while Kingambit (or Sneasler) is beside it (2x to the ally). EQ is safe next to Charizard/Dragonite (Flying), Whimsicott, Milotic.
- Any boosted Rock move OHKOs Charizard-Y (4x). Protect Drogon vs active Tyranitar in sand — even over securing a KO.
- Protect Mega Dragonite's Multiscale from Fake Out / chip; once broken, Froslass Blizzard and Floette Moonblast OHKO it.
- Prankster Tailwind/Encore fail on Dark types.
- Biggest unsolved matchup: Salamence-Mega + Metagross-Mega + Arcanine-H (~50%).
