Regulation M-C will use the same Pokemon and format as Regulation M-A . Additionally, a total of 22 new Pokemon and six Mega Evolutions will be made available and should shake up the meta with their introduction. Regulation M-C Schedule and Rules Regulation M-C Runs from September 09 until December 02, 2026 Start Date September 09, 2026 (Thu) at 02:00 UTC End Date December 02, 2026 (Thu) at 05:59 UTC Available Pokemon ・ Regulation M-C Roster , which includes all M-A and M-B Pokemon along with the new batch for M-C Restricted Pokemon ・None Regulation M-C of Ranked Battle is the upcoming ranked battle series and runs from September 09, 2026 until December 02, 2026 . This is the third Regulation following M-B and uses the same Pokemon as the last two Regulations , on top of the new Pokemon introduced here. This season keeps Mega Evolution as the main gimmick. Pokemon Ranked Battle Tier Lists Singles Tier List Doubles Tier List Will Likely Have Three Seasons like Regulation M-B Just like the previous Ranked Battle Seasons, Regulation M-C will last for 85 days . It is likely that there will be a third Ranked Battle Season for Regulation M-C just like Regulation M-B. The new Z Megas and a bunch of classic Terrain setters have been added, which are sure to change up the meta game! List of Newly Added Pokemon All Pokemon in Regulation M-C Roster All Pokemon that could be transferred into Champions via Pokemon HOME are available to use for Ranked Battle Regulation M-C. Some forms of Vivillon and Furfrou , along with Eternal Flower Floette , are still exclusive via Pokemon HOME transfer and cannot be recruited at the Ranch . Pokemon Champions Roster: All Pokemon List &amp; Complete Pokedex All Mega Evolutions Available in Regulation M-C Mega Evolutions of Pokemon that were available in the Regulation M-A and M-B Rosters are still allowed to be used. The Z Megas for the previously available, Lucario, Absol, and Garchomp, along with Mega Baxcalibur and Golisopod are also eligible for use in this Regulation. List of All Mega Evolutions Details of Legal Teams &amp; Pokemon A player's team cannot contain two Pokemon with the same Pokédex number. Your Team can have more than Mega Stone, but you cannot Mega Evolve more than one Pokémon per battle. Two Pokémon cannot hold the same item. Regulation M-C Ruleset Rankings for Single and Doubles are separate. You cannot go down a rank once your tier has been raised. No matter how many times you lose, your Tier will stay the same. An Internet connection is required in playing Ranked Battles. You will need an Nintendo Switch Online Subscription to participate in all online activities in Pokemon including Ranked Battles. Single Battle Game Pokemon Champions Battle Format Single Pokemon in your current team or registered to your Battle Team From 3 to 6 Pokemon All Pokemon will have their level automatically set to Lv. 50 during battles. Battle Times Total Time: 20 minutes Your Time: Max 7 minutes Selection Time: Each Trainer gets 90 seconds to select Pokemon. Turn Time: Each Trainer gets 45 seconds to select an action each turn. Double Battle Game Pokemon Champions Battle Format Doubles Pokemon in your current team or registered to your Battle Team From 4 to 6 Pokemon All Pokemon will have their level automatically set to Lv. 50 during battles. Battle Times Total Time: 20 minutes Your Time: Max 7 minutes Selection Time: Each Trainer gets 90 seconds to select Pokemon. Turn Time: Each Trainer gets 45 seconds to select an action each turn.
