# Analysis

**Record in this batch: 3-7.** Three recurring, fixable causes.

## 1. Garchomp-Mega-Z is an unanswered win condition (2 losses)
Both the Blz4 and Test52152 losses follow the identical script: Garchomp sits unpressured, Megas, then Power Gem OHKOs Drogon and Draco Meteor/Dragon Pulse OHKOs Dragonite. We lose our two biggest damage dealers to one Pokémon. The existing "no Drogon vs Rock-move Mega stones" rule was *stated* in preview notes but not followed (Blz4 game brought Drogon anyway, Test52152 brought both). It also needs extending: Mega Garchomp-Z kills Dragonite too. The correct four is Milotic/Kingambit/Whimsicott/Garchomp, and it must die turn 1 before it Megas.

## 2. Psychic Terrain: splitting attacks loses the game
vs mikaakim, turn 1 we split Iron Head into Gardevoir (which Protected) and Heat Wave into Sneasler. Gardevoir then Mega'd and solo-killed Drogon, Whimsicott and Dragonite with Expanding Force/Hyper Voice. The project note already says "Mega Alakazam must be double-attacked the turn it appears" — this generalises to Mega Gardevoir. Psyterrain sits at 7-17; the sole wins (blewd) came from clearing the Psychic carry fast.

## 3. Wasted turns into Protect/Detect
vs Zdijfgnj T4 we threw Draco Meteor AND Scald into a Detecting Mega Blaziken that had already revealed +Speed Boost snowball. That cost Dragonite and ultimately the game — we won every other exchange (Milotic scored two KOs). Against Detect/Protect-capable setup mons, keep one action on the partner.

## Secondary
- Protean Greninja invalidates type-based calcs (154% → 39%). Treat it as unpredictable typing.
