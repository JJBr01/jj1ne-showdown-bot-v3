# Coaching Report — 12 games, 6–6

## What's working
- **Rain/Sun matchups (3–0).** Charizard-Y + Whimsicott Tailwind blanks Pelipper/Politoed and nukes Archaludon/Espathra. Keep this plan.
- **Killing the enabler on T1.** vs marillat (Indeedee), vs econ gang (their Mega Zard), vs purevessel (Espathra) we removed the win condition immediately. First KO ours = 5–1 record; first KO theirs = 1–4. **The single strongest predictor of winning is getting the first KO.**

## What's losing games
1. **Charizard-Y into Rock/Steel "standard" teams is 0–2 (and 0–1 sand).** vs Ddgedftff we previewed Mega Dragonite… then Glimmora Power Gem did 76% to Dragonite T1 anyway and we were mega-less and down a Pokémon by T3. Rule: **if the opponent shows Glimmora, Tyranitar, Kingambit, Gholdengo, or Rillaboom, do not lead a 4x/2x Rock-weak Mega into them.** Lead Sneasler + Milotic/Whimsicott and mega later.
2. **Whimsicott dies more than anything else (6 faints, 5 KOs).** It's being left in to attack after Tailwind. Tailwind, then **switch Whimsicott out on turn 2–3** unless it's KOing something. Its job is 4 turns of speed, not 45% Moonblasts.
3. **Shell Smash / Calm Mind not punished fast enough.** Torterra Shell Smash (T2) wasn't attacked until T4 — it then killed three of ours. We used Tailwind T3 *while* it was +2. Against a fresh White-Herb Shell Smasher, **double-target it the turn it boosts**, not after.
4. **Garchomp Scarf is dead weight: 1 KO / 4 faints.** It's locked into a move, 4x Ice-weak vs Inteleon/Politoed/Abomasnow, and can't EQ beside Sneasler. 
5. **Damage calc only 50% in-range** — we over-trust spread KOs (Heat Wave on Espathra predicted 102–120%, got 82%).

## Testable changes
- Stop bringing Scarf Garchomp vs rain/snow/water leads; bring Milotic instead (Milotic: 6 KOs / 3 faints, best ratio).
- Treat "standard + Glimmora/Gholdengo" as a **Sneasler + Milotic + Whimsicott + Dragonite** bring, mega Dragonite only after Rock coverage is gone.
- Assume spread moves roll **−15% vs the calc** before committing to a "double KO" line.
