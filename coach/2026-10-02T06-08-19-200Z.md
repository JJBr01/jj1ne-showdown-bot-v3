## Analysis

**Record: 4-6 in the last ten; 50/105 overall.** Two clear structural leaks.

**1. Kingambit is being sacrificed on turn 1.** In five of the last ten games Kingambit was OHKO'd on turn 1 or 2 by a Fighting move (Sneasler Close Combat ×2, Lucario-Mega-Z Aura Sphere, Raichu-Y Focus Blast) or Volcarona Overheat, after dealing ≤1 attack. The "Kingambit is Psychic/Prankster-immune, lead it vs psyterrain" rule has become a reflex that ignores the partner Fighting attacker, which is present on essentially every psyterrain sheet (Sneasler/Lucario). Kingambit's value is late, as a Sucker Punch finisher under their Tailwind — not as a lead chip-dealer. Where Kingambit led and survived (mrchris21, ballz_uj2) we won.

**2. Protect-respect is inverted.** vs feraligatrv12 we burned turns 2, 3, 4 and 5 trying to kill a Dragon Dance Feraligatr that Protected three times; it was still at 73% and +1 when we were 0-4. We also used our own Protect (T3 Kingambit) into a Fake Out, losing Drogon anyway. The coach rule "never double-attack a foe that Protected" exists but was applied as "keep aiming at it anyway with one big move." Correct line: once a foe has Protected twice, treat it as unkillable this turn, hit the partner, and reposition.

**3. Protean Greninja broke the calcs** (predicted 154-183% Solar Beam, actual 39%). Protean means Greninja is *never* the type you calc against. It must be removed physically (Kowtow Cleave) or with Ice Beam, and planning a Grass/Fire OHKO on it is a free loss of two turns.

**4. Psychic Megas remain the worst matchup** (Gardevoir/Alakazam = 14 KOs). Drogon keeps trading into Expanding Force. psyterrain with Charizard 7-6 is salvageable, but only if Drogon isn't left in front of the Mega after turn 1.

Keep: Mega Charizard in sun (9-1), Mega Dragonite in standard (7-3), Milotic-into-Gholdengo games.
