# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **The lead was perfect.** Kingambit + Mega Charizard-Y into a Psychic Terrain / Indeedee sheet is exactly the Must-Come pairing from the preview playbook. Kingambit is immune to Expanding Force, immune to Prankster, and **Defiant turned Indeedee's Mystical Fire into a +2 Attack boost**.
- **Turn 1 was a double KO.** Heat Wave finished Indeedee (through its attempted pivot) and chipped the incoming Garchomp to 62%; the +2 Kowtow Cleave then deleted Garchomp. One turn removed Psychic Terrain, the Expanding Force user, the Trick Room threat, and their Ground attacker.
- **Correct move selection under terrain:** Kowtow Cleave, not Sucker Punch. The guardrail held.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before

## 3. What Failed
**Turn 2 was a zero-action turn.** Board: our Kingambit at 9%, Drogon full; their fresh Gholdengo + fresh Arcanine-Hisui.

- Drogon clicked Weather Ball at Gholdengo — the single most obvious focus-fire target on the board, with Protect on its sheet. It Protected. Blank.
- Kingambit Protected at 9% instead of pivoting out, so the whole turn produced nothing.
- Worse, the threat display explicitly said **Arcanine-H Head Smash = 235–384% on Drogon (guaranteed OHKO)**. Our win condition stood in front of a guaranteed OHKO and aimed its action at the *other* slot. We survived only because Arcanine chose Flare Blitz into the Protected Kingambit.

**Turn 3 repeated the identical move into the identical Gholdengo.** That is the "after Wide Guard/Protect, don't repeat into it" rule broken on consecutive turns. Across T2–T3 we spent 4 actions and dealt 0 damage while the only piece that could OHKO our Mega was never touched.

The correct T2 line: **Kingambit switches to Garchomp (or Milotic), Drogon Protects** — or Drogon fires Weather Ball/Heat Wave at **Arcanine-Hisui**, the actual threat, where Protect was not expected. Either line keeps the 4-2 lead without handing them two free turns.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. **Anti-blank:** when one foe is the obvious focus target and has Protect, send our action elsewhere or use a spread move. Never re-click the same single-target move at a slot that Protected last turn.
2. **Mega-under-OHKO:** if a live foe displays ≥100% on our Mega, the Mega may only Protect, switch, or attack *that* foe. No chip plays into the back slot.
3. **No repeat Protect at low HP:** ≤15% HP + Protected last turn ⇒ switch.
