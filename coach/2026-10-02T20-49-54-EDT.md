# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**Turn 1 was close to perfect.** Focus-firing Farigiraf removed, in a single action pair, both the Trick Room setter and the Armor Tail priority blocker. That is Wolfe's "deny the most valuable future board state" principle executed exactly: the opponent never got speed control, never got Trick Room, and our Sucker Punch/Fake Out options stayed live for the rest of the game.

**Mega Evolving Drogon turn 1 into a rain team** was correct and decisive. Sun overwrote the rain plan before Politoed ever came in, halved any Water damage, and turned Weather Ball into a 150 BP STAB. Drogon finished with **314% total damage and 3 KOs** — it won the game essentially alone from 20% HP.

**Ancient Power on T2** was the hidden MVP. The omniboost (+1 everything) is what allowed a chipped Drogon to outspeed and overpower Archaludon, Mega Charizard-Y and Grimmsnarl in the endgame. Choosing a single-target Rock move into the opposing Charizard rather than a spread was also correct per the "unknown Mega" rule.

**T5 Archaludon burst** followed the stored rain lesson precisely — we identified it as the win condition and dumped everything into it in one turn rather than chipping it across three and letting Stamina + Electro Shot snowball.
