## 3. What Failed

- **Protect was allocated to the wrong slot.** The threat board read:

  - Drogon ← Jolteon Thunderbolt: 72–135%, **56% OHKO** — and Drogon is our Mega/win-con with no Sash.

- One turn of data only, so no deeper micro to grade.
