# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **T2 switch Milotic → Garchomp**: correct recognition that Electric Terrain + Zap Cannon/Rising Voltage made Milotic a dead slot, and that Garchomp is Electric-immune. Mega Dragon Pulse simultaneously removed Pincurchin, deleting Electric Terrain and the Discharge paralysis engine.
- **Macro read on Raichu-Mega-Y**: identifying their single win condition early was right. Zap Cannon KO'd three of our Pokémon; everything else on their side was support.
- **Garchomp as the carry**: 130% damage and 2 KOs, including the eventual Raichu kill, despite being flinched twice and dropped to -3 Atk.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ Did not Earthquake into our own Flying ally.
- ✅ Did not Protect with a slot that had nothing to gain long-term (Milotic attacked once in the Ice Beam turn).
- ❌ **ANTI-REPEAT-PROTECT TARGETING violated (T7)**: Raichu Protected on T4 and was the obvious low-HP focus target; we put *both* actions into it and it Protected again. 100% of a turn surrendered.
- ❌ **GUARANTEED-OHKO LEAD RULE violated (T3)**: Dragonite was at 46% HP, paralysed, with the threat list reading "Zap Cannon 41-71% (KO 83%)". The rule says that slot Protects or switches. We attacked. Our Mega died on turn 3 after one KO.
- ❌ **TAILWIND DEADLINE violated**: zero Tailwind turns again. Whimsicott entered on turn 5 purely as a body.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Lead construction.** Milotic + Dragonite into a sheet with Pincurchin and Raichu is the single worst possible pairing. Fake Out flinched Milotic (one wasted action), Discharge then hit *both* our leads, paralysing Dragonite — breaking Multiscale's value and halving its speed before it ever acted at full strength.
2. **Losing the Mega on turn 3.** A paralysed, half-health Mega Dragonite facing a listed 83% KO chance attacked anyway. Protect there costs nothing (Raichu was going to fire regardless), and preserves our only real power source.
3. **The T7 double-Protect waste.** Not only did both actions whiff, Incineroar got a completely free Parting Shot that put Garchomp at another -1/-1 and reset its own position
