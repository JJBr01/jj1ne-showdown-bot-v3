## Analysis

**Record in these 10: 5-5.** Losses cluster around two repeatable technical errors plus the known Drogon fragility.

### 1. The Ancient Power mirror (creeperninja99)
Turn 1 Drogon used Ancient Power into an opposing Charizard that used Ancient Power back. Both got +1 all stats, but theirs survived at 1% and was *faster* than our +1 Drogon at the start of T2 — Extreme Speed from Dragonite finished Drogon anyway. We traded a Mega for a 1% Charizard. Heat Wave in our own sun does comparable damage without gifting them a speed/SpA boost, and against a sun mirror the Fire resist is irrelevant because Zard is 2x weak to Rock either way. This is now an explicit standing rule in the adjustments but it was violated anyway — restating it as a hard "never."

### 2. Drogon keeps dying before it acts (74 faints, team-worst alongside Whimsicott)
vs ten feet twenty: Sneasler was on the sheet with Rock Slide already in the meta notes, yet Drogon led and was OHKO'd before moving. The existing rule ("bench Kingambit when Sneasler is on the sheet") is too narrow — Sneasler's Rock Slide threatens Drogon just as hard. Against psyterrain + Sneasler sheets we should bring Garchomp/Milotic/Whimsicott/Dragonite and win via Scarf EQ on Indeedee, not try to race with a 4x-Rock-weak Mega.

### 3. Choice-Trick on Milotic (arayalex90)
Milotic was Tricked a Choice item, then Protected on T5, T6 and T7 — permanently locked into Protect, 0 damage all game while Rotom and Mega Garchomp cleaned up. The adjustment exists; the pilot still protected. Needs to be restated as absolute.

### What's working
Sun matchup is 13-2 — the Drought mirror plan is strong when we don't trade Ancient Powers. Trick Room wins (000red000, vavou11) came from KOing the setter/enabler quickly and letting Milotic grind. Kingambit vs psyterrain (pokemongamer1981) again proved its Psychic immunity worth the slot.
