## 1. What Worked

- **Preview plan (psyterrain → Kingambit | Charizard-Y, "zk")** was correct and is now our best psyterrain line at 9-6. Kingambit's Psychic immunity blanked Indeedee entirely; Gardevoir never even came.
- **Turn 1 Mega + Heat Wave** established sun immediately, chipping both foes while Kowtow Cleave put Milotic to 23%. Drought also pre-loaded Weather Ball as a 150 BP STAB nuke for the rest of the game.
- **Turn 2 (engine fallback)** still took the Indeedee KO, removing Psychic Terrain on schedule.
- **Turn 4 was the game-winning turn and a model of 2-action economy**: two declared KOs on two different targets (Kowtow → Milotic 42%, Weather Ball → Hydrapple 33%), both landed, 4v2 → 4v0 pending.
- **Turn 5 Protect on 25% Kingambit** while Drogon took the final KO — exactly the "when ahead, the partner Protects, never attacks" coach rule.
