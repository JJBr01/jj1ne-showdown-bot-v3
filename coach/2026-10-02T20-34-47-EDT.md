# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Preview call**: Kingambit lead into a previewed Indeedee/Psychic Terrain team is the correct, now-proven line. Kingambit ignores Expanding Force entirely and Kowtow Cleave is unaffected by terrain priority blocking.
- **Turn-1 Mega on Drogon**: Mega-evolving immediately set Sun, which turned Weather Ball into a 150 BP Fire STAB that chopped Annihilape to 41% before it could act again.
- **Punishing setup instantly**: Annihilape clicked Bulk Up (+1 Atk / +1 Def). We did not Protect-stall; we focus-fired and removed it that turn. This is exactly the Wolfe directive on setup sweepers, and it ended the game.
- **Free Flying immunity**: Altaria's Earthquake did nothing to Drogon and chipped its own Annihilape for 12% — the "Flying Mega beside Ground spread" rule paid off passively.

## 3. What Failed

1. **Damage calculator bug (critical)**: `Drogon Weather Ball → Annihilape: predicted 0%–0%, actual 59%`. The calc did not resolve Weather Ball to Fire type after the pending Mega/Drought. A 0% prediction is the single most dangerous output a calc can produce — a disciplined pilot would discard the move. We won *despite* the calc, not because of it. This must be guarded in code.
2. **Both actions on one slot**: Altaria was left entirely untouched. Against a Tailwind/Dragon Dance Altaria instead of a passive Earthquake one, that is a free setup turn. The redirect check (is the stronger attack lethal at min roll?) was never run because the calc said one of them did 0%.
3. **No speed-control insurance vs a boosting sweeper**: If Annihilape had come down at +1 with a partner that redirected or Protected, we had no Icy Wind, no Thunder Wave, and Whimsicott was benched. Tailwind does not answer an already-faster boosted Fighting type.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time

- **Treat 0-0% calc lines on weather/terrain-variable moves as unreliable.** Weather Ball, Terrain Pulse, Revelation Dance, Nature Power, Judgment: resolve type manually from the field we are about to create (Drogon's Mega sets Sun → Fire).
- **Run the overkill-redirect check before double-targeting.** If attack A's *minimum* roll kills, attack B goes at the partner.
- **Lock Kingambit + Drogon as the psyterrain lead.** 5-4 vs Charizard|zw's 10-12 and Dragonite|zw's 2-7 — it is the only above-even psyterrain plan.
- **Flag any foe that uses a boosting move as the win condition for the remainder of the game** and never let it get a second free turn.
