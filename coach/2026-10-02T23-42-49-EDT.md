# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Preview call was correct.** Against a Torkoal/Sinistcha/Slowbro Trick Room shell with Pyroar sun support, bringing **Whimsicott + Kingambit + Garchomp + Milotic** and sheltering both Megas was sound. We never needed a Mega and won anyway — pure action-economy value.
- **Turn 1 removed their disruptor and pre-empted speed control.** Moonblast OHKO'd Pangoro (Fake Out / Parting Shot body), and Iron Head took their Whimsicott to 1 HP through Focus Sash. That is the correct "deny the setter" priority from the Speed Control hierarchy.
- **Turn 2 defensive safe play.** Whimsicott at 36% was shown a 51-101% Heat Wave roll, so we switched it into Milotic (Fire resist, Leftovers, Competitive). Textbook Archetype-2 defensive reposition.
- **Turn 3 spread usage was legal and optimal.** Kingambit was already fainted, so Earthquake next to Milotic broke no team rule, hit both live foes, closed the 1 HP Whimsicott, and set up Scald to finish Pyroar at 8%. Two KOs in one turn.
- **Ahead-in-count discipline.** At 3v2 with Scarf Garchomp live we took the 100% accurate line rather than gambling on Rock Slide flinches.
