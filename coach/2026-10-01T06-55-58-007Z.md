# Coach Review — 11 games, 6-5

## What's winning
Rain/sun/snow matchups (4-0): Charizard-Y weather override + Whimsicott Tailwind is a clean, repeatable plan. Sneasler is our best performer (7 KOs / 3 faints) and Milotic quietly cleans (6 KOs / 2 faints). **First KO correlates hard with wins: 5-0 when we get it, 1-3 when they do.**

## What's losing
**1. Whimsicott is a liability lead into Psychic Terrain / special spread.** 6 faints (most on team), 5 KOs. Against Indeedee+Armarouge, Expanding Force 99%'d it on T1 *and* Psychic Terrain blanked Prankster Tailwind — the pilot's own preview note said "Psychic Terrain blocks Prankster priority anyway" and still led Whimsicott. Same story vs Glimmora (Sludge Bomb 65%) and Kingambit (Iron Head 99%).

**2. Sand/standard = 1-5.** Losses share a shape: our Mega eats a big special hit T1 (Glimmora Power Gem 76% on Dragonite, Armarouge EF 70%), Multiscale is gone by T2, and we're down a Mega with no board.

**3. Scarf Garchomp is a trap in these games.** 1 KO / 3 faints across 11 games. Locked Stomping Tantrum into a Rillaboom switch-in = Wood Hammer 88% and a free KO for them.

**4. Calcs are shaky:** 10/22 in range. Two "likely OHKO" calls (Espathra 115-136% → 77%) drove overrides that didn't pay off.

## Concrete changes

- **Preview:** vs any team with Indeedee / Armarouge / Psychic Surge, **do not bring Whimsicott**. Default to Drogon + Sneasler + Milotic + Garchomp; Tailwind is dead weight under Terrain.
- **Preview:** vs Glimmora or Tyranitar on preview, Dragonite leads but **does not Mega turn 1** — hold the Mega until Power Gem / Rock Slide user is KO'd or the turn is safe. Multiscale is worth more than one Draco Meteor.
- **Pilot:** when leading Whimsicott into a known special spread attacker (Armarouge, Glimmora, Abomasnow), Tailwind is not mandatory turn 1 — Whimsicott dying T1-T2 has cost us the Tailwind anyway in 3 of 5 losses.
- **Pilot:** demote Scarf Garchomp to a late-game revenge-killer only; never click a Scarf move turn 1-3 unless the KO is confirmed on a *revealed* set.
- **Pilot:** require ≥90% low-roll to call a move a "KO" in reasoning; treat 100-120% predictions as non-lethal given our 45% calc accuracy.
