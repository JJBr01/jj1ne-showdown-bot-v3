## 3. What Failed
**Turn 2 was the single losing-shaped turn** (we won anyway, but only because Scarf Garchomp's Earthquake bailed us out).

Our own displayed calc read:
```
Drogon Heat Wave -> Sinistcha 119-153% OHKO   (guaranteed)
Kingambit Kowtow Cleave -> Sinistcha 92-122%
```
Heat Wave's **minimum roll already killed Sinistcha**. Kingambit's entire action was therefore pre-deleted — a 50% surrender of the turn's action economy, exactly the Lecture-1 failure mode. Meanwhile the real threat on the board was a **+1 SpA / +1 SpD / +1 Spe Volcarona at 59%**, which then moved first and Fiery Danced Kingambit from 162/207 to 0.

Secondary issues:
- Turn 1 we accepted a free Quiver Dance. Spreading into both slots was reasonable, but the board state after turn 1 ("a live booster exists") never changed our targeting priority.
- We under-weighted Fiery Dance's damage onto a Dark/**Steel** Kingambit. Fire is super-effective on Steel; +1 SpA turned a 2HKO into a clean OHKO. The threat line even showed Volcarona Struggle Bug (17-27%) and we implicitly treated that as the ceiling.
