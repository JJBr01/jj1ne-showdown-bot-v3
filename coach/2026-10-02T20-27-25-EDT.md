# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 was excellent.** Focus-firing Indeedee (Moonblast to break Sash + Iron Head) removed Psychic Surge and the Expanding Force win condition on the first turn. Leading Kingambit into previewed Psychic Terrain was the textbook "Must-Come" pick.
- **Whimsicott out-performed its slot**: 123% damage, the Kleavor KO, and it forced their Whimsicott into Tailwind-refresh mode.
- **Milotic's Ice Beam target choice at the end** (their Whimsicott, the speed-control engine) was correct even in a lost position.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied:
- Win-condition denial on the terrain setter (Indeedee) turn 1.
- Kingambit led into Psychic Terrain (immune to Expanding Force, Prankster-proof).

Violated:
- **"Protect belongs on the Pokémon with the largest listed incoming OHKO."** T2 Kingambit was listed at 104–153% incoming Close Combat and still attacked. T3 Drogon mega-evolved into a live Kleavor.
- **"Set Tailwind on turn 1–2 while Whimsicott is at full HP."** Tailwind went up on turn 4 with two Pokémon left and Whimsicott fainted the same turn.
- **"Never let the opponent have both speed and power."** Their Whimsicott had Tailwind from turn 1 to the end; we never contested it.

## 3. What Failed
**T2 — the losing turn.** Kleavor Close Combat was listed as a *guaranteed* OHKO on Kingambit. Our line (Iron Head 53% OHKO + Moonblast) was a coin flip, and they moved first under Tailwind. We lost our best piece and left Kleavor at 16%. Correct: Whimsicott Moonblast into Kleavor (breaking it to ~36%) while Kingambit switches out to Milotic or Drogon — or Kingambit Sucker Punch (Psychic Terrain was gone after Indeedee fainted, and Kleavor was committed to attacking).

**T3 — the fatal technical error.** Drogon mega-evolved and Heat Waved with Kleavor (a Rock-type, Stone Axe revealed) alive and faster under Tailwind. The calc printed 39–63%; reality was 100% because Charizard-Y is **4x weak to Rock**. Drogon dealt zero damage all game. Protect was free and obvious.

**Macro failure.** We spent three turns killing Indeedee and Kleavor while their Whimsicott — the thing providing speed to every piece on their team — sat untouched, re-set Tailwind on turn 5, and KO'd our Whimsicott. Their Whimsicott was alive until turn 5 of a 5-turn game.

**T4** Tailwind was a donated action: Whimsicott was at 58/137 with an incoming Moonblast listed at 33–64% KO 70%. It should have Protected or we should have conceded the speed race and had Milotic Recover.
