# Coach Report — 16 games, 50% WR

## The dominant pattern: Psychic Terrain + Expanding Force is killing us

Expanding Force accounts for **7 of 32 recorded KOs against us** (Alakazam-Mega x4, Armarouge x2, Indeedee x1). Indeedee appeared in **4 of the last 5 games**. Our current "anti-Indeedee" response — lead Sneasler+Dragonite and double-target the redirector — failed twice because:

1. **We KO Indeedee, but Alakazam/Armarouge is the actual win condition.** vs paserod2 we spent T1+T2 doubling Alakazam into double Protect, lost Sneasler, then never recovered — 0-4 sweep.
2. **Sneasler is a free KO under Psychic Terrain.** Fake Out is blocked, Unburden never activates, and it eats a priority-immune Expanding Force for 100%. Sneasler leading into Indeedee has died turn 1-2 in both losses.

**Testable fix:** when preview shows Indeedee/Armarouge/Alakazam, **do not lead Sneasler**. Lead Whimsicott + Drogon/Dragonite: Prankster Taunt on Indeedee (not Dark-typed, works) shuts off Follow Me *and* Trick Room without needing a KO, and Whimsicott survives Expanding Force better than Sneasler. Bring Milotic as the Expanding Force sponge.

## Second pattern: Setup not punished

- **Torterra Shell Smash** (marillat): we saw +2 and responded with Tailwind instead of attacking. It then got 3 KOs. Tailwind does not beat +2 Speed Torterra — we needed Dragonite Ice/Draco + Sneasler onto it immediately on the turn it smashed.
- **Corviknight Bulk Up** (VictoriousDancing): identified correctly, never actually removed; it KO'd Milotic and chunked Garchomp.

**Rule to add:** against a Shell Smash/Bulk Up user, speed control is *not* the answer — the answer is damage or Haze. Milotic should run/use Haze pressure as the designated reset.

## Third: Rock Slide tax on Charizard-Y

Drogon died to Tyranitar Rock Slide twice-ish (52% + sand + KO) and contributes only 3 KOs across 16 games vs Sneasler's 10. **Charizard-Y into any sand team is a liability.** Preview stats confirm: sand/Charizard 1-2, while Dragonite megas are 2-0.

## Mega selection is miscalibrated

Dragonite|zw/zf: **3-0**. Charizard|zw: **6-6**, and trickroom/Charizard is **0-2**. Default should flip to Dragonite; Charizard only vs rain/sun/standard with no Rock coverage.

## Garchomp

6 faints, 3 KOs, frequently Scarf-locked into Earthquake that does nothing (Indeedee, Corviknight, Inteleon all neutral/immune-ish). Consider non-Scarf or at least avoid leading it into Flying/Levitate cores.
