# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

- **Preview plan was exactly right.** `Drogon + Whimsicott` into a Trick Room archetype (Heracross / Pinsir / Farigiraf / Kingambit / Skeledirge): Drought removes any weather plan, Heat Wave is super-effective or neutral on five of their six, and Whimsicott's Encore is the designated Trick Room reversal. This is the confirmed highest-EV opening for this team and it produced a 2-turn win.
- **Focus-fire on the single win condition.** Mega Heracross (Megahorn 77–145% on Whimsicott, Double-Edge 48–80% on Drogon) was their only true carry. Both of our actions pointed at it on T1 and the KO landed on T2. Correct macro.
- **Encore on a Protect-cycler was a game-ending play.** Heracross clicked Protect on T1; Whimsicott Encored it on T2, the forced Protect **failed**, and Heat Wave hit for 100%. This is the "Encore on a defensive move is a full win condition" durable lesson executed perfectly, generalized from Iron Defense to Protect.
- **No Tailwind into Trick Room.** Farigiraf set TR at the end of T1 and Whimsicott correctly picked Encore over Tailwind on T2. The hard guardrail held.
- **Heat Wave burn on Farigiraf** was free extra value: it halved a physical TR setter's output and added residual that would have mattered if the game had gone longer.
