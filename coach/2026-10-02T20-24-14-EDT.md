## 3. What Failed
- **In-game: nothing.** A 1-turn 2-0 is as clean as it gets.
- **Process failure — rate limits**: The team-preview call fell back to the engine (HTTP 429). This is the 3rd preview/turn fallback in the last 5 games. Preview is the single highest-leverage decision in VGC (Wolfe's elimination technique); losing it to an API rate limit is an unforced structural error.
- **Calc miscalibration risk**: Being wrong by 2x in our favour is lucky here, but the same miscalibration will one day cause us to *decline* a winning spread line because the preview shows "no KO." The fix belongs in code, not in hope.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. **Codify the sun spread bonus**: when Drought is active (or Charizard-Y is mega-evolving this turn), Heat Wave / Weather Ball get a hard score boost and Protect gets a penalty when both foes are listed ≥45% max roll.
2. **Retry preview calls on 429** with backoff before falling back.
3. **Vs Electric Terrain Raichu leads**: this exact line (Whimsicott Tailwind + Drogon mega Heat Wave) is now a proven turn-1 double KO — repeat it.
4. **Keep the sun plan**: `sun / Charizard|zw` is now 18-3. Do not experiment with Dragonite leads in sun.

The opponent led two Pokémon both hit neutrally-or-worse by Fire spread (Mega Raichu-X, Metagross), with no Wide Guard and no Protect, and lost on turn 1. The takeaway is defensive, not offensive: **a team with no answer to a spread engine simply dies**. Our spread engine is Drogon, so the priority is protecting it, not adding more single-target damage.
