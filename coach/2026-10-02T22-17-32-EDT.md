## 3. What Failed

**Turn 2 is the entire loss.** Board: Aerodactyl (100%, confirmed Rock Slide aimed at Drogon last turn, faster) + their Whimsicott. Our Drogon at 153/153 under a displayed *268-367% OHKO*. Our Whimsicott at 89/137 behind a Focus Sash.

We chose: **Whimsicott Protect + Drogon Heat Wave (29-55% on Aerodactyl)**.

That is precisely backwards. The slot under a 3x-overkill OHKO attacked; the slot that was safe (Sash, and not even the Rock Slide target) Protected. Correct lines, in order:
1. Drogon Protect + Whimsicott Moonblast (breaks Sash, chips Aerodactyl) — Drogon survives and Aerodactyl is in Iron Head range next turn anyway.
2. Drogon switches to Milotic (Rock-neutral, Scald) + Whimsicott Moonblast.

We traded a 153-HP Mega Charizard-Y for ~40% of damage. From that point we were playing 3v4 with no Mega, no priority, and no answer to their Mega Charizard.
