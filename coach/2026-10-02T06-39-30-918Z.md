## Analysis

**Record in these 10: 6-4.** The wins came from the same template: Mega Dragonite Draco Meteor removing their fastest/most threatening piece on turn 1, then Kingambit/Whimsicott cleaning. That plan is now 10-3 in "standard" and should stay the default.

**The two psyterrain losses are the same game twice.** Both times we led Kingambit + Drogon into Sneasler + a Psychic setter, reasoning "Kingambit is Psychic-immune." But Sneasler isn't a Psychic attacker — it's a Fighting attacker, and Kingambit is 4x-pressured by Close Combat. Kingambit died turn 2 in both games while contributing 0 damage in one of them. Simultaneously, Sneasler's Rock Slide took Drogon to 5% on turn 1 (F37 Iosif), making our Mega a non-factor. We paid the full Sneasler tax on *both* leads. The existing rule "Sneasler on their sheet means bench Whimsicott and Drogon" needs extending to Kingambit — the correct four is Dragonite + Whimsicott + Milotic + Garchomp, where Dragonite's Draco Meteor OHKOs Sneasler (confirmed vs GEZolt).

**The rain loss** was lost at preview-adjacent speed: we knew Blastoisinite was on the sheet (adjustment already in effect) but still allowed +2 Water Spout to hit Kingambit and Whimsicott at full HP. Shell Smash went unpunished turn 1 because we spent Draco Meteor on Inteleon instead. Once it is +2, nothing on our team survives; the KO window is turn 1 only.

**sengeki (raichu-staraptor, now 0-3)** repeats the Staraptor pattern: three of our Pokémon died to Close Combat. Note also three consecutive Heat Wave calcs landing ~30-35% low — their Staraptor is physically defensive or we're mis-assuming sun. Treat Staraptor as needing two full attacks, not one.

**Whimsicott (64 faints)** remains our most-killed piece, but it also scored 40 KOs — it's doing work, it just dies the turn after. Switching it out once Tailwind is up (or not needed) is undervalued.
