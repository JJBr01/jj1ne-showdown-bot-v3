- No Kingambit lead into a Sneasler sheet (the ANTI-SNEASLER LEAD BAN proposal).

- Mega by turn 1.
- Damage was focus-fired, not split, on both T1 and T2 (all 4 damage-calc checks landed inside the predicted range).

Violated:
- **"Calm Mind / Coil setup must be answered with BOTH actions the turn it starts."** Swampert-Mega Bulk Up'd on T1, T2 and T5 with zero response.
