# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Heat Wave through Protect (T2)**: Drogon's spread Heat Wave ignored Mega Gallade's Protect and still deleted the 1% Whimsicott — correctly removing their Tailwind setter rather than wasting both actions on the protected slot.
- **Mega-Stone priority**: We correctly identified Gallade (Galladite) as the win condition and Whimsicott's Moonblast finished it on T4.
- **Drogon damage output**: 176% total damage in Sun, by far our best piece again.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Lead choice (preview)**: Kingambit into a team with Gallade. Gallade is a Fighting-type that 1-shots Kingambit; this is the *second consecutive game* where a Kingambit lead was deleted on turn 1 (Armarouge Armor Cannon last game). That is an instant 2-action → 1-action deficit before the game starts.
2. **Rate-limit fallbacks (T1, T3, T4)**: Three of six turns were engine-piloted.
   - T3 double Protect: neither slot was actually in KO range; it donated a Mamoswine switch-in, a Tailwind turn, and a Gallade Mega turn.
   - T4: Drogon attacked into a Psycho Cut listed as a 92% OHKO instead of Protecting — the exact "our Mega can't KO the threat → Protect" rule.
3. **Speed and Power simultaneously conceded**: From T2 the opponent had Tailwind + Mega Gallade. Our Golden Rule says never allow this. We had no Tailwind, no Icy Wind, and no priority answer.
4. **Whimsicott's Sash burned by Flamethrower, then Ice Shard** — our only speed control died having set 0 useful Tailwind turns.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
- **Preview hard rule**: if their team shows any Fighting attacker (Gallade, Urshifu, Lucario, Conkeldurr) or a Superpower/Close Combat user (Mamoswine, Great Tusk), Kingambit goes in the BACK, never leads. Lead Drogon + Whimsicott (Tailwind) or Milotic + Drogon.
- **Tailwind discipline**: Whimsicott's first healthy turn = Tailwind. Not turn 5, not from 1 HP.
- **Double-Protect ban** unless both slots face ≥60% listed damage or we're stalling the last turn of their field effect.
- **When they get Tailwind and we don't**: our Mega stops trading. Protect/pivot and burn their 4 turns, then win the natural-speed endgame.

## 5. Team Evolution & Opponent Inspiration (Mega Dragonite + Mega Charizard Core)
funnyblueberrycat's structure was simple and brutal: a support Pokémon that *buffs the attacker* (Beat Up → +4 Gallade), then a Mega Gallade that one-shots everything, with Tailwind backing it. The lesson is that their support slot was never passive — it converted directly into power.

- **Kingambit role change**: strictly a late-game Dark closer, never a lead into Fighting/
