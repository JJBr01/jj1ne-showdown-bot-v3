## Analysis

**Record 18-35 overall; 2-5 in the last ten.** The wins are fast (2-6 turns) and come from Drogon getting a clean turn-1 KO (Ancient Power on Volcarona/Talonflame/Zard-X — renew that adjustment, it's producing immediate KOs).

**Loss pattern 1 — Whimsicott evaporates.** Whimsicott has 17 faints, the most on the team. In both raichu-staraptor losses Arcanine-Hisui's Flare Blitz took it to 1 HP on turn 1 and killed it on turn 3; against samtheworld we then spent Whimsicott's *second* turn on Protect, wasting the sash resource entirely. Against these Fire/priority leads the Sash buys nothing. Milotic walls Arcanine, Rillaboom's Glide and Sylveon, and in both games Milotic was our best performer (120% damage, 2 KOs vs samtheworld).

**Loss pattern 2 — Trick Room still 1-6.** Vs aperturebl we correctly focused Farigiraf but it had a Sash and moved first anyway; TR went up, we then burned turn 2 finishing a 1% Farigiraf while Mega Blastoise (Water Spout + Ice Beam) got free turns and killed four of ours. The right turn-2 play was double-target Blastoise. Vs yohn7 the same shape: setter died turn 1, but Mega Mawile under its own board killed Dragonite and Kingambit. The lesson is that killing the setter is only half the plan — the *abuser* is the win condition.

**Loss pattern 3 — Choice-locked Garchomp.** Three of five recent losses included Garchomp dying while locked and contributing little (7 KOs in 35 games, 13 faints — our worst ratio). Treat the lock as a liability and switch out the moment a check appears.

**What's working:** Kingambit is our best piece (4 faints, 5 KOs, 174% in one game) and should be brought more against Psychic/TR. Drogon with Ancient Power turn 1 is a reliable opener. First-KO correlation remains decisive: 17-5 when we KO first, 1-10 when they do.
