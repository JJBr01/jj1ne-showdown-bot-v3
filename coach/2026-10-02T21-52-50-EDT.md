# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 focus fire on Toxtricity.** Whimsicott Moonblast + Mega Charizard Weather Ball removed the foe with a 74% OHKO chance on Drogon and a guaranteed OHKO on Whimsicott, even through Light Screen. Correct application of "deny the opponent speed AND power."
- **Mega-evolving Drogon on turn 1.** Sun went up immediately, which cut Blaziken-Mega's Flare Blitz damage for the rest of the game and gave us a 100% accurate STAB Weather Ball.
- **Kingambit Iron Head on Grimmsnarl (T3).** Displayed as a guaranteed OHKO; removing the Prankster support stopped further screens/Swagger and prevented their "support + Mega" engine from assembling.
- **T5 target selection.** Choosing to finish the 43% Garchomp instead of chasing full-HP Mega Blaziken was macro-correct: it cleared the board so Scarf Garchomp (231 Spe) could revenge-kill the +2 Speed Blaziken on T6.
- **T6 discipline.** Stomping Tantrum instead of Earthquake next to Kingambit (team rule respected), and Kingambit Protect against a displayed 180–257% Close Combat (threat-line rule respected).

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied correctly:
- Never Earthquake beside Kingambit → Stomping Tantrum T6.
- "If the threat line shows ≥100% minimum damage, Protect or switch" → Kingambit Protect T6.
- "Bring the Mega in rather than hoarding it" → Drogon mega T1.
- "Punish/remove the setter immediately" → Grimmsnarl OHKO'd the turn it was reachable.

Violated:
- **"When a foe sits low and has Protected recently, redirect at least one action."** On T4 we sent *both* attacks into a Garchomp that had Protected on T2. It Protected again. That is a full 2-action turn surrendered — the exact failure mode the active proposal "POST-KO PROTECT READ" was written to prevent, generalised to repeat-Protect reads.

## 3. What Failed
- **Turn 4 — zero-action turn.** Both Moonblast and Kowtow Cleave hit Garchomp's Protect. Meanwhile Blaziken Mega-evolved, wasted Upper Hand, and *banked a free Speed Boost*. We handed the opponent a tempo turn for nothing. The correct play was Moonblast → Blaziken (chip the fresh Mega / break any Sash) + Kowtow Cleave → Garchomp, or Kingambit Protect + Whimsicott chip.
- **Turns 1–2: Grimmsnarl got Light Screen *and* Misty Terrain for free.** Our T1 both-into-Toxtricity was defensible (it threatened an OHKO), but T2 was an engine fallback (HTTP 429) that produced Tailwind + a switch while Grimmsnarl set terrain unopposed.
- **Whimsicott attrition.** Focus Sash was broken by incidental Overdrive spread damage on turn 1 and was therefore irrelevant when Earthquake and Close Combat arrived. Whimsicott is our most-fainted Pokémon at 153 faints across 240 games, and in this game its Tailwind turns produced almost no value (Garchomp was Scarfed; Kingambit is slow regardless).
- **API rate-limit fallback on T2** cost us a planned turn; not a strategic error, but it means T2's switch wasn't deliberate.
