## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Applied: priority-blocker awareness, Earthquake ally safety, win-condition identification in preview.

Violated:
- *"Our Mega/key piece must Protect whenever the displayed max roll against it is >=100% from a faster foe"* — the log literally printed `Kingambit threatened by Sneasler Close Combat (153-222% OHKO)` and we attacked anyway.
- *"Never split/commit both actions onto one target when another foe kills one of our slots first."*
- *"A Pokémon about to be KO'd that can't win the trade should Protect or switch."*
