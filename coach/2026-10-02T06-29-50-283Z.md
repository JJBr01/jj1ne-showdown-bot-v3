## Analysis

**Record in these 10: 5-5.** Wins came from the familiar engine: KO the enabler turn 1 (Raichu-Y, Talonflame, Indeedee, Volcarona/Garchomp-Z) with Mega Draco or Heat Wave + Kowtow, then grind with Milotic/Kingambit. That pattern is reliable — keep it.

**Loss 1 (2xdheeraj, rain).** The only real error was structural, not tactical: Blastoise Shell Smashed on T1 while our Draco Meteor missed Inteleon. From then, +2 Water Spout one-shot every Pokémon we had. On T2 we split — Draco at Blastoise and switched Whimsicott — instead of committing both actives to remove the +2 sweeper. The setup-punish rule exists; it was not applied to a *rain* sweeper. Also Dragonite leading into a revealed Inteleon Ice Beam is a bad trade on paper.

**Loss 2 (sengeki, raichu-staraptor).** 0-3 in this archetype now. Mega Staraptor with Contrary/Reckless Close Combat + Roost + Tailwind + screens is a hard matchup for a team whose answers (Milotic, Kingambit, Garchomp) are all Fighting-weak or slower. We had Staraptor at 8% on T3 and never finished it — it Roosted twice while we chipped Raichu and Protected with a burned Garchomp. Lesson: a Roost user below 50% is the only target that matters.

**Loss 3 (Pem779567, psyterrain).** Kingambit died turn 1 to Raichu-Y Focus Blast before doing anything — exactly the pattern the current adjustment forbids. The rule needs renewing, and preview should stop pairing Kingambit lead with revealed Focus Blast users.

**Loss 4 (feraligatr).** Mega Feraligatr Dragon Dance went unpunished through a Protect-and-chip sequence; Drogon died T3 and Gholdengo cleaned. Same setup-punish failure.

Three of five losses share one root cause: **a boosted sweeper got one free turn and was never focus-fired**. That's the single highest-value fix.
