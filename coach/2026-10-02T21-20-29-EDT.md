# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Target identification on T1** was correct in principle: Mega Raichu-Y Zap Cannon is a known Drogon killer (10 recorded KOs on us), so Weather Ball into Raichu was a reasonable allocation of Drogon's action.
- **Kingambit into +2 Blastoise (T2)** was the right slot — we correctly identified the boosted sweeper as the win condition rather than chipping Pelipper.
- **Milotic carried the entire game**: 2 KOs (Raichu, Abomasnow), survived 6 turns of +2 Mega Blastoise through Protect/Recover cycling, and was the only Pokémon that dealt damage. Milotic continues to be our most reliable anti-weather/anti-water piece.
- **Drought overwrote Snowscape instantly on T1**, denying Abomasnow's Aurora Veil plan before it started — exactly as the previous coach note predicted.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ **Mega Protect enforcement** was invoked on T2 (Water Spout 103% max) and attempted on T3 (Surf 259% max).
- ✅ **Anti-setup targeting**: we aimed at Blastoise, not the partner, once it was boosted.
- ❌ **"Punish setup NOW"** was violated on the exact turn it mattered. Turn 1, Kingambit — facing only a Fake Out — clicked Protect while Blastoise Shell Smashed for free.
- ❌ **"Never Protect twice in a row"** was violated by our own pilot on T3. This is a rule written verbatim in the system prompt and it still happened.

## 3. What Failed

**Turn 1 — The game-losing turn.**
Kingambit's listed threat was `Raichu Focus Blast (71-108%)`, but Raichu actually led with Fake Out — and more importantly, Blastoise was the Shell Smash user. We spent one of two actions on Protect and got nothing: Fake Out hit Drogon instead, Drogon flinched (Weather Ball never fired), and Blastoise reached **+2 Atk / +2 SpA / +2 Spe unpunished**. Available line: **Kingambit Kowtow Cleave → Blastoise (68-107%, 19% OHKO)** plus Drogon Weather Ball. Even without the OHKO, Blastoise enters T2 at ~25% and cannot Mega-sweep. Both of our actions were spent on a Fake Out that costs 16% HP.
