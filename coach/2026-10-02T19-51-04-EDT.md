# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**The Kingambit + Drogon lead vs an Armor Tail / Trick Room shell was the right call and the game was effectively over by turn 3.**

- **Turn 1 Protect on Drogon** — Glimmora was sitting there with an unused Mega Stone and Power Gem (4x on Charizard-Y, calc showed 129–246%, a guaranteed OHKO). Protecting the Mega while Kingambit swung was the textbook "shelter the win condition" line. Bonus: it also dodged Mortal Spin's poison.
- **Iron Head over Sucker Punch, all four turns** — Farigiraf's Armor Tail was on the field from turn 1. The hard guardrail held; we never threw away an action on a blocked priority move.
- **Turn 2 Weather Ball → Farigiraf (OHKO)** — This is the single best move of the game. It removed *both* the Trick Room setter **and** the Armor Tail priority blocker in one action, re-enabling Sucker Punch and denying the entire opposing game plan. Kill the engine, not the attacker.
- **Turn 3 focus-fire through Follow Me** — Clefable redirected, so Weather Ball chipped it to 4% and Kingambit's Iron Head finished it. We punished the redirector rather than flailing at the protected slot.
- Result: 2–0 up with zero losses, opponent forfeited.
