# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Whimsicott at 1 HP was the MVP.** Focus Sash absorbed Flash Cannon, the T2 Protect preserved it, and it landed the finishing Moonblast on Archaludon *and* the finishing Moonblast on Floette-Mega. Two KOs from a 1 HP Pokémon.
- **T4 switch Whimsicott → Milotic** was correct macro: it removed Whimsicott from Floette's guaranteed-kill range and brought in the Incineroar wall.
- **Milotic's Scald KO on Incineroar (T5)** and the near-1v2 comeback show the back half of the team was fine. We lost this game in the first two turns only.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- **Violated — the single most-repeated rule in the playbook.** The T1 calc explicitly printed *"Drogon threatened by Archaludon Electro Shot (108-201% OHKO)"* and there was also an unflinched Fake Out user (Incineroar) on the field. Both the "Protect Drogon on the Mega turn vs a guaranteed OHKO" rule **and** the "FAKE-OUT MEGA TURN" active proposal demanded Protect. We attacked.
- **Violated — the anti-rain burst rule.** "Never chip Archaludon over several turns (Stamina + Electro Shot snowball)." We chipped it from two slots with a 30-49% Moonblast and a 7-12% resisted Weather Ball; it gained +1 Def from Stamina, healed, then Electro Shot +1 SpA'd and killed our Mega.
- **Applied correctly** — Protect on the 1 HP Sash mon, switching the doomed Whimsicott out of Light of Ruin range, and KOing Archaludon the moment it was in range.
- **T3 was a `wolfe-fallback`** turn with no guardrails — the known fallback gap.

**Drogon's damage line for the whole game: 0%.** We nonetheless took 3 of their 4 Pokémon. This loss is 100% attributable to turns 1–2.

## 4. How To Improve Next Time
1. **Mega-turn OHKO lock (now hard-coded):** any displayed max roll ≥100% against our Mega on its Mega turn → Protect or switch only.
2. **Type-effectiveness floor for focus fire:** never count a resisted/sub-20% move as the second half of a KO. Fire into Steel is not damage.
3. **Persistent-threat switch rule:** if Protect was already spent and the OHKO threat is still live next turn, the Mega leaves the field.
4. **Archaludon protocol:** Garchomp (Ground) + Whimsicott (Moonblast) only. Drogon never attacks it.
