# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked

**Team preview was the single best decision of the game.** The sheet showed Tyranitar **and** Excadrill (sand) **and** Indeedee-F (Psychic Terrain). Charizard-Y into that board is a 4x Rock weakness plus a Sandstorm residual tax — leaving it home was mandatory, and we did it. Kingambit (Psychic-immune, Defiant) + Whimsicott (Tailwind) lead with Scarf Garchomp and Milotic in back is exactly the "Must-Come / Never-Come" elimination technique.

**Turn 1 was textbook win-con denial.** Kommo-o with Clangorous Soul is the piece that, left alone, becomes speed AND power in one move. Kingambit had a listed 100-153% Aura Sphere pointed at it, so Protect was its *only* legal action — and Whimsicott's Moonblast was a 98% KO. We removed their sweeper on turn 1 for zero resources. This is the exact pattern the playbook demands: *don't Protect-stall while a booster boosts; delete it before it starts.*

**Garchomp's Scarf was the engine of the win.** Stomping Tantrum (not Earthquake — Milotic was beside it) moved before Tyranitar twice, took it from 100 → 41 → 0, and then finished the 11% Excadrill. 111% damage, 2 KOs. The sand matchup record is 2-10 overall; this configuration is the one that works.

**Milotic in sand is quietly excellent.** Leftovers out-healed sandstorm chip, Ice Beam 2HKO'd Mega Pidgeot through confusion, and it never dropped below 60%.
