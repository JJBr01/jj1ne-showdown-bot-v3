## Analysis

**Record this block: 5-5.** The wins (sun/Torkoal, Alakazam psyterrain, zazzeq standard) came from clean focus-fire and early removal of the terrain/boost piece. The losses share three repeatable errors.

**1. Setup went unpunished again (LankyHanky).** Turn 2 Kingambit used Swords Dance unopposed while both our moves went into a 49% Cofagrigus. Turn 3 we then double-Protected "to burn a Trick Room turn" — a +2 Kingambit is a win condition, not a clock to stall. It proceeded to KO Whimsicott, Drogon and Dragonite. The rule exists in the playbook; the pilot cited Trick Room stalling as an override. Protect-stalling is only correct when nothing is snowballing.

**2. Whimsicott as a 1-17% attacker.** Two losses featured Whimsicott either switched back in at 1% or kept out at 17% to "chip." It is our only speed control and Moonblast matters; once Tailwind is up and it is under 20%, it should leave the field, not eat a Sucker Punch/Icicle Crash. 99 faints across 161 games is by far our worst number.

**3. Ignoring the scaling threat.** Basculegion's Last Respects grew with each of *their* faints — by the time two had fallen it was an 85%+ spread-less nuke, and we never aimed a single attack at it. Same pattern as Rage Fist/Archaludon: identify the mon that gets stronger and kill it first.

**Mega Mawile** is now a confirmed problem (4 KOs). Play Rough one-shots Dragonite and Milotic; only Drogon's Heat Wave answers it, and we lost the Drogon tempo by keeping Milotic in front.

**Archetype trends:** psyterrain 10-28 and sand 2-10 remain the sinkholes; Drogon dying turn 1 to Sneasler Rock Slide repeated exactly as predicted, so the "leave Drogon home" preview rule is renewed. Sun (13-15 wins) and standard/Dragonite (15-6) are our best plans — keep leading them.
