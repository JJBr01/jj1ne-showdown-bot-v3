# Coach Report — 15 games, 7-8

## The one pattern that is costing us games: Psychic Terrain + Indeedee

Four of our losses trace to the same shell: **Indeedee-F + a Psychic abuser (Alakazam-Mega, Armarouge, Indeedee itself)**. Alakazam-Mega Expanding Force alone has 4 kills — a full 4-0 sweep in the paserod2 game. Against that core we keep doing the same two things wrong:

1. **We double into the Alakazam/Armarouge behind Follow Me.** T1 and T2 vs paserod2: double-targeted Alakazam, both turns it Protected/was redirected, zero damage, then it Mega'd and swept. The correct line is **kill Indeedee first** (it's the frailer mon, it's the terrain/redirect engine, and once it's gone Expanding Force drops from 120 BP spread to 80 BP single-target). We *did* do this vs marillat T1 and it won us the first two KOs.
2. **We lead Sneasler into Psychic Terrain.** Sneasler died turn 1-2 to Expanding Force in both the VictoriousDancing and paserod2 losses. Sneasler is our best Pokémon by a mile (9 KOs, team-high) and we keep throwing it away into a move it's 2x weak to with Fake Out already disabled by terrain. The "anti-Indeedee rule: lead Sneasler+Dragonite" note in the preview plan is **actively wrong** and needs deleting.

## Secondary issues

- **Charizard-Y into sand/rock is still losing.** `standard/Charizard|zw` 0-2, `trickroom/Charizard|zw` 0-2. Drogon has 3 KOs across 15 games and 4 faints — worst KO:faint ratio on the team. Mega Dragonite lines are 2-0.
- **Scarf Garchomp Earthquake is a trap vs Psychic Terrain teams** — Indeedee is often Levitate-less but Flying/Psychic Seed holders and our T3 EQ did literally 0% (Follow Me redirect). Chomp has 3 KOs / 6 faints.
- **Tailwind is not the answer vs Trick Room.** 0-2 on `trickroom/Charizard|zw` where we set Tailwind anyway. Against TR we want to stall the 4 turns or pressure the setter.

## Concrete, testable changes

1. Vs any Indeedee team: **do not bring Sneasler** unless Indeedee is already predicted to be benched; bring Milotic + Whimsicott + Dragonite + Garchomp.
2. Vs Indeedee: **T1 both attacks into Indeedee**, never into the Psychic abuser. Draco Meteor + Moonblast/Scald guarantees it.
3. **Default Mega = Dragonite** unless opponent has zero Rock/Electric/Ice and no Psychic terrain. Charizard only vs rain/sun/grass-heavy.
4. Vs confirmed Trick Room: Whimsicott **Tailwind is banned**; use Encore on the TR setter or Protect-stall the 4 turns.
