# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Whimsicott Protect** blanked both a Blizzard and a Dire Claw while Dragonite set Tailwind — a clean two-for-zero on the action economy.
- **Turn 3 Kowtow Cleave OHKO on Mega Froslass** removed the Aurora Veil + Blizzard engine, which was the correct target priority (their speed/power enabler).
- **OHKO-band Protect discipline** was honored on T4 (Kingambit vs +2 Close Combat), T6 (Dragonite at 29% vs Dire Claw) and T7. Those were correct, forced plays.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- The **hard OHKO-band block** was obeyed three times — good.
- The **snow override was violated**. Our own durable lesson says: "Alolan Ninetales / Froslass / Abomasnow on the opposing sheet makes Mega Charizard-Y mandatory — Drought removes Snowscape, Blizzard's perfect accuracy and Aurora Veil tempo in one move; never spend the Mega slot on Dragonite there." We brought Mega Dragonite into a Froslass sheet and it ate a 100%-accurate Blizzard for 71% on turn 1, never attacked usefully, and died at 29%.
- The **"after repeated Protect, target the other slot"** rule was violated twice.
- The **"punish setup immediately"** rule was violated on T4 (Swords Dance went unanswered).

## 3. What Failed
1. **Mega selection (the root cause).** Snowscape + Aurora Veil gave them speed-insurance *and* power while Blizzard never missed. Drought would have cancelled all three. Preview plan "snow / Dragonite|zw" is now 1-0 by luck but the mechanical argument is decisive.
2. **Four wasted actions into Sneasler Protect.** T3: Garchomp Stomping Tantrum → Protect. T5: Garchomp Stomping Tantrum *and* Kingambit Iron Head → Protect, on the exact turn Garchomp died to Wood Hammer. A foe that Protected last turn, is the declared KO target, and has a partner doing the damage will Protect again — that is a textbook "obvious shield" we were told never to re-click into.
3. **Unpunished +2 Swords Dance.** On T4 we Protected with Kingambit and flinched with Garchomp. Sneasler got free setup and then closed the game with Dire Claw/Close Combat.
4. **Slot-blind Protect.** Kingambit Protected on T4 while Garchomp stood in a 58-101% Wood Hammer with no escape plan. Protect on the safe slot while the other slot dies is a half-wasted turn.
5. **Zero damage after T3.** From T4 onward we produced 1 KO's worth of chip against a Rillaboom healing 6%/turn in Grassy Terrain.
