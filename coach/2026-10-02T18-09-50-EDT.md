# Coach Review

## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Tailwind** went up with Prankster before anything could stop it, and Drogon Mega'd to overwrite rain with sun. Correct opening framework.
- **Turn 2 focus fire on Mega Gengar** was the single best decision of the game: Moonblast chip + Weather Ball removed the Shadow Tag / Perish Song trapper before it could trap a Pokémon. This directly followed the "KO the trapper fast, don't split" rule.
- **Turn 4 Dragon Claw on Pelipper** correctly killed the rain/support engine with a single-target move rather than overkilling with a spread.
- Damage calcs were accurate — 4/4 inside the predicted range.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
**Applied:**
- Tailwind by turn 1 (we've had five games with 0 Tailwind turns; this one had 3).
- Shadow Tag trapper removed immediately via focus fire.
- Support engine (Pelipper) targeted with a single-target attack once revealed.
- Charizard-Y brought to flip rain to sun.

**Violated:**
- **"If the threat calc shows a guaranteed OHKO on our slot from a faster foe, that slot Protects or switches — it never attacks."** Turn 3, Drogon was explicitly flagged *threatened by Pelipper Weather Ball (90-158% KO)* and we clicked Ancient Power. Archaludon's Electro Shot finished it. This is the exact rule that has now cost us multiple games.
- **"KO the rain setter first, then burst Archaludon in one turn — never chip it over several turns."** We did the opposite: chipped Archaludon on T4 for 51% while it gained Stamina +1 Def, and it proceeded to 1-shot Kingambit and Garchomp.

## 3. What Failed
**T1 — Fake Out blindness.** A fresh Infernape lead is a near-certain Fake Out. We put our Mega's entire turn into Weather Ball and got flinched. Meanwhile Gengar Sludge Bombed Whimsicott to 1 HP, meaning our Tailwind setter was dead weight for the rest of the game. Drogon should have Protected or we should have accepted the Mega loses tempo and played around it.

**T3 — The game-losing turn.** Board: Whimsicott at 1 HP, Drogon at 133/153, foes = Archaludon (fresh switch-in) + Pelipper at 100%. The threat table listed OHKOs on *both* our slots. We attacked with both. Archaludon charged Electro Shot (instant in rain, +1 SpA) and deleted our Mega; Pelipper's Hurricane finished Whimsicott. We lost our win condition and our speed control in one turn while killing nothing.

The correct line: **Drogon Protect, Whimsicott Moonblast Pelipper.** Drogon survives, Archaludon's Electro Shot is absorbed by Protect, and we keep a Mega in sun vs a 67% Pelipper.
