# Coach Report — 17 games, 8 wins (47%)

## The one pattern that decides games: Psychic Terrain Expanding Force

Eight of our last ~20 faints came from Expanding Force (Alakazam-Mega 4, Reuniclus 4, Armarouge 2, Indeedee 1). Every Trick Room loss and both recent blowouts are the same script: Indeedee/Psychic Surge lands, we kill the *wrong* psychic first, and a Mega Alakazam or Calm Mind Reuniclus 1-shots our whole side. Record vs trickroom: **1-3**, and `trickroom/Charizard` is **0-3**.

Key errors, concretely:
- **vs paserod2:** we doubled into Alakazam T1 *and* T2 while it was behind Follow Me/Protect, scoring zero KOs for two turns. The correct target is **Indeedee** — removing Psychic Surge halves Expanding Force *and* deletes Follow Me/Trick Room in one blow.
- **vs nethen119:** same mistake. We killed Armarouge T1, then let Reuniclus Calm Mind freely (T3 note literally says "Reuniclus is boosting" and we spread Heat Wave instead of focusing it). Never leave Indeedee alive while a Calm Mind user exists.
- **Prankster is dead in Psychic Terrain** — yet we led Whimsicott Tailwind into it twice and got 0 Tailwind turns.

## Secondary leaks
- **Tailwind turns us 3, them 0, still losing.** Speed control is not our problem; survivability vs 150+ SpA spread is. Sneasler died T1 in 3 of the last 5 games — it is our top KO scorer (10) *and* top faint (7) because it leads into Psychic Terrain where Fake Out fails.
- **Charizard-Y is 3 KOs / 5 faints.** It dies to Rock Slide (TTar, Excadrill) and Expanding Force. Mega Dragonite plans are 2-0; Charizard plans are 6-7.
- **Calc accuracy 15/30.** Heat Wave/Dire Claw ranges are mis-set; stop treating Heat Wave as a KO move on neutral targets.

## Testable changes

1. **Preview rule:** if opponent has Indeedee/Indeedee-F *or* any Psychic Surge, bring **Garchomp + Milotic + Dragonite + Sneasler**, bench Whimsicott (Prankster blanked) and Charizard. Mega Dragonite.
2. **Target rule:** vs Psychic Terrain, **Indeedee is target #1 on turn 1**, not the Mega/boost sweeper. Follow Me doesn't redirect spread — lead Heat Wave/Earthquake/Rock Slide where possible, or Dire Claw + Draco into Indeedee directly.
3. **Boost rule tightening:** if a foe uses Calm Mind and we cannot KO it this turn, switch the threatened mon out rather than spread-chipping.
4. **Never lead Whimsicott vs a team sheet containing Indeedee/Psychic Seed.**
5. **Bench Charizard vs sand** (TTar/Excadrill Rock Slide): sand record 1-3, Drogon 52–61% per Rock Slide.
