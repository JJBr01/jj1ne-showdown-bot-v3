## 1. What Worked
- Turn 1 Tailwind went up uncontested; Whimsicott survived Close Combat thanks to bulk.
- Recognizing that Drogon was in OHKO range and Protecting it on T3 was correct **once**.
- Whimsicott's Moonblast chipping Grimmsnarl was the only sustained offense we had.

That is genuinely the entire list. This was a 0-KO loss.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
Mostly **violated**:
- "Never let the opponent have BOTH speed advantage AND power" — Staraptor got Parting Shot boosts (+1 Atk/+1 SpA ×2), Charm (+2 Atk via Defiant/Competitive-style), **and** their Whimsicott's Tailwind on T5. We allowed the exact forbidden board state.
- "Don't Protect-stall while a foe boosts" — we Protected Drogon on T3, T6, T7, T8. Four Protects. Staraptor was never damaged a single time all game.
- "Switch a Pokémon out when it is about to be KO'd" — fine, but we switched *into* the KO: Milotic entered on T5 directly into +1 Close Combat and died from full.
- The preview rule "what must we bring to not lose on the spot" was ignored. Mega Staraptor is 4x weak to Rock. We brought zero Rock moves.

## 3. What Failed

**Preview (the fatal error).** Their sheet: Metagross, Staraptor, Whimsicott, Grimmsnarl, Rillaboom, Incineroar. Staraptite on Staraptor means a 178-Speed Reckless Brave Bird / Close Combat Mega. Our picks — Whimsicott, Charizard-Y, Dragonite, Milotic — contained **not one Pokémon that outspeeds or super-effectively hits Staraptor**. Charizard and Dragonite are both Flying-neutral/Fighting-weak-adjacent, Milotic eats Close Combat, Whimsicott dies to anything. Garchomp (Rock Slide, 4x) and Kingambit (Sucker Punch, revenge) both stayed home. The game was lost at team select.

**T2 target choice.** We attacked Grimmsnarl, a support mon, with both actions. Grimmsnarl responded with Parting Shot (+1/+1 to Staraptor) and pivoted to Incineroar, who Parting Shot again on T3 (+2/+2 total). We fed the sweeper two free boosts while hitting a sack.

**T3–T5 action bankruptcy.** Three consecutive turns of zero damage: switch+Protect, switch+Protect (Encored), double switch. Staraptor accumulated +4 Def/+4 SpD from Close Combat drops being irrelevant and stacked attack. Milotic walked into the +1 Close Combat and died.

**T6–T8 Protect loop.** Drogon Protected three turns in a row behind Light Screen. The Encore on T6 locked Staraptor into Close Combat — its best move anyway, so the Encore was a wasted action. We had no win condition on the board.

**Light Screen.** Grimmsnarl's screen halved Moonblast/Weather Ball/Heat Wave for the entire first half — another reason physical attackers (Garchomp, Kingambit) were mandatory.
