# Analysis

**Record: 4-6 in this block, 72-151 overall.**

## Self-inflicted losses (2 of 6)

**creeperninja99 (sun, T5):** Garchomp Earthquake KO'd our own 70%-HP Milotic. The standing rule only bans EQ next to Kingambit/Sneasler; it must be extended to *any* non-Flying ally at low HP. This turned a winnable 2v2 into a 1v2 — Garchomp then lost to Body Press at 3% Tyranitar.

Also T1 of that game: Ancient Power mirror vs their Charizard. Both got +1 all stats; theirs survived at 1% and their Dragonite Extreme Speed finished our 9% Drogon next turn. Heat Wave in our own sun would have done more and not handed them a boost.

**arayalex90 (trickroom, T4-T8):** Rotom Tricked a Choice item onto Milotic. We then Protected with it — permanently Choice-locked into Protect. Milotic spent the entire endgame unable to act and we lost 1v2 with a full-HP Pokémon on the field. This is a hard rule: after a Trick, the next move must be the attack we want to be locked into.

## Structural problems

**Sand is 2-10 and Mega Tyranitar Rock Slide is our single biggest killer (17 KOs).** vs test0824 it 4-for-1'd us across three turns while we rotated Protects. Our whole bring (Drogon/Whimsicott/Dragonite/Milotic) is neutral-or-worse to Rock and none can switch out of range. Garchomp resists nothing but at least 2HKOs Mega TT with Scarf EQ (58% observed) and outspeeds. The current preview plan ("Charizard + Whimsicott Tailwind, 70.7% tested") was tested in simulation but is 2-5 in real games — the sim doesn't capture that we keep leaving bodies in front of Rock Slide.

**Trick Room is 11-29.** Wins came when we removed Indeedee early (JinshiOS, arayalex90 win). Losses came when the opposing *non-TR* threat (Mega Garchomp-Z Power Gem, Lucario-Mega) killed Drogon turn 1. Drogon should not lead into any sheet with Garchomp-Z, Aerodactyl, or Tyranitar.

## What's working

Mega Dragonite in standard is 14-4. Kingambit vs Indeedee teams keeps delivering (193%, 173% damage games). Keep both.
