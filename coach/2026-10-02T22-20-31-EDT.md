## 1. What Worked
- **Turn 1 Mega + Protect on Dragonite** was textbook: Aerodactyl's Rock Slide was a displayed 217-335% OHKO on an un-Protected Dragonite; we Mega'd (Multiscale online) and blanked it. This rule is now reliably firing.
- **Dragon Pulse eventually removed Aerodactyl** (T5), the piece that killed Kingambit and chipped everything.
- **Heat Wave cleanup on Absol-Mega-Z** (T7) was the right call as the last Pokémon — Protect there only delays.

## 2. Lessons Applied From Before
- ✅ "Mega-evolving does not require attacking" — applied correctly turn 1.
- ❌ **"Never lead Kingambit when the sheet shows Fighting/Ground pressure"** — violated. Excadrill was visible at preview. High Horsepower did **88% on turn 1**.
- ❌ **Rock-lock discipline** — Aerodactyl's Rock Slide was confirmed turn 1, yet we never pivoted the slots it threatened, and on turn 8 Drogon attacked into a displayed 97-159% OHKO instead of Protecting.
- ❌ **"Tailwind is worthless on a turn where our only other action is weak"** — Dragonite burned its turn-2 action on Tailwind while Whimsicott (the Prankster Tailwind user) sat in the back.

## 3. What Failed
1. **Kingambit was a zero-damage slot.** 207 HP → 25 HP in one turn to High Horsepower, flinched that turn, Protected turn 2, died turn 3. **0% damage dealt in the entire game.** That is a 1-for-4 team selection error, not a piloting error.
2. **Split damage on turn 3.** Kingambit Iron Head → Aerodactyl, Dragonite Dragon Pulse → Absol. Aerodactyl survived, killed Kingambit, and chipped Dragonite twice more. The confirmed killer must eat both actions.
3. **Burned Dragonite double-Protect.** Protect at 17% (T4) and 9% (T6) while burn ticked. A burned Mega at single digits cannot be preserved — it must spend its remaining turns on damage or leave the field.
4. **Whimsicott never set Tailwind** and dealt 75% total. Its Prankster speed control, the single most valuable piece on this team per simulation, was wasted — Taunt on turn 4 then death.
5. **Two rate-limit fallbacks** (T2, T5) handed critical turns to the engine.
