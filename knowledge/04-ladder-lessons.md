# Lessons from the first ladder games (1-6 with the engine-only pilot)
- Setup sweepers went unpunished: Espathra Calm Mind x4 → Baton Pass to Mega Slowbro, Iron Defense x4 → Stored Power swept. Baxcalibur Swords Dance unpunished. Punish setup immediately (attack/Encore the booster, don't Protect-stall).
- Repeated Heat Wave into Aegislash's Wide Guard (3 times). After Wide Guard, use single-target moves (Solar Beam / Ancient Power) or target around it.
- Set Tailwind on the same turn the opponent set Trick Room (Farigiraf) → moved last both ways. Under Trick Room, stall its turns (Protect/switch) or attack; don't add Tailwind.
- Perish Song (Gengar-Mega + Politoed) took two Pokémon: switch out at perish count 1.
- Double-Protect with no threat gave free setup turns.
- Tyranitar Rock Slide KO'd Drogon in sand while it attacked — Protect Drogon vs Tyranitar.
- Ladder teams are often off-meta (Espathra, Slowbro-Mega, Aegislash, Kangaskhan-Mega, Malamar, Inteleon, Gliscor): rely on revealed information over assumed sets.

# Lessons from the first 20 Claude-piloted games (9-9 in real games)
- Indeedee Psychic Terrain + Expanding Force (Armarouge, Mega Alakazam, Reuniclus, Scarf Indeedee) caused 5 of 9 losses: it sweeps our grounded team, OHKOs Sneasler (4x weak) and blocks Fake Out. Leave Sneasler home; KO Indeedee and the Expanding Force user first with the Flying Megas.
- Drogon was KO'd on turn 1-2 in 4 games (Garchomp-Z Power Gem, Inteleon Snipe Shot, Rock Slide x2) — twice after overriding the engine's Protect. Protect Drogon turn 1 when a faster foe likely OHKOs it.
- Setup sweepers (Espathra Calm Mind/Speed Boost, Kommo-o Clangorous Soul, Torterra Shell Smash, Double Team Dragalge) were punished correctly by attacking or Encoring immediately — keep doing it.
- Psychic Terrain does not block Tailwind (it only blocks priority moves aimed at grounded foes).
- Malamar's Skill Swap gives a partner Contrary: that Incineroar got STRONGER from its own Overheat/Close Combat (+4/+5) and swept a −6 Dragonite. When a foe boosts from a stat-drop move, KO it immediately.
- Don't fire Draco Meteor into a target a weaker move already KOs (Malamar at 22%) — it left Dragonite at −4 for the rest of the game.
- Sand (Mega Tyranitar Rock Slide + Excadrill): don't lead Whimsicott into it; Rock Slide double-KOs. Ladder record vs sand: 1-4.

- (Oct 2, 20 games at ~1,150 rating) Rain is the worst matchup by volume: KO the rain setter (Politoed/Pelipper) first, then burst Archaludon in one turn — never chip it over several turns (Stamina + Electro Shot snowball).
- Baton Pass users (Espathra): KO or Encore them on turn 1 — chip damage doesn't stop the boost pass.
- Vs sand: Mega Charizard + Whimsicott Tailwind lead (tested 70.7%); Mega Dragonite + Milotic lead is the worst plan (53%).
- Mega Gengar Shadow Tag + Perish Song: we can't switch; KO Gengar fast or outlast the counts — don't attack into its Protect.
