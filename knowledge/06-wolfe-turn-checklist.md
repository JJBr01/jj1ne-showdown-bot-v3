# Wolfe Glick Turn-By-Turn Decision Checklist (Every Turn)

Execute these steps in strict sequence before picking your move:

1. **Board State Check (Zero Technical Errors)**:
   - Identify active field effects: Is Tailwind active (how many turns left)? Is Trick Room active (how many turns left)?
   - Priority blockers: Is Psychic Terrain active? Are Armor Tail, Dazzling, or Queenly Majesty on the field? (Blocks all priority moves: Fake Out, Sucker Punch, Grassy Glide, Prankster).
   - Choice lock: Is any active ally or foe locked into a move?

2. **Speed Order & Priority Ladder**:
   - Determine exact movement order this turn: +3 (Fake Out) -> +2 (Extreme Speed) -> +1 (Sucker Punch, Grassy Glide) -> 0 (Standard speed order: Tailwind/Scarf/Boosts/TR inverted).
   - If our piece is faster or has priority, can it KO an opponent before they act? Finish low-HP targets immediately before they can move.

3. **Pressure Dynamics & Immediate KO Threats**:
   - Check the Smogon Damage Table: Which of our Pokémon can be 1HKO'd or 2HKO'd this turn? Which foes are in KO range?
   - Map opponent reactions: A threatened foe will (a) Protect/Detect, (b) Switch out, or (c) Trade damage. An obvious Protect is often ignored to double-target the partner.

4. **Safe Play Search (The 3 Archetypes)**:
   - **Offensive Safe Play**: Does an attack guarantee a KO on the active target while also dealing lethal/critical damage to any incoming switch-in?
   - **Defensive Safe Play**: Protect on our threatened piece while switching the partner into a resist, immunity, or Intimidate pivot.
   - **Macro Safe Play**: Chipping a defensive wall (e.g. Incineroar) to put it into guaranteed late-game sweep range.

5. **When to Predict vs. When to Play Safe**:
   - **Ahead in Mon Count (4v2, 4v1, 3v1)**: NEVER make a high-risk prediction. Take the 100% accurate, safe line to close out the win.
   - **Hopelessly Behind or Bad Matchup**: Safe plays will slowly lose. Make the targeted double-attack call-out to eliminate their primary win condition.
   - **50/50 Endgame**: If splitting attacks cannot KO either target, you must double-target the offensive threat.

6. **Win Condition Denial**:
   - Never allow the opponent to simultaneously have Speed and Power.
   - Prioritize KOing or neutralizing their speed control setter (Whimsicott, Farigiraf, Porygon2) before it establishes field advantage.
   - Delay KOing a passive, non-threatening foe if taking the KO gives their lethal sweeper a free switch-in on an unfavorable turn.

7. **Protect Discipline**:
   - Use Protect to: shield a threatened sweeper while partner removes the threat; scout a Choice lock; stall out opposing Tailwind / Trick Room / weather turns.
   - Avoid double Protect unless ahead, stalling the final turn of a field effect, or when forced by game state.
   - Sucker Punch discipline: Never click into Psychic Terrain, Armor Tail, or predictable status/Protect turns; under Trick Room, prefer Kowtow Cleave.
